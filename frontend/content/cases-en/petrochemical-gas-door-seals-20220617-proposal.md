Locking a raised door, compressing its seals after closing, and releasing bogie seals before movement are different states. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s gas-fired bogie-hearth furnace proposal dated 17 June 2022 separately lists door safety pins, pneumatic perimeter compression seals and bogie side seals, with interlocked actions. “Door in position” does not mean all these actions are complete. Define each mechanism, feedback signal and permitted action separately.

## Three states and three configurations

| State to confirm | Proposed configuration | Main check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raised-door retention | Safety-pin locking, plus electrical and mechanical limits | Lock state and conditions permitting bogie travel. |
| Door-perimeter compression/release | 4 cylinder sets compressing soft fibre seals | Compression/release actions and position feedback. |
| Bogie side sealing and travel | 4 cylinder sets, 2 per side | Interlock between seal release and bogie movement. |

The source links door-cylinder compression, release and position to door movement and bogie travel. Bogie clauses also specify mechanical/electrical limits and interlocks with side seals, door and rear wall. These are proposed control relationships. Confirm how each state is detected, which signals collectively permit movement and how abnormal signals are handled. An “automatic operation” screen cannot replace that mapping.

## Reaching position and making seal contact are different checks

The door uses adjustable soft fibre-skirt seals compressed against its frame by cylinders. Bogie sides use cylinder-operated swinging fibre-block seals. The source recognises relative furnace/bogie movement and thermal expansion. Both motion coordination and sealing-surface adjustment matter.

A limit or travel signal represents only its actual detection object. It does not automatically establish contact over all fibre surfaces or unchanged sealing when hot. Record mechanical position, cylinder action, contact adjustment and cold/hot conditions separately. Designer and site provider must agree methods and criteria; this article provides no project-specific adjustment values or operating steps.

## Mechanical interlocks and combustion protection have separate boundaries

The proposal also includes gas-main emergency shut-off, burner flame-failure protection and ignition purging. These combustion functions must coordinate with door and bogie states, but a mechanical interlock is not complete combustion protection. Define their handover conditions and responses/recovery for faults in gas, air or movement feedback.

Source sections disagree on gas pressure, consumption, chamber dimensions and effective heating zone; measurement records also list both 6 and 12 channels. This article selects no set as a final capacity commitment. For a similar project, reconcile equipment drawings, gas conditions and measurement schedules before confirming coordinated controls.

## What do Suneng and the site provide?

Suneng proposes furnace design, manufacture, installation, cold/hot commissioning and inspection/testing, excluding civil foundations. It also provides rail-installation structural requirements and undertakes rail installation adjustment before equipment installation, coordinating with civil works. The buyer provides foundations, embedded parts, buildings and utility construction. Even equipment-side interlocks require rails, foundations and energy for site verification.

Initially provide representative workpieces and loading, the furnace-front operating area, rail/foundation information and existing door/bogie or seal drawings. Then define normal/fault states, feedback points, site support and verification items. The historical proposal supplies a checking approach, not current equipment design or site-test records.
