Two November 2023 Suneng quotations for the same 12.5-metre gas-fired bogie-hearth furnace list different burner configurations: sixteen 450 kW units and twelve 400 kW units. Adjustable door-seal skirts, standard refractory fibre blanket quantities and electrical controls also differ. Compare quantities, specifications, pricing and user-supplied items separately. A price change alone does not establish changed scope, and the two versions cannot be assumed to have equal processing capacity.

## Separate configuration changes from pricing changes

Pages 4–5 of each list show differences in door-seal skirt and blanket quantities and monitor specifications. Pages 6–7 show controller and thermocouple quantities. These are directly checkable. Skirt-set count is not door count, and material quantity cannot directly establish lining thickness, insulation performance or final construction scope.

Both versions list RQTSi5 heat-resistant cast-iron bogie edges at 2.9 t, but price them differently. Some identical-specification control items also have price changes. Different subtotals for doors, lining or controls therefore cannot all be described as scope changes. List actual quantity and specification differences first, then confirm the selected configuration and responsibilities against final drawings and the supply-responsibility schedule. Do not allocate the price difference evenly across the omitted burners.

## Support-block scope differs from the earlier commercial bid

The October 2023 commercial bid prices equipment and heat-resistant steel support blocks separately before producing a combined equipment-with-blocks total. Both November quotations make blocks optional and user-supplied. This is an explicit inclusion difference requiring a common supply and pricing basis before comparison. Both November documents state that comprehensive prices include profit, tax and charges; withholding the actual amounts does not change that basis.

These are revisions for one project, not amounts to add into one purchase total. Moving the commercial bid's support-block cost into another revision does not produce a final contract price. Confirm tracks against each version's separate quotation scope. Historical tax/charge treatment and current purchasing terms require valid commercial documents; internal rates are not used to infer a current tax-exclusive selling price.

## Choose a comparison baseline for a new purchase

Create one confirmation list covering the selected version's burner count and power, door/lining arrangement, controls and user-supplied items. Add workpiece dimensions, loading, process curves and validation criteria. The sources list design, manufacture, installation and acceptance work, but these remain proposed tasks, not evidence of delivery or acceptance. Software/control licensing, actual functions and commissioning scope also require final confirmation and cannot be assumed included from a historical total price.
