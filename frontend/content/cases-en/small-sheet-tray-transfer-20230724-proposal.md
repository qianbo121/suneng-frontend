Delivering a tray outside the furnace does not mean the workpiece has reached the next cooling station. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s small quenching-furnace proposal dated 24 July 2023 concerns solution heating of opening-frame blanks and small sheet-metal parts. It specifies “no more than 5–7 seconds” from door opening to the tray reaching its discharged position. Define that position before planning unloading and onward transfer; this interval is not complete immersion time.

## Tie the discharge finish to the receiving position

The proposal uses a lifting door and chain-driven push/pull mechanism. The tray rests on heat-resistant rollers and connects to a drive chain at the rear. After heating/holding, the door opens and the mechanism pushes the tray out. This defines tray discharge, but the cited clauses do not define the full later sequence of unloading, travel to cooling and complete immersion.

“No more than 5–7 seconds” needs an executable threshold and timing method agreed before acceptance. Do not select either 5 or 7 seconds as an approved single limit or compare it directly with another project's door-to-full-immersion time. Identify the position represented by the arrival signal, the waiting operator/receiver position and the start of the next action.

## Show workpieces and the two-level frame together

The source records maximum existing product dimensions of length 800, width 700 and height 450 mm and requires suitable effective space. One frame is supplied, with an insertable divider for two levels. Product envelope, effective zone and divisible frame describe different things; they do not establish that maximum-size products fit simultaneously in two layers.

Overlay tray, frame bottom, divider height and workpiece envelope, checking roller support, receiving space and handling. Maximum loading is written “>50 kg”, without a common mass definition for workpieces, frame and tray. Do not turn it into a delivery guarantee for 50 kg of net workpieces.

## Verify the working zone, not only instrument indications

Maximum temperature is 600°C, working-zone uniformity ≤±3°C and control accuracy ≤±1°C. The latter does not replace the former. The single zone uses a dual-element thermocouple separately connected to control and paperless recording, plus a single-element sensor for overtemperature alarm and main heater-power isolation. Hardware alone does not establish loaded uniformity.

Final-acceptance test temperatures are 340, 410, 475, 500 and 535°C. These are proposed verification points, not universal sheet-part solution-treatment temperatures. Agree representative loading, test region, instrument/thermocouple verification records and trials. Maximum temperature 600°C does not determine material suitability or holding regime.

## Include site construction interfaces alongside receiving actions

The supplier undertakes transport, unloading/site transfer, installation and commissioning before handover. The buyer provides an overhead crane, power and lighting. Supplier crane operators require appropriate qualifications, and a site safety officer is required. Cables, air pipes, protective cable trunking and installation between equipment and the buyer's building distribution cabinet are assigned to the supplier; breaking out foundations is buyer work. A parenthetical approximately 10 m cable length does not replace route and actual-quantity confirmation.

At final layout review, check the discharged tray, receiving people/equipment, access and these services together. Verify action coordination against the proposed fan, heater, door and movement interlocks. Manual mode does not imply permission to bypass interlocks for transfer.
