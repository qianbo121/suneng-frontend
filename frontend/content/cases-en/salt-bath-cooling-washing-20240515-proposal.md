## Air agitation and washing equipment do not prove results

The cooling tank uses compressed-air agitation, and working tanks have overflow and replenishment pipes. The source provides no cooling curves for different loads, washing-residue measurements or wastewater-treatment results. Determine replenishment, drainage, agitation and verification from workpiece and medium conditions; the presence of agitation and a washing tank does not establish satisfactory performance.

Explain the medium, substances carried in by workpieces and intended cooling/washing method. Allocate transfer, splash collection, exhaust and subsequent liquid treatment. These need design and site documentation; a tank list is not complete process-suitability or abnormal-condition approval.
