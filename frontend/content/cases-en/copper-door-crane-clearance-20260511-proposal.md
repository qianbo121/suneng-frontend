Suneng's May 2026 copper-alloy wire annealing furnace proposal uses four chamber furnaces sharing one loading/unloading fork truck. It explicitly states that the overhead crane can pass when a door is closed but cannot pass when the door is fully raised for unloading. Review both states and coordinate raised-door space, crane travel and truck handling. The parked equipment outline alone does not establish workshop fit.

## Check two door states, not one equipment height

| Position or state | Original proposal | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fully raised door | Elevation +5345 mm above floor datum | Source explicitly prohibits crane passage in this state |
| Highest lifting-mechanism point | Elevation +4500 mm above floor datum | Separate from the raised door's highest point |
| Handling arrangement | 4 furnaces share 1 loading/unloading fork truck | Check furnace approach/withdrawal, transverse transfer and crane-route occupation separately |

The two heights concern different objects: +4500 mm is the mechanism's highest point and +5345 mm the raised door's highest point, both relative to the specified floor datum. The lower height cannot substitute for the door's operating envelope. Neither is a universal workshop-clearance requirement guaranteeing installation. Match actual crane rails, beam undersides, lifting attachments and foundation elevations.

Because the source identifies restricted crane travel with the door raised, the layout should distinguish usable areas for open and closed states. If opening occupies an essential lifting route, resolve the conflict in layout or operating sequence rather than merely adding a warning sign after installation. Furnace location, travel route or scheduling changes require site drawings and the responsible designer's confirmation.

## Include loading and unloading platforms in the shared-truck layout

One loading platform and one unloading platform are provided, respectively for load preparation and cooling after discharge. Workpieces are prepared on the loading platform; the truck picks them up, returns, traverses to the selected furnace and loads longitudinally. After a furnace signals process completion, the truck removes its load and transfers it to the unloading platform. Furnace frontage is only part of the route; preparation, cooling and transverse travel also occupy space.

When several furnaces finish close together, timely receiving by the shared truck and unloading platform requires one sequence chart covering discharge signals, tray transfers and platform occupancy. The source supports independent annealing by furnace and ordered operation by furnace number, but supplies no actual schedule proving zero waiting for every batch. Verify discharge requirements rather than multiplying continuous output by four furnaces.

## Existing door interlocks do not establish crane-control integration

The door has dual upper/lower travel-limit protection and interlocks with the truck; the control section also interlocks door operation with fork entry/withdrawal. These support checking door/truck actions but do not provide complete wiring or validation of crane travel permissions. Define crane-versus-door prohibitions, permissions and abnormal handling in final control scope. Automatic crane collision prevention is not claimed here.

## Preserve actual workpiece and atmosphere conditions after layout approval

The application is copper-alloy wire annealing at a proposed operating temperature of 550–700°C. Effective loading dimensions are φ1710×1600 mm high, while the effective chamber is 2100×2000×1800 mm. Check load envelope with trays and fork-support locations. Chamber dimensions are not the maximum assembled-load outline, and the temperature range is not an annealing curve for every copper-alloy grade.

The source includes a nitrogen inlet and roof pressure-relief pipe, while controls reserve output terminals for later connection of a nitrogen solenoid valve. If protective-atmosphere annealing is required, confirm the actual extent of gas supply and control included now, with atmosphere transitions during door opening/loading, discharge and workshop ventilation. Reserved interfaces are not a complete automatic nitrogen system.

Suneng proposes furnace bodies, circulating heating, fork truck, loading/unloading platforms and controls. The installation section assigns equipment interconnection, wiring and installation to the seller, with water/electricity/gas connections and part of electrical-cabinet installation to the buyer. For initial discussions, provide workshop sections, crane/attachment envelopes, tray-loading drawings and furnace process schedules. Clarify raised-door space and handling sequence first, then interfaces and acceptance.
