Suneng’s fork-handling furnace carries long bars parallel to the furnace door. A charging car lifts the complete batch between the hearth supports. The agreement permits no more than 30 seconds from first outward movement to full immersion with 5 tonnes of workpieces.

## Project features

**A wide doorway for transverse loading** — The bars rest across the supports parallel to the door along the 6 m working width. The forks enter between the supports and lift the batch together.

**Quenching the entire batch outside the furnace** — After the door opens, the rail-mounted car lifts the bars out, moves them to the tank and lowers them. The bars retain their transverse orientation.

## Equipment arrangement and process sequence

**Charge across the door** — Lay the long bars on the supports parallel to the door across the 6 m working width.

**Lift on the forks** — Insert the forks between the supports and lift the complete batch.

**Withdraw the batch** — Once the door is fully open, start timing at the first outward movement.

**Fully immerse the bars** — Keep the transverse arrangement. Stop timing when the last bar is fully submerged.

## Supply responsibilities

**Suneng** — Provides foundation construction information, installs and commissions the equipment, and trains operators.

**Buyer** — Supplies the water-quench tank and heat-resistant steel basket, prepares the installation site to the foundation drawing and assists with water, electricity, gas and lifting equipment.

## Scope of supply

Suneng supplies the resistance-heated chamber furnace, door and lift, rail-mounted fork car and electrical controls. The furnace heats in zones. The car lifts and transfers the entire batch.

## Acceptance procedure

**Factory inspection before dispatch** — After manufacture, the agreement calls for the buyer to inspect assembly quality, safety items and associated equipment at the factory.

**Loaded commissioning on site** — Cold commissioning precedes heating under load. The acceptance criterion is compliance with the contract’s technical requirements.

## Common questions

**Is 6 m the chamber depth or the width along the door?** It is the working width along the door. The working depth is 1.3 m and the working height is 0.6 m. The bars lie across the width.

**What do the 5 tonnes and 30 seconds refer to?** The 5 tonnes is workpiece weight excluding the support blocks. With the door fully open, time from first outward movement until the complete batch is submerged.

**Who supplies the quench tank and handling equipment?** The buyer supplies the tank. Suneng supplies the rail-mounted fork car. The interfaces include the tank opening, track and clearance for lowering the forks.
