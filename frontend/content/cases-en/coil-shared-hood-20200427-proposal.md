Two furnace bases do not provide the capacity of two complete heating furnaces. A historical bell furnace proposal from Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd. for bearing-steel wire coils specifies one heating hood, two sets of bases and retorts, and one cooling hood. Production planning must identify which batch occupies each hood and include loading, unloading, atmosphere changes and crane transfers in the same schedule.

## Interpret equipment quantities by process stage

Each of the two bases has a retort, and the proposal includes two vacuum-pump sets. There is one heating hood and one cooling hood, both handled by an overhead crane and located by guide columns. For each batch, record the equipment occupied during loading, atmosphere preparation, heating and soaking, cooling and unloading. The source contains no validated alternating-cycle chart, so two bases cannot be assumed to heat two batches simultaneously.

## Turn 20 t and 24 hours into comparable production conditions

The source lists ten coils at 2 t each, arranged in two layers of five, giving a 20 t load, and an annealing cycle of 24 hours per batch. These are the proposal's loading and cycle figures. They do not establish 40 t per day from two bases. Clarify the stages covered by 24 hours, how hood occupation is staggered, and whether crane handling, loading, unloading and atmosphere changes are included.

Coil dimensions are approximately 900 mm internal diameter, 1200 mm external diameter and 1300–1600 mm high. The effective workpiece envelope is φ3600×3200 mm. Two coils at the maximum height of 1600 mm already total 3200 mm. This is a dimensional calculation only, excluding interlayer supports and clearances; it does not prove that two layers of the tallest coils have sufficient space. Verify usable height against the final support-plate and coil-loading drawings.

## Cooling does not mean opening the retort

Cooling blowers outside the stainless-steel retort direct air tangentially to create a spiral flow. Inside, a central circulation fan in the base and flow guides circulate the retort atmosphere. Moving the cooling hood and releasing the retort seal are separate actions. A hood in position does not automatically permit opening and unloading. Confirm seal-release conditions in the final process and atmosphere documents.

The source describes pre-evacuation, nitrogen purging and propane protection, with interlocks between atmosphere preparation before heating and the temperature permissive for propane. A separate safety clause prohibits combustible atmosphere below 500°C. This is a design condition in the historical document, not a universal operating threshold. Implementation still requires complete procedures for purging, exhaust combustion, abnormal gas shutoff and permission to open the retort. No unprovided operating procedure is added here.

## Confirm site conditions and drawings with the batch schedule

The proposed maximum operating temperature is 850°C and the usual temperature 800°C. The actual coil process must be established from material and inspection objectives. The supply list assigns the nitrogen generator, furnace-side nitrogen and propane lines, and pressure-reducing valves to the user. The configuration description also excludes power wiring between the furnace and control cabinet and equipment foundation construction. Suneng was to design, manufacture, transport, install and commission the equipment. These exclusions and overhead-crane conditions affect whether the two bases can operate to the proposed sequence.

The cover is dated 18 May 2026, while the final page is signed 27 April 2020. Both dates are retained; no actual project implementation date is inferred. Once the schedule, loading drawings and atmosphere conditions are confirmed, acceptance should follow the same agreed document revision.
