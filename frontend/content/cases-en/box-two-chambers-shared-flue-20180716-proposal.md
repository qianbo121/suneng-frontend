Suneng's 2018 twin-chamber gas-fired box furnace proposal gives each chamber one control zone and one burner, while both share one chimney. Each chamber also has its own air preheater at the chimney base. Review individual controls together with shared exhaust, fuel and air supply. Separate temperature control does not establish validation of every operating combination.

Each chamber has a control thermocouple at its roof-burner position, forming a loop with the controller and burner. This defines chamber-temperature measurement/control, not the complete temperature distribution of every workpiece. Separate preheaters still operate within the shared exhaust arrangement. Confirm each chamber's air and flue-gas paths in piping drawings.

## Test the operating combinations actually required

If both chambers must heat together, one must run alone, or they must operate at different stages, submit these combinations for design confirmation. Final system drawings and test records must show how furnace pressure, exhaust and combustion air remain within agreed conditions. This page neither assumes inevitable interference nor infers complete independence from two controllers.

The common inlet has a natural-gas emergency shutoff valve, with protection for low pressure, fan faults and flame failure, plus door/heating interlocks. Confirm whether each fault affects one chamber or shared equipment and how air, exhaust and fuel conditions relate. General protection descriptions do not replace a signal/action scope table. No ignition or fault-changeover procedure is supplied here.

## Keep per-chamber loading distinct from equipment quantity

The table gives maximum loading of 1 t/chamber×2 chambers and one complete machine. This does not permit 2 t in either chamber or one workpiece spanning both. The source does not define whether 1 t includes fixtures. Reconcile the load definition with actual loading drawings, then match heating, soaking and inspection requirements for each chamber.

The document title describes a shell-mould baking furnace, while the application lists annealing, quenching, normalising and tempering of steel parts. These workpiece/process descriptions cannot be reconciled from the title alone. This page covers only twin-chamber configuration. For a specific shell mould or metal part, define actual condition, purpose and loading before selecting process and acceptance criteria.

## Acceptance should cover each load and the shared system

The source schedules furnace trials for process curves, industrial-utility consumption and workpiece temperature differences during soaking, with both parties agreeing charge quantity, quality and specifications. Add required chamber combinations to the test plan, recording individual loads and shared supply/exhaust states. Planned acceptance, preheaters or a tall chimney do not establish energy savings or emissions compliance.

Suneng's proposal lists furnace, combustion, pipework and controls; the buyer provides workshop foundations and utilities, with foundation information from the seller. Supply intended workpieces for both chambers, any differing operating stages, and site fuel/air/exhaust layouts. Clarify shared-system and single-chamber boundaries before finalising supply and validation.
