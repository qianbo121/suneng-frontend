Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s gas-fired solution-furnace proposal for long bars, dated 6 March 2025, specifies door, travelling-frame and fork conditions alongside combustion-air, gas-pressure and flame monitoring. Map mechanical positions to combustion permissions before verifying loaded discharge and immersion.

## The source specifies several movement conditions

During heating, neither the fork machine nor travelling frame may move. Heating starts only after door closure; frame entry/exit requires the door at its specified raised position; fork lifting/lowering requires the frame fully inside. Limit switches are provided at corresponding door, frame and machine positions. These are distinct permissions, not one generic “in position” signal.

Pneumatic safety pins lock the raised door and release for lowering. Confirm acquisition and verification of door position, pin state and frame position, and permitted actions if a signal is lost. Proposed protections are not completed site fault tests.

## Match combustion permissions to door, frame and fork states

The source requires combustion-air, cooling-air and gas-pressure checks before starting. Low gas pressure or fan failure closes gas and raises an alarm; combustion clauses also specify flame monitoring and fail-closed valves on power loss. Mechanical readiness alone does not establish these conditions. Use final controls documentation, not door closure alone, to assess starting permission.

An action schedule should define combustion state during door closing/raising, frame travel and fork movement, plus mechanical responses to gas, fan or flame faults. Manual control does not remove protection. This article adds no undocumented purge duration, automatic reset or emergency-discharge sequence.

## Reconcile burner and zone counts before checking configuration

Main parameter/combustion sections generally list 6 × 450 kW burners, while an air-piping paragraph supplies 8 burners. Temperature parameters give 3 zones and the shell 3 thermocouple sockets, whereas later controls text states 8 thermocouples and 8 zones. Reconcile the final list, gas piping and zone drawing. Dividing burner count by zone count cannot establish each zone's configuration.

These inconsistencies do not remove the documented mechanical and combustion permission requirements, but they affect signals, valve groups, sensors and acceptance lists. Holding uniformity ≤±10°C is proposed. Zones or high-velocity burners do not establish improved workpiece uniformity or gas savings.

## Include buyer-supplied tank and supports in rapid-transfer checks

Discharge-to-water time is ≤45 s, with actual start/finish events to be agreed. Do not redefine it as door opening to full immersion. The buyer-supplied tank requires circulation/replenishment. Verify representative loading, hot extraction and immersion position with the permissions above; approximately 20 m/min travel speed cannot replace a complete transfer trial.

Buyer scope includes internal cast support blocks, high-temperature baskets, tank and foundation construction; equipment-foundation design is assigned to the seller. Supports affect fork entry and lifting, so include these items on interface drawings. Proposed Suneng furnace and fork-machine supply does not include all loading and site facilities by implication.
