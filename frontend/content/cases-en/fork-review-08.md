Suneng’s gas-fired fork-handling furnace uses a rail-mounted car to transfer the fittings and fixtures to water together. In this project, the buyer supplies both the solution-treatment tank and the trays.

## Project features

**Whole-batch transfer of fittings at the furnace door** — Heat the fittings on a tray. After soaking, the car lifts out the parts and fixtures together and lowers them into the buyer’s solution-treatment water tank.

**Furnace-side piping supplied with the equipment** — Suneng supplies the furnace-side natural-gas piping, valves, air ducts and fan. The combustion system has 8 burners rated at 450 kW each.

## Equipment arrangement and process sequence

**Load on the tray** — Charge the stainless fittings as a batch on the tray.

**Heat with natural gas** — The gas, combustion-air and burner systems heat and soak the charge to the process requirements.

**Withdraw with the fork car** — The forks support the parts and fixtures and carry them out together.

**Immerse the batch** — Lower the workpieces and fixtures into the buyer’s water tank together.

## Supply responsibilities

**Suneng** — Provides foundation design, furnace and drives, gas and air systems, installation and commissioning.

**Buyer** — Supplies the quench tank, trays and fixture blocks, constructs foundations and embedded items, and provides the cable to the low-voltage distribution cabinet.

## Scope of supply

Supplier’s scope includes the furnace, door lift, fork car and drive, sealing system and operating platform, gas piping and valves, combustion air, burners, electrical controls and equipment documentation.

## Acceptance procedure

**Factory inspection before dispatch** — Once the equipment is manufactured, the supplier invites the buyer to the works to inspect fabrication, assembly and associated equipment.

**Installation and commissioning under load** — Install and commission once the site is ready, then check the technical parameters under load. Acceptance is against the contract specifications.

## Common questions

**Are the tank and trays ordered separately?** The buyer provides both. The contract equipment list assigns the tank to the buyer. Suneng’s engineering records confirm the buyer also supplies the trays. Suneng supplies the charging car with the furnace.

**Who provides the gas piping, fan and water tank?** The technical documents assign the gas piping, valves, air duct and fan to the supplier. The buyer provides the water tank.
