Furnaces purchased together do not necessarily perform the same task. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s eight-furnace package dated 8 August 2023 covers electroslag-ingot annealing, electrode and slag baking, solution treatment, ageing, diffusion annealing and launder baking. Each has separately specified handling, heat sources and proposed acceptance criteria. Map every unit to its workpieces, process and site interfaces; one total load or temperature criterion cannot describe the whole package.

The summary comprises 6 electrically heated units and 2 natural-gas units, totalling 8. It also lists an industrial computer and programming for “distributed control of 8 furnaces”. This establishes a central-control requirement, not automatic workpiece flow in table order or a continuous production line. Whether and how workpieces move between units must be defined by the actual process and logistics drawing.

## Identical chamber dimensions still need separate temperature verification

The bogie solution and ageing sections both specify 4500 × 2200 × 1000 mm chambers and rated loads of 20 t. The former lists a rated temperature of 1200°C and uniformity ±7°C. The latter lists a maximum operating temperature of 950°C, uniformity ±5°C and an additional high-temperature recirculation/air-guide system. Equal dimensions and loads do not make them the same equipment.

These are proposed furnace-temperature criteria, not evidence of superior workpiece properties from either furnace. Separately define test temperature, effective zone, empty/loaded state, sensors and assessment method, and include product quality requirements in acceptance. Neither 950°C nor 1200°C replaces a material-specific process curve.

The compilation has unresolved differences: the diffusion-furnace summary and individual section differ in dimensions and uniformity; the launder-furnace overview states 1300°C while its main table states 1100°C. This article does not select a final value. Approve versions and acceptance clauses for each unit rather than copying one summary as the common acceptance basis for all eight.

## Check buyer-supplied tanks and launder fixtures with their own equipment

The fork-handled solution furnace explicitly includes a rapid loading/unloading machine and a buyer-supplied water tank. Its 5 t/batch excludes fixtures and support blocks. Check cooling, tank and transfer against its loading method; do not borrow load ratings or fixture configurations from other bogie furnaces.

The launder baking furnace uses a lifting furnace body and loading bogie, lowering the body around the launder for heating. Buyer scope includes fixture support blocks. Workpiece restraint, body lifting and site conditions require separate confirmation. The shared label “furnace” does not establish interchangeable lifting gear, fixtures or operating space.

## Read the total quotation with each unit's supply scope

The historical summary quotation of 8 August 2023 includes 13% VAT, design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning. It excludes foundations, embedded parts, and secondary cables and trays between furnace and cabinet. These are terms for that eight-furnace configuration and listed auxiliaries, not a current quotation, signed contract value or payment record.

Cross-check individual sections, notes and cost items. For example, the launder-furnace cost table names an electrical control system “including secondary cables and trays”, while a note on the same page excludes those items. The item name alone does not establish inclusion or justify another deduction from the discounted total. Agree document version, pricing basis and supply definition before confirming the contract schedule.

## Which table should be prepared first?

By equipment number, list the treatment object, material and process requirements, batch loading drawing and verification tasks. Mark shared utilities, fixtures, logistics and central-control handovers. Suneng can then assess which configurations need individual design and which services can be coordinated. Form the overall budget after unit scopes are clear. This preserves package coordination without substituting one parameter for different purposes.
