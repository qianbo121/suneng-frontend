Compare chamber size, doors, heating zones and circulation together when selecting an aluminium-tube annealing furnace. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s 2022–2023 records for 6061 aluminium tubes include several new-furnace sizes and a 300 kW retrofit quotation. Configuration changes involve more than increased power. These documents support initial comparison, not a confirmed sequence of capacity expansions or completed modifications.

## Distinguish different sizes from different configurations at the same size

| Document configuration | Chamber length × width × height | Rated power and load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 2022 proposal | 2400 × 1600 × 1750 mm | 150 kW; 2 t |
| December 2022 proposal | 5000 × 1600 × 1850 mm | 300 kW; 4 t |
| Proposal with July 2023 cover date | 8300 × 2100 × 2100 mm | 480 kW; 10 t |
| Proposal with September 2023 cover date | 8300 × 2100 × 2100 mm | 520 kW; 10 t |

All are proposed values. Inclusion of racks and other auxiliary loads needs agreement. The two 8.3 m records have identical chambers and 10 t ratings but different power and structure, showing why a fixed kilowatt-per-tonne conversion is inappropriate. Establish tube length, individual weight, layers and clearances first.

## Door changes alter loading and closing checks

The 150 kW, 300 kW and July 480 kW proposals have lifting doors with internal heaters, permitting bogie travel only after the door is fully raised. The September 520 kW record fixes the door to the bogie, using bogie travel and a tapered mating interface to close the opening. Do not transfer raised-door signals or all movement conditions between these designs.

For an integral door/bogie, assess loading with bogie position, door engagement and rails. For a lifting door, check opening clearance, raised-door retention and travel interlocks. Final mechanical drawings and action schedules determine selection; a later date alone does not establish cancellation of an earlier configuration.

## Fan counts and control zones have no universal one-to-one relationship

The 150 kW proposal has 1 zone and 1 × 7.5 kW circulation fan; the 300 kW unit has 2 zones and 2 × 7.5 kW fans. Both 8.3 m proposals have 4 zones, but July lists 4 × 7.5 kW fans and September 1 × 37 kW fan. Counts or motor power alone cannot rank circulation performance, and one fan does not mean one temperature zone.

Earlier designs place elements around the chamber and beneath the hearth. The September 520 kW text instead describes resistance wire wound on ceramic tubes spanning the chamber in three layers, with 4 heating-chamber zones and 4 furnace monitoring points. Compare these with rack perforations, guides and actual obstruction to determine airflow coverage. The text alone cannot supply a complete airflow diagram or assign all 8 thermocouple roles.

The proposals list rated temperature 500°C and uniformity ≤±5°C. These are equipment criteria; 500°C is not a universal annealing temperature for 6061 tubes. Process curve and uniformity test temperature/loading must match material and product requirements. Empty heat-up time or water-cooled fans do not prove uniform workpiece annealing.

## A circulation retrofit may involve furnace, bogie and controls

The related November 2023 quotation for a 300 kW retrofit separately lists furnace extension, modified/partly reused elements, bogie extension, a 30 kW high-temperature circulation fan, heating chamber/ducts, and fan-control/inverter changes. This supports the point that circulation modifications can also change space, supports and controls, rather than merely replacing a fan. It does not establish completed work.

That quotation gives working-chamber height 1750 mm, while the December 300 kW new-furnace proposal states 1850 mm. They cannot be merged into one definite final unit. New cables are buyer-supplied and modified furnace-to-cabinet power wiring is excluded. Check materials and installation against the applicable new-build or retrofit scope.

## Using the records for initial selection

Fix representative tubes, batch layout and annealing requirements, then select the chamber/door proposal to compare. Map heating, circulation and sensors onto one drawing. For an existing furnace, add measured space, reusable components and site cable schedules. Suneng proposes the documented equipment or modifications and installation/commissioning; foundations, energy and wiring follow each document's clauses. Do not transfer scope between different dates.
