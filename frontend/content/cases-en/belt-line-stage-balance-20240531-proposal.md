Line output requires coordinated heating, oil cooling, de-oiling, washing and tempering. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s mesh-belt line proposal dated 31 May 2024 explicitly qualifies approximately 1000 kg/h as quenching-and-tempering capacity and gives operating times and speed adjustment for different sections. That figure is not directly carburising capacity, and quench-furnace heating power alone cannot establish line output.

## Match approximately 1000 kg/h to process and loading

The stated purpose is protective-atmosphere heat treatment of small/medium metal parts. Although the heating unit is named a continuous carburising/quenching-and-tempering furnace, the approximately 1000 kg/h figure is explicitly for quenching and tempering. Shared naming does not establish that all case-depth requirements or curves achieve the same output. Define parts, individual weight, loading and process first.

## Different widths and residence times must handle the same flow of parts

The quench belt is 800 mm wide with a 10000 mm heating length; the tempering furnace lists width 1000 mm and length 14000 mm. The oil-tank elevator belt is 900 mm wide. Different widths/times do not establish automatic capacity matching. Calculate transfer quantity and mass per unit time against permitted loading depth in each section, particularly when slowing one section to meet residence time.

The 40–200 min range is defined as entry-to-exit travel, not freely selectable holding for every part. Agree heating, oil-cooling and tempering curves separately. When output changes, check every section's movement and accumulation; accelerating one section alone does not establish 1000 kg/h.

## De-oiling and washing also consume real processing capacity

The oil tank proposes jets, oil-temperature control, a water-cooled exchanger and an elevator belt, with requirements against dropped parts and belt jams. Downstream de-oiling/washing includes oil/water separation, sprays and air blowing. These affect extraction, cleaning and onward transport; tank volume alone does not represent cooling and handling capability.

Describe surface oil/residual liquid, cleaning requirements and permitted transfers, and test representative loading through the sections. Coordinate oil-fume extraction, oil temperature, water, liquid level and conveyor-fault responses with tank and adjacent-furnace interlocks. The proposed extraction/combustion concepts do not establish complete exhaust or wastewater treatment.

## Furnace temperature and atmosphere have separate applicability conditions

The quench-furnace range is 760–950°C; tempering is 200–650°C. However, ±5°C uniformity within the uniform-temperature zone is limited to 400–650°C, not the whole lower-temperature range. Material/product requirements determine workpiece temperatures and properties, not reverse inference from equipment ranges.

The source also proposes atmosphere/carbon-potential control, an inlet flame curtain, oil-fume extraction, sequential drive interlocks and rotation-fault alarms. Atmosphere lists differ, so this article does not combine them into a definite gas mixture. Confirm media and controls for purging, gas faults, exhaust and shutdown. A flame curtain or instrument count alone does not establish suitability.

## Relating installation and trials to output verification

Suneng proposes the stations and controls, installation guidance and commissioning. The buyer supplies primary cables, power cabinets and secondary-wiring materials, among other listed items; the supplier installs secondary wiring. Allocate external atmosphere supply, cooling water and environmental facilities individually. “Turnkey” does not remove these conditions.

Final acceptance proposes 24 hours of fault-free continuous operation and at least 1 hour of continuous production with specified test pieces. These remain proposed criteria. To verify current quenching-and-tempering output, additionally define representative parts, conforming net weight per unit time, process curves and stable section conditions. Operating duration alone is not a 1000 kg/h conforming-output record.
