Different heating units in one cylinder project require separate loading and transfer agreements. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s three proposals dated 19 May 2020 cover a vertical solution furnace with rack lifting and a movable water tank, a bogie-hearth ageing furnace, and a box-type rotary system for curing after winding. Match each stage to the workpiece's condition before checking racks, bogies and rotating supports. The documents do not establish a shared temperature programme or fixture for every stage.

## Identify the workpiece entering each stage

The stated purposes are aluminium cylinder solution treatment, ageing, and curing after full carbon-fibre winding. The first two concern aluminium heat treatment; the third explicitly concerns the wound assembly. Confirm actual stage conditions from the current process documents. Do not carry the wound-workpiece description into solution treatment, or infer a material/resin process merely because temperature is adjustable.

| Process proposal | Loading and movement | Interface needing separate agreement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solution treatment | Rack lifting, tank moving beneath the furnace, additional forced-down mechanism | Rack, lifting connection and tank positioning. |
| Ageing | Bogie carries a fixed rack into the chamber | Rack and workpiece restraint on the bogie. |
| Post-winding curing | Loading cart; adjustable supports with a translating rotary mechanism engaging cylinder centre rods | Cart, adjustable support and centre-rod connection. |

This compares equipment/loading interfaces, not an established continuous line. Stage-to-stage transfer, fixture sharing and connector changes require separate loading drawings. Similar cylinder dimensions do not establish fixture compatibility under different motion and temperature conditions.

## Check rapid immersion together with rack restraint

The solution proposal specifies transfer ≤15 s, explicitly timed from door opening to complete workpiece immersion. It considers buoyancy from the cylinder's internal volume and proposes forced-down mechanisms on both sides of the tank. Rack lifting has anti-slip limit protection; the tank includes circulation/agitation, temperature measurement, replenishment and overflow. Motion, restraint and tank conditions all matter; lowering speed alone cannot establish the 15 s requirement.

For new cylinders, check openings, venting and immersion orientation together with rack, forced-down mechanism and tank layout. The cited clauses contain no project-specific internal vent path or verification result. Forced submergence does not establish resolved internal venting. Include both timing finish and actual workpiece condition in acceptance.

## Ageing and curing support different load arrangements

The ageing bogie is rated at 6 t per load, explicitly uniformly distributed and including fixtures/support blocks. This is not 6 t of net cylinders. The curing proposal has adjustable supports and a translating rotation mechanism engaging the centre rods; the loading cart is buyer-supplied. Assess bogie/rack load distribution for ageing and end connections, spacing adjustment and cart handover for curing. Bogie tonnage does not establish curing-rack capacity.

## Separate combustion, internal circulation and curing exhaust

Gas-heating configurations do not replace process confirmation for each stage. The solution proposal lists a gas fast shut-off valve, high/low pressure switches and flame detection. The curing proposal adds an extraction fan, drawer-type resin tray and combustible-gas alarm to heating/circulation. Allocate gas safety shut-off, recirculation, curing-emission extraction and resin cleaning separately. An exhaust fan is not completed gas purification.

Curing requires current resin/emission information, discharge connections and a basis for detection and fault interlocks. Listed probes represent their own monitoring coverage; they do not establish concentration measurement everywhere, adequate air exchange or complete downstream treatment. Equipment upper temperatures cannot prescribe a resin-curing curve.

## Prepare a stage-by-stage handover drawing

Show cylinder condition, dimensions/weight, rack and end connections, process curve, handling equipment and next station for every stage. Then allocate Suneng and buyer supply. For example, the ageing proposal assigns equipment integration, wiring and installation to the seller and water/electricity/gas connection to the buyer; the curing cart is explicitly buyer-supplied. Only consistent stage interfaces support cycle assessment. Adding equipment parameters cannot calculate whole-line output.
