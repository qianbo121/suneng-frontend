Suneng’s proposed furnace allows a maximum 7.5-tonne charge including the coil rack. Effective chamber dimensions are 2.4 m long × 2.5 m wide × 1.8 m high. Coil count depends on rack tare, individual coil weights and the positions actually available on the rack.

## Loading coils on the rack

**Deduct the rack weight** — The 7.5 tonnes includes the charge and rack. Only the remaining allowance is available for coils.

**Count whole-coil positions** — Check outside diameter, width and rack layout. Use the lower of the weight and space limits.

**Use the complete cycle for shift output** — Heat-up and soaking total 8 hours. Allow additional time for handling and waits while the furnace is occupied.

## Loading and annealing the coils

**Weigh the rack and coils** — Weigh the empty rack and each coil separately.

**Position coils to the drawing** — Check each coil position within the agreed loading envelope.

**Anneal the loaded rack** — The fork car charges the furnace. Heat and soak according to the proposal.

**Calculate the net charge** — At 6 coils of 0.8 tonnes each, this example gives 4.8 tonnes of copper alloy.

## Worked batch example

Assume a 0.7-tonne rack and 0.8 tonnes per coil. This leaves a weight allowance of 6.8 tonnes for coils.

## Which dimensions should be used for the coil layout?

The proposed effective chamber measures 2.4 m × 2.5 m × 1.8 m. Use the agreed loading envelope, actual rack, coil diameter and width to draw the layout. For mixed loads, sum individual weights and confirm each position. The proposal allows 3 hours for heating and 5 for soaking. Include handling and waits when calculating shift output.

## Common questions

**Can the 5.5 tonnes in the four-chamber contract be used for this proposal?** The 2020 four-chamber contract specifies 5.5 tonnes per chamber including the rack. The 2023 proposal specifies 7.5 tonnes including the rack for different equipment.

**Why does the six-coil example also total 5.5 tonnes?** Six coils at 0.8 tonnes weigh 4.8 tonnes net. Add the assumed 0.7-tonne rack to obtain 5.5 tonnes gross.

**If the weight limit allows eight coils, can eight be scheduled?** Check the available rack positions. Outside diameter, coil width and usable space determine how many whole coils can be loaded.
