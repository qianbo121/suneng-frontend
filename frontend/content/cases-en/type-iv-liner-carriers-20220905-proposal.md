Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s Type IV cylinder-liner annealing proposal dated 5 September 2022 positions polymer liners in a frame cart, then transfers and secures it on a bogie for horizontal furnace entry. Confirm workpiece location, both support interfaces and hot-air passages together; chamber dimensions alone cannot select the frame.

## The frame locates workpieces; the bogie moves them into the oven

The frame cart has double-opening doors and castors. Its upper locating frame adjusts forwards/backwards for workpiece size, with manually positioned anti-collision bars between pieces. The source requires the cart to be centred on the bogie with its castors locked. Workpiece location within the frame and frame restraint on the bogie are therefore separate levels.

Check contact with locating frames/bars and the frame-to-bogie locating, restraint and support interface separately. Locked castors do not establish verified stability for every load. Confirm crane or forklift transfer, bogie capacity and site access for the actual workpiece/fixture combination.

## A different liner requires more than adjusting the upper frame

The source provides an adjustable upper frame, perforated bottom plates for circulation and anti-collision bars between workpieces. Changed dimensions also require checks of contacts, bar spacing, arrangement and blockage of perforations. Moving the upper frame until the part fits does not establish suitability.

Side-duct heaters, circulation fans and air guides form internal hot-air circulation. A changed liner/frame arrangement can change ventilation around the parts. Agree liner-temperature and quality verification against the actual process. Proposed furnace uniformity ±3°C is not proof that every polymer liner reaches the same temperature or dimensional stability.

## Liner annealing is distinct from curing the wound layer

The stated purpose is annealing as-manufactured polymer liners for Type IV pressure vessels, at a rated equipment temperature of 200°C. This is not a rotary-curing proposal for wound cylinder layers, nor does it recommend a curve for a polymer grade. Establish liner material, forming condition and annealing requirements before loading and programming. The word “cylinder” does not justify importing another curing process or rotation mechanism.

## Separate internal circulation from external exhaust

A double-wall exhaust duct discharges furnace gas through its inner passage and admits fresh air through the outer passage, exchanging heat between them. A variable-frequency extraction fan regulates discharge by process stage. Circulation fans move air inside; the extraction fan and external ducts manage discharge and make-up air. Total fan power cannot combine these distinct duties.

The buyer explicitly fabricates and installs the section from the extraction-fan outlet to its exhaust-treatment equipment or central discharge system. Determine emissions, extraction needs, external treatment/discharge and fault responsibilities from the actual liner material and process. Heat recovery does not establish gas treatment, and the source does not justify inventing logic such as continued heating without extraction.

## What information should each party prepare?

Suneng proposes the oven, bogie, frame cart, controls and documents, with equipment integration, wiring and installation. The buyer connects site energy. Furnace-to-cabinet power cables are buyer-supplied and seller-installed. Specify cart quantity, transfer lifting gear, access, external exhaust and cable supply separately; equipment installation does not include all site services by implication.

Start with liner dimensions, material and forming condition, batch count and layout, plus existing frames, transfer tools and furnace-front space. Suneng can assess location/support, entry movement and circulation, then identify outstanding process-verification and exhaust interfaces.
