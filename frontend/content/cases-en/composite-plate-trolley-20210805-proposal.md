For heavy thick-plate loads, check the load path at each contact from workpiece through support blocks and load-bearing columns to the bogie frame beams. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s proposal dated 5 August 2021 specifies a rated furnace load of 170 t and states that the support columns bear on the bogie's steel frame beams. Its quotation lists equipment support bases but excludes workpiece support blocks. Connect the equipment supports and buyer-supplied blocks on the loading drawing; neither 170 t nor the number of supports alone establishes a suitable arrangement.

## The 170 t figure cannot be divided equally between supports

| Parameter | 2021 proposal record | Basis for practical comparison |

| Chamber dimensions | 4500 × 3500 × 2300 mm, length × width × height | Check with workpieces, blocks and handling space. |
| Rated furnace load | 170 t | Fixture inclusion, hot conditions and load distribution are undefined; neither a net workpiece allowance nor permission for local loading. |

| Heating and control | Rated 1200 kW; six zones | Compare zone positions with loading; rated power is not actual consumption. |

The proposal uses a double-layer steel-beam bogie frame and large hollow heat-resistant support columns to transfer loads to the beams. Nine column supports appear in the quotation. Trace contacts through “workpiece—support block—column—bogie frame beam”, particularly whether buyer-supplied blocks align with the designed load-bearing structure. This is a checking method, not evidence that an approved plate-loading drawing exists in the document set.

Nine supports do not mean each carries exactly one ninth of the total. Off-centre plates, stacking or changed block positions alter contact and reaction forces. Provide separate plate and fixture weights, centres of gravity and support drawings, then have the supplier check structural compatibility under hot load. This remains necessary even if total weight is below 170 t.

## Support positions also affect heating beneath the load

The proposal places resistance strips around the chamber and beneath the bogie hearth, with 30 mm-thick heat-resistant hearth plates. Plate and block placement determines both force transmission and bottom heat-transfer space. Process specialists, as well as mechanical designers, should compare the six heating zones with stacking, gaps and principal cross-sections to identify temperature-verification locations.

The proposal includes raised-door safety pins and normal-position door/bogie interlocks. Once the plate-and-block arrangement is established, recheck the verification conditions for hot loaded travel and position retention on power loss against that arrangement.

## Six control zones and twelve thermocouples describe different things

The parameter table specifies six-zone control. The quotation lists six programme controllers, twelve thermocouples and one twelve-channel recorder. These describe control zoning and proposed measurement/recording quantities respectively. The source lacks a final point schedule sufficient to assign every thermocouple specifically to control or alarm duty.

The proposed furnace uniformity is ≤±10°C and control accuracy ≤±1°C, without complete reference bases, test temperatures or loaded sensor layouts. Mark block obstruction, stacking gaps and critical cross-sections on the loading drawing for later alignment with zone and sensor drawings. The twelve thermocouples cannot be allocated directly to those positions without the missing final roles and layout.

## What still needs coordination when blocks are buyer-supplied?

The RT4-1200-10 thick-plate bogie-hearth resistance-furnace quotation of 5 August 2021 includes tax and proposes design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning. Power wiring between furnace and control cabinet, workpiece support blocks, foundations and foundation construction are excluded.

Distinguish equipment support columns from blocks supporting workpieces. Buyer supply of blocks does not remove the supplier's need to check support positions. Include block drawings, material and contact area in loading assessment. Although power wiring appears as a buyer-supplied reference item, the quotation composition and explicit exclusion show that it was outside the historical discounted-total calculation. It cannot be deducted from that total again.

## Starting a comparison for similar thick plates

### Can similar plate dimensions justify reusing the six-zone configuration?

Similar dimensions only start the space comparison. Different materials, thicknesses, stacking, block obstruction and process curves can change critical heating locations. First map the load onto the six zones. Where a critical section lacks a representative temperature point, revise measurement and testing rather than directly adopting the old proposal as an acceptance basis.

### Which information is most useful for an enquiry?

Provide plate material, thickness and outline, batch arrangement and support drawing, separate workpiece and fixture weights, the heating/holding curve and temperature-verification requirements. Existing block or bogie drawings are also useful. These help identify whether support, heat-transfer space or measurement needs adjustment before allocating equipment and buyer-supplied items.
