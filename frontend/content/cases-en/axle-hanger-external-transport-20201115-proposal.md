A through-feed furnace proposal for mine-car axles dated 15 November 2020, retained in Suneng's records, includes high-temperature hangers, the furnace and controls. External transfer equipment and the spray-quenching area are buyer-supplied. The hangers connect to the external transfer mechanism by pins and have guiding supports and a travelling seal at the furnace roof. Review the complete operating route through the hangers, connections and roof passage. Supplying hangers with the furnace does not mean that all transport and quenching equipment is included.

The process begins by mounting parts on heat-resistant steel hangers in the loading/unloading area. An external drive moves them into the furnace and, after heating, to the external spray and unloading areas. Scope review therefore cannot stop at the furnace opening. The hanger travels through these areas, so external-equipment parties must help confirm its connection and movement conditions. Chamber dimensions do not establish the suspension system's allowable total load or external-track capacity.

## Check the travelling roof seal along the hanger route

The proposal places a guided sliding support frame and travelling seal outside the roof, with protection of the roof lining as a stated purpose. Check hanger position relative to the roof passage on entry, during travel and on exit, and how guidance, support and sealing follow these movements. The source provides no complete load-path drawing. The purpose of protecting the lining does not establish that one particular roof support carries the hanger's entire weight.

Three parts per hanger is the proposal's fixture quantity and must be related to actual axle types and individual weights. Mark suspension points and pin positions, part-to-part and part-to-wall clearances, then check orientation and available space throughout transfer. Three small axles and three large axles are not equivalent loads simply because the count matches. The source does not specify workpiece materials or total hanger load; this article adds neither grades nor tonnage.

## Automatic doors do not establish complete external-drive interlocks

Pneumatic front and rear doors open for entry and discharge respectively, and the temperature-control section includes a drive-abnormality alarm. These describe intended actions and protection, but do not replace an interface table for door position, external-drive position, hanger location and readiness of the spray area. Where the buyer supplies external equipment, define who supplies each signal, who permits movement, and how parts in the furnace are handled if receiving fails or a drive stops. Verify these arrangements against the final documents.

Because spray quenching is within the buyer's scope, the actual route from heating exit to cooling position must be checked against process limits. The source gives no confirmed complete transfer time, spray pressure or cooling curve. Automatic door opening cannot be recast as an achieved rapid-quench cycle, and the 950°C rated temperature does not establish axle material or process.

## Hand over supply, condition and inspection information together

Other listed parameters are 550 kW, a chamber size of 3800 × 1200 × 3600 mm and furnace-temperature uniformity of ≤±10°C. The parameter and heating-element sections state six zones, whereas one shell paragraph states four zones and four thermocouple sockets. Final measuring points and control zoning must be reconciled before creating an acceptance schedule. Hanger interfaces and furnace-zone temperature verification are separate checks.

The source classifies the high-temperature hangers as wear parts and calls for condition checks during loading and unloading. Handover can include hanger construction and material documents, loading drawings, pin interfaces, inspection requirements and spare-parts scope. A hanger warranty clause does not remove routine inspection or establish verified long-term suspension capacity. Neither the draft supply scope nor acceptance plan proves completed manufacture, operation or acceptance.

Initial discussions with Suneng should include axle and suspension-point drawings, the three-part loaded arrangement, and layouts of existing external drives and spray equipment. Then define the roof passage, sealing and door-position signals. The original proposal allocates building power, utilities and cooling facilities to the buyer, and secondary electrical work between equipment and control cabinet to the seller. Connect these elements through the scope signed off for the current purchase; a through-feed furnace does not automatically include every external conveyor.
