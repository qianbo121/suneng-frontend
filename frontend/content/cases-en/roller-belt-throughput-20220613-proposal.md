Belt speed determines passage through the equipment, while the proposed approximately 1000 kg/h capacity also depends on loading and process. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s support-roller mesh-belt furnace proposal dated 13 June 2022 lists a 9 m heating section, 1000 mm effective width and 20–100 mm/min drive speed. It explicitly makes output dependent on workpiece shape, weight, stacking and process requirements. Use representative parts to establish loading and heating needs before assessing the capacity figure.

## Establish how parts lie on the belt

Effective width is 1000 mm and effective height 150–300 mm; opening height is separately 350 mm. Passing the opening does not establish permissible stacking in the effective zone. Provide geometry, individual weight, orientation, layers and gaps together with heat-treatment requirements. Filling the belt width does not justify adopting the output figure.

The mesh belt and supporting rollers move synchronously, with edge guards on both sides of the high-temperature belt. Rollers and belt have separate structural roles. Check contact/local loads, loading stability and guard clearances for new parts; supporting rollers do not establish stable transport for arbitrary piles.

## Belt speed affects heating-section residence; loading also constrains output

| Proposal information | Value | Assessment basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heating-section length | 9000 mm | Heating only, excluding inlet and later cooling sections. |
| Drive speed | 20–100 mm/min | Adjustable configuration, not maximum speed suitable for every process. |
| Production capacity | Approximately 1000 kg/h | Explicitly depends on workpiece, stacking and process. |

Higher speed shortens passage through a fixed section. Reaching the required condition also depends on size, material, arrangement and actual temperatures. First confirm that the process fits the residence time, then assess load mass per unit belt length. Faster travel alone does not establish more conforming output.

The detailed sequence is manual loading, belt heating/equalisation, high-temperature cooling, air cooling and discharge. Heating residence is not total loading-to-discharge time. Downstream cooling or collection bottlenecks also constrain capacity. Other equipment names appearing in the source overview are not automatically added to this detailed sequence.

## Match zone records to actual heating conditions

The source lists 400 kW, four control zones and four roof circulation fans, with heaters in air ducts. These are configurations, not verified results for every stacking pattern. Uniformity ≤±5°C is limited to the same zone; hot acceptance additionally describes three-point measurement at 950°C. It is not a ±5°C guarantee at every line position or on every workpiece.

To verify approximately 1000 kg/h, use agreed representative parts/loading and record belt speed, zone temperatures, cooling and discharge conditions, then assess workpiece results as agreed. The proposal includes deformation and hardness in final acceptance. This describes what must be checked, not successful trial results.

The buyer provides primary wiring from building supply to cabinet; the seller provides secondary wiring and materials from cabinet to equipment inputs. Site water, electrical and gas services and a cooling pond or tower are buyer scope. The buyer conducts process commissioning with seller assistance. Supply representative parts, process requirements and site cooling conditions together; equipment installation does not replace these services.
