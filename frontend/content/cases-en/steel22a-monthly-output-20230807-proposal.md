Monthly output needs calculation from actual batch loading and the complete furnace cycle. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s 22A steel annealing proposal dated 7 August 2023 addresses a 500 t/month requirement with two bogie-hearth furnaces, five pieces per furnace and approximately 2–2.2 t per piece. This supports initial selection, but “two furnaces, five pieces each” does not establish 500 t/month production.

## Separate the monthly target from the five-piece batch

The source gives φ1500 × height 940 mm parts, approximately 2–2.2 t each, with five per furnace and two furnaces. Customer monthly demand, batch count and equipment quantity are distinct. Changes to individual mass or layout affect scheduling; establish actual net batch input from current parts and drawings.

The same paragraph says “approximately 12 tonnes/batch”, and the table rates loading at 12 t. Yet five pieces at 2–2.2 t total approximately 10–11 t. Therefore 12 t is not directly the net weight of those five, and the difference cannot automatically be assigned to fixtures or spacers. Separate workpiece weight, auxiliary loads and equipment capacity in a loading schedule.

## The complete cycle matters more than empty heat-up time

Empty-furnace heat-up is ≤2 h; loaded heating is described only as programmable and continuously adjustable. Empty time cannot replace heating five parts, much less the full cycle. Identify equipment occupancy for loading, heating, holding, cooling and unloading, then use production days and downtime to determine monthly cycles.

The two furnaces can be scheduled separately, but the source gives no complete staggered schedule or verified cycle. It does not establish doubled output. If one furnace cannot achieve planned cycles, identify whether handling, loaded heating, cooling or available days is limiting before changing equipment. Merely increasing rated loading on paper is insufficient.

## Map five pieces to chamber and airflow conditions

The proposed chamber is 8200 × 1700 × 1200 mm, rated at 750°C and 450 kW, with four zones along its length, for annealing and other heating of 22A steel parts below 750°C. Part dimensions φ1500 × height 940 mm are only initial conditions. Show gaps, supports and handling; chamber dimensions do not establish usable space for five tightly packed parts.

Sectional heat-resistant steel hearth plates and four water-cooled circulation fans affect bottom heat transfer, circulation space and site water. Recheck actual supports/gaps after loading changes. Four zones or fans do not prove workpiece uniformity or output.

## What makes the monthly schedule credible?

Provide net weights, dimensions/loading, the specified annealing curve, permitted cooling, shifts and monthly available hours, plus lifting/unloading conditions. Schedule both furnaces using actual conforming output per batch and complete cycle duration to assess the 500 t target.

Suneng proposes site installation/commissioning; the buyer prepares foundations/facilities and provides air, water, electricity, gas and overhead-crane support. The source explicitly places empty commissioning before loaded heat-up commissioning. If monthly output is central, include representative loading and cycle verification in the parties' agreement. Installation or empty commissioning is not monthly-output verification.
