Suneng's completion proposal for a 20-metre variable-volume gas-fired furnace lists the rear-wall sealing mechanism as already installed on site, but explicitly states that its sealing pipework is still missing. It also schedules lining installation, mechanical completion and electrical-control work. Readiness for commissioning depends on the connections between actuators, sealing sequence, position signals and controls, not merely the presence of mechanisms.

## Distinguish existing equipment from work still required

| Item | Source status | Pre-commissioning check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rear-wall sealing mechanism | Equipment already provided on site; sealing pipework outstanding | Actuators, pneumatic circuit and control signals connected as one system |
| Combustion and air supply | Burners and air-supply equipment listed as already provided | Actual condition checked with completed pipework and controls |
| Lining, mechanics and controls | Fibre-module installation, mechanical finishing and electrical-control completion | Connections between new and reused parts and commissioning responsibilities |

The proposal lists effective lengths of 20 m and 10 m, a maximum working temperature of 1100°C and 20 temperature-control zones overall. Maximum load is stated only as “according to the original agreement, excluding support blocks”, without tonnage. Width also appears as both 6 m and 5.84 m after construction. This page does not import tonnage from another project. Reconcile effective space, structural clear dimensions and the original agreed loading in the final revision.

The parameter table specifically gives variable lengths of 20 m and 10 m, maximum working temperature 1100°C, and 20 control zones for the whole furnace.

## Trace rear-wall sealing through sequence, pneumatics and feedback

The rear wall is attached to the bogie frame, with pneumatic push rods and refractory-fibre sealing blocks around its perimeter. After the bogie reaches its intended position, the blocks extend; after heating, they retract in sequence. The rods have different stroke lengths. Match each block, cylinder, pipe, action sequence and position feedback at site inspection. Demonstrating that one cylinder extends and retracts does not validate the entire perimeter seal and bogie sequence.

The control section allows bogie operation only after the door reaches its highest position and rear-wall seals release. Door lowering and rear-wall sealing follow only after the bogie reaches its entered position. Verify the final programme against actual position detection. Cabinet buttons or installed mechanisms do not replace interlock testing.

## Reduced chamber length also changes control and exhaust conditions

The source specifies settings and interlocks after volume changes. Two exhaust outlets serve the two-section space and share one waste-heat recovery unit. For operation at 10 m instead of 20 m, confirm active burners, measurement points, exhaust and furnace-pressure conditions in the final design. Twenty zones overall do not automatically mean ten active zones in the shorter chamber, and halving length does not prove halved gas consumption.

## Use a completion list before testing

The seller's scope includes completion of lining, door seals, natural-gas pipework, air supply, combustion and electrical controls, with secondary service lines expressly included. The buyer connects power to the low-voltage distribution cabinet, provides the gas-supply interface, and supplies support blocks and fixtures. Reconcile each existing item and outstanding task. “Complete finishing package” does not mean replacing all old equipment, and this project's secondary-line inclusion must not be applied to other proposals.

The plan calls for equipment performance tests and two trial-production batches, checking process curves, gas consumption, furnace uniformity during soaking and workpiece temperature differences. These are proposed pre-delivery checks; corresponding completion records are absent. Review the original agreement, current condition and final drawings first, then test with defined workpieces and operating conditions. “Already provided” and acceptance clauses are not proof of acceptance.
