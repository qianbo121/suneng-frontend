Suneng's 2021 continuous stainless-steel annealing furnace specification uses flue gas first to preheat strip, then combustion air, and finally to generate steam in a waste-heat boiler. Steam heats drying air through an exchanger and is also distributed through a pipe network. Beyond proposed steam output, confirm destinations, timing and heat demand at each user, and how these match changing furnace load.

## Draw flue-gas and heat-user paths separately

The three recovery stages serve incoming strip, combustion air and boiler feedwater. In the drying section, steam heats air that is then directed onto the strip; neither combustion gas nor steam is described as blowing directly onto workpieces. Check flue-gas discharge, steam distribution and drying air separately. Connected heat-transfer paths do not make their media interchangeable.

## Match steam production to demand

The steam-control section describes workshop-network distribution and separately addresses excess steam. Generation and demand are not inherently equal. For recovery assessment, list drying and other heat users' schedules, loads and connection requirements against the actual furnace production plan. Multiplying 5 t/h by annual hours does not establish customer energy savings already achieved.

The boiler section gives maximum recovered heat of 5000 kW, while the preceding combustion-air exchanger has its own heat-transfer rating. These belong to different locations and cannot simply be added to fuel costs as benefits. The historical proposal describes a heat-use approach. Actual usable heat requires calculation from flue-gas inlet conditions, feedwater and steam states, user demand and metering boundaries.

## Check drying air and strip temperatures separately

Drying comprises ambient-temperature high-pressure blowing and medium-pressure hot-air blowing, addressing surface water and subsequent drying. The 80–120°C range belongs to the hot-air nozzles; permitted strip temperature at dryer exit is separately stated as 80°C. These temperatures are not interchangeable. Suitable steam pressure alone does not establish drying performance. Validate airflow, hot-air temperature and exit strip temperature at actual line speed and incoming surface-water conditions.

## Confirm design, supply and metering in one interface package

The specification lists steam flow, temperature and pressure measurements and records, separates workshop pipework from equipment connections, and requires the seller to provide medium, flow, pipe-size and connection data. Suneng undertakes design, supply, installation and commissioning within its allocated scope. Confirm the boiler, network, instruments and inspection documents individually. Inclusion of a waste-heat boiler in a furnace project does not prove that Suneng manufactures every component itself or that inspection is complete.

The flue-gas section both describes discharge to a chimney and separately provides connection to the buyer's workshop desulphurisation main. Confirm the final discharge interface against the general arrangement and responsibility schedule. Heat recovery, flue-gas treatment and steam use are related but distinct. Preheating or steam-generation capability does not prove emissions compliance. Historical valve-adjustment or abnormal-operation text is not used here as site operating guidance.
