Suneng's 2024 continuous stainless-steel annealing furnace documents separate lifting rolls into lifting mechanisms, brackets, seals and roll drives, with a further roll-changing mechanism using an overhead crane. Confirm strip support, lifting at both ends, sealing, drive control and handling space together. Lifting rolls alone do not establish roll changes without stopping production under every operating condition.

The strip-support table lists water-cooled lifting support rolls in preheating and heating sections, with different rolls at cooling, squeeze-drying and drying locations. Not every roll has the same lifting construction. The layout should identify the specific roll being replaced, which rolls continue supporting the strip, and removal/installation space.

## Connect movement, sealing and drive matching to controls

The source drives furnace rolls through geared motors and universal couplings, with encoders and one-way bearings to match roll and strip speeds. Main-line electrical controls provide drive-control components, cabinets and variable-frequency drives. The furnace supplier's mechanical parameters and functional requirements must reach the whole-line control supplier. Demonstrating roll rotation or hoist movement alone is insufficient.

The detection list includes seal-position feedback, and alarms include abnormal roll temperature and limit position. These lists do not define every motion permission. Final controls should specify when lifting/lowering is allowed, how position is confirmed, when seals restore and how signals affect whole-line operation. This article does not create a roll-changing procedure from detector names.

## Prepare the crane and platforms

The changing section lists a 1.1 kW motor and nearby power boxes. That is roll-changing equipment, not the furnace-roll drive rating. Roll drives appear as both 3 kW and 2.2 kW in the structural section and motor table; reconcile them in the final motor list. Crane capacity, lift points, clearance and service platforms depend on roll/mechanism weights and motion envelopes. No lifting capacity is invented here.

The buyer's design scope includes workshop cranes; its supply scope lists service-hoist beams and transport equipment. The seller provides mechanical/electrical function descriptions, motor lists and installation/maintenance documents. Match tools and lifting preparations to the mechanical/electrical responsibility table, avoiding unresolved routes or power locations when furnace equipment arrives.

## Validate uninterrupted roll changing under agreed conditions

The overview states rapid roll changes without production stoppage as a design objective. Commissioning requires planned and recorded tests of limits, interlocks, roll drives and cooling. Buyers can request validation conditions based on actual strip width, tension, speed, roll temperature and handling arrangements, then establish the applicable operating range. The proposal contains no completed roll-changing operating record. Having the mechanism does not prove uninterrupted changes under every condition.
