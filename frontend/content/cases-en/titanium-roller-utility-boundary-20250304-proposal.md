Even a “turnkey” scope needs separate utility, power and foundation handover points. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s titanium-plate roller-hearth furnace proposal dated 4 March 2025 sets utility-piping handover 1.5 m outside the equipment and electrical handover at the incoming side of the distribution cabinet's main switch. These concern different work; one distance line cannot define all site responsibilities.

## Separate external-network design from equipment piping

The buyer designs piping from the utility network to the handover point. The seller designs piping/accessories from there to equipment use points and piping within its supply scope. Seller equipment piping includes connection to buyer handover points, connecting fittings and hardware. The seller must also recommend accessory types at buyer interfaces.

The 1.5 m distance is therefore a location, not automatically the boundary for all material and installation costs. Separately allocate upstream pipe materials, construction, testing and connection conditions. Interface drawings must identify actual equipment outlines, joints and media, rather than only a distance on a plan.

## Main power and fault-condition power need separate handovers

The buyer brings main power to the incoming side of the cabinet's main switch; the installation section specifies a three-phase, four-wire AC380V feed to the main incoming cabinet. A dual-supply automatic transfer cabinet is proposed for protected supply to furnace rollers, doors, entry rollers and control/display systems, with an independent uninterruptible supply for controllers.

These serve different objects. Main supply depends on the complete load. Backup supply needs its source, capacity, duration and required retained actions defined, then switching/interlocks checked. A transfer cabinet or uninterrupted controller does not prove that plates can exit or rollers keep turning after a failure. Confirm actions through final electrical design and tests.

## Hand over water and compressed air by use point

This is electrically heated equipment. The specified site “gas” utility is compressed air; it does not imply a fuel-gas system. Circulating-water conditions include maximum inlet 35°C and outlet 45°C. Furnace roller bearing housings are water-cooled, connected through metal-braided hoses, with water-pressure display and alarms.

Assign inlet temperature, pressure, return conditions and flow to actual use points. Bearing cooling is not a plate-cooling process. Confirm affected components, interlocks and responses for water interruption, low pressure or power failure with the interfaces.

## Confirm plate scope and final furnace configuration before interface drawings

The product range is titanium/titanium-alloy plate, 10–160 mm thick, 800–2800 mm wide and 1500–15000 mm long. Different combinations affect occupied space and entry/exit handling. External handovers follow the final general arrangement, not one representative plate alone.

The source retains differing furnace lengths, effective widths and temperature statements or options. This article selects no final ordering configuration. Confirm the product range and parameter set before updating furnace, handling auxiliaries and maintenance space. The explicit handover locations/responsibilities are usable references; not all dimensions are frozen.

## Suneng drawings and site installation: what must the buyer prepare?

The buyer constructs foundations from the seller's foundation information, with joint inspection before installation. Suneng proposes foundation and water/electricity/compressed-air interface drawings and site equipment installation/commissioning. Required documents also include general arrangement, pneumatic/hydraulic and electrical schematics, wiring and control programmes. Site networks, foundations and incoming power still need individual allocation; installation/commissioning does not mean the seller supplies every factory condition.
