Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s September 2023 aluminium cylinder solution-furnace proposal coordinates lifting with a forced pull-down mechanism in the tank. Transfer is timed from door opening to complete immersion, while both the upper lifting hook and lower wire-rope hook are explicitly connected manually. Include these manual steps on the same operating diagram when assessing the complete sequence.

## Manual connections are part of the loading and rapid-lowering arrangement

The operating outline first moves the loading cart beneath the furnace and lowers the lift. An operator secures the lifting hook to the rack's upper suspension point, then raises the rack into the heating zone. The solution-treatment tank moves to its specified position beneath the furnace; an operator connects its pull-down wire-rope hook to the rack's lower anchor before closing the door and heating. Upper load support and lower pulling connections have different duties; this is not simply “automatic loading”.

After heating/holding, the door opens and the lift lowers rapidly while the tank pull-down mechanism starts. Afterwards, the rack is lifted clear, the lower connection is manually released, the tank moves away and the loading platform moves beneath the rack. The rack is lowered, unhooked and unloaded. For each connection, release and movement, establish what supports the rack, who confirms connection state and how personnel leave before the next action.

## Verify 15 seconds together with connection states

Operating temperature is 550°C and design temperature 650°C; the purpose is aluminium cylinder solution treatment. Confirm rack, cylinder openings and water-entry conditions for actual parts. Forced pull-down is a proposed mechanism, not evidence that trapped air clears itself. Nominal descent speed is not a measured complete-immersion time for all cylinders.

The document describes chain pull-down, wire-rope pull-down and a hydraulic-cylinder/pulley assembly in different places. These support discussion of the interfaces, but approved drawings must reconcile the final drive and connection structure. This article selects none or adds automatic hook-lock detection. Representative-load trials must use the reconciled mechanism, connection states and timing points.

## Separate a moving full tank from the lifting rack

The tank trolley uses a braked variable-frequency motor, dual protective limits at both travel ends and door interlocks. It moves the tank assembly; rack lifting and tank travel do not share one load definition. Confirm liquid, agitators and rack positions in each state, including release of the lower connection before moving the tank away.

The proposal includes internal agitation, adjustable water temperature 30–40°C, replenishment, overflow, sludge drainage and temperature recording, plus recirculating cooling. Confirm continuous-operation water temperature, effective level and supply/drain interfaces together. One successful immersion does not establish identical cooling for every subsequent batch.

## Use the same operating version for equipment and process acceptance

Elements form 2 upper/lower control zones, with uniformity ≤±4°C. Verify these with the actual rack and representative loading. Fan descriptions include both a water-cooled support and air-cooled bearings; map final components and water/air interfaces individually. Fan cooling and tank cooling are not one system.

Suneng proposes installation/commissioning; the buyer prepares facilities from foundation information and supplies air, water, electricity, gas and an overhead crane. Before handover, include manual connections/releases, tank positioning and lifting trials in individual and integrated records, followed by agreed loaded verification.
