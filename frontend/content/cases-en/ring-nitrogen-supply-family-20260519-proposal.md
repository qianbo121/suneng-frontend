Nitrogen protection in a proposal does not necessarily mean that a nitrogen generator is included in the equipment quotation. Two bearing-ring spheroidising annealing furnace documents from Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd., dated May 2026, set different boundaries: one explicitly excludes nitrogen supply equipment, while the other lists nitrogen-generation equipment and states that Suneng will purchase it from its manufacturer. Separate the furnace gas circuit from the gas-source equipment before comparing responsibilities.

## These documents are compared for supply scope

The two proposals cover different working dimensions and configurations. This page compares only their explicit gas-supply responsibilities. The sources do not establish that they are alternative proposals for one project. Do not combine their specifications into a single configuration or infer which is cheaper or more energy-efficient from dimensions or heating power alone.

## Define the gas source before connecting the furnace circuit

Although the φ1500 mm proposal includes automatic protective-gas admission and discharge valves, it expressly excludes nitrogen supply equipment. The buyer should establish the conditions available from the intended site gas source before matching it to the furnace connection. “Nitrogen-protection capability” and “gas-source equipment included” need separate purchasing entries.

The φ1100 mm proposal says Suneng will purchase a nitrogen generator. However, the cited section does not fully specify output, purity, pressure or the associated auxiliary equipment. It confirms proposed inclusion of nitrogen-generation equipment, but does not establish that the compressor, receiver and all pretreatment equipment are included. Obtain a nitrogen-system configuration list matched to the furnace's backfilling and operating requirements.

## Check gas quality together with loading conditions

The nitrogen-equipment section relates oxidation and decarburisation after annealing to the loading-space gas tightness, protective-gas type and purity, and workpiece surface cleanliness. Equipment comparison therefore requires more than checking whether a fan or nitrogen generator is present. Provide the actual ring material, surface requirements and loading cleanliness conditions, and define the relevant measurements. The source provides no completed workpiece-surface inspection; buying a nitrogen generator is not evidence of zero oxidation.

Both proposals include water-cooled retort seals and pressure-related components. The φ1500 mm document also describes furnace-body cooling air and discharge of hot air outside the workshop. Connect protective nitrogen, equipment cooling water and furnace-body cooling air separately according to the drawings. External cooling air is not the protective gas inside the retort. Final gas-circuit and control documents are still needed for abnormal operating conditions in both proposals; this article adds no unprovided gas-admission thresholds or exhaust procedures.

## Carry the same boundary into quotation and acceptance

When comparing prices, first list furnace pipework, the external gas source, connection work and commissioning responsibilities for each proposal. Identify omissions before comparing totals. Both proposed that Suneng undertake equipment interconnection, wiring and installation, while the buyer connected water, electricity and gas and completed part of the electrical-cabinet installation. An installation agreement does not automatically supply equipment explicitly excluded elsewhere. Trial production and seal checks also appear in the sources; verify the specific surface quality and gas-source performance against the confirmed supply scope and workpiece requirements.
