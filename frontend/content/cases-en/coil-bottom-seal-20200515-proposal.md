A tight lid alone does not establish the sealing of a bottomless muffle retort. Suneng's proposal for spheroidising annealing of bearing-steel wire coils describes two separate arrangements: the retort bottom inserted into an oil-seal trough, and a clamped lid at the furnace-mouth flange. Both have specified cooling provisions. Check each seal, its cooling and its abnormal-condition signals separately; one component in position or one temperature alarm does not prove that the whole retort has passed a seal test.

## Distinguish the two sealing arrangements

| Location | Original proposal | Interface to confirm separately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retort bottom | Bottomless muffle retort inserted into a water-cooled sealing trough using oil as the sealing medium | Liquid level, oil temperature, cooling and retort insertion geometry |
| Lid | Two annular packing grooves in the furnace-mouth flange; lid clamped by handwheels | Packing, clamping contact and water cooling at the seal |
| Effective loading space | φ3450×3200 mm inside the flow-guide cylinder | The φ3750×4300 mm retort interior is not the loading envelope |

## What does the oil-temperature alarm establish?

The bottom-seal section specifies a water jacket, oil filling and draining ports, and a level indicator. If seal-oil temperature exceeds the stated conditions, an alarm prompts personnel to check cooling-water passage and flow. This supports the statement that oil-temperature monitoring is proposed. It does not establish automatic shutdown of all gas or heating upon cooling-water failure. Confirm the actual alarm interlocks in the relevant control drawings and abnormal-condition procedures.

The upper seal consists of the furnace-mouth flange, packing, handwheel clamping and water cooling. Checking lid clamping does not replace checking the bottom oil seal. Cooling-water connections alone do not prove adequate cooling at every seal. List the upper and lower structural drawings, cooling branches and inspection methods separately at handover.

## Use the correct space for loading and circulation checks

The effective space is φ3450×3200 mm inside the flow-guide cylinder; the muffle-retort interior is φ3750×4300 mm. These are different dimensions. The guide cylinder works with the retort, the base supports workpieces or baskets, and circulation enters the workpieces from below. The loading drawing should show coils, bases or baskets and the guide cylinder together. Additional space inside the retort cannot automatically be counted as usable loading space.

The source lists approximately 2.1 t per coil, eight coils and a loading range of 16–20 t. Multiplying the stated piece weight and quantity gives approximately 16.8 t. This is an arithmetic check of those figures, not evidence for 20 t of net workpieces per batch. Confirm actual coil weights, how baskets or bases are counted, and stacking clearances.

## Hand over protective-gas and supply interfaces clearly

The proposal lists a maximum operating temperature of 850°C, a usual temperature of 810°C and nitrogen and propane as protective gases. These figures do not constitute a complete process suitable for every bearing-steel wire. It specifies a gas valve rack and furnace-pressure regulation, while assigning the nitrogen generator, nitrogen pipework from generator to furnace, and pipework and pressure-reducing valve from propane cylinders to furnace to the user. Confirm gas equipment, seal cooling and furnace regulation within their respective boundaries, and obtain operating and abnormal-condition documents applicable to the equipment.

Suneng's proposed scope includes design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning, but excludes power wiring between furnace and control cabinet and equipment foundation construction. Supplied documents were to include gas-circuit and electrical schematics and operating and maintenance instructions. For a new project, ensure these cover both seals, cooling, gas supply and corresponding abnormal-condition actions, then agree the inspection records. “Installation and commissioning included” does not mean all external interfaces are included.
