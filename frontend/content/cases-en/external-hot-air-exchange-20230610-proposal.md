A 2023 Suneng hot-air chamber-furnace proposal separately provides external recirculating heating and fresh-air replacement controlled by oxygen measurement. Recirculation sends furnace air through an external heating chamber and back; fresh-air replacement exhausts used gas and introduces new air according to oxygen readings. Review each system's purpose and control conditions separately.

## Recirculation returns heat; fresh-air replacement has separate paths

The external circulation system comprises insulated ducts, a heating chamber, an open-pattern plate heater and a high-temperature induced-draught fan. Resistance strips are inside the external heater. The fresh-air system separately has exhaust and inlet ducts and a heat exchanger. Mark furnace return flow, heated supply, fresh-air inlet and exhaust outlet individually. An external heating chamber does not make every recirculating airflow fresh air.

## An oxygen probe does not complete atmosphere and extraction requirements

The proposal starts the extraction fan when oxygen is insufficient, discharging used gas and bringing fresh air through the exchanger; it stops that fan when oxygen is sufficient. This describes an oxygen-signal-based ventilation concept but gives no project oxygen target, probe position, exchange flow or complete response to probe failure.

If parts release gas or volatiles during heating, confirm their types, quantity per batch and release stages. Even a target oxygen reading does not establish removal of every released substance. Minimum process exhaust, external treatment and abnormal-condition actions require separate definition. The reviewed draft does not map these into corresponding design conditions, and this page does not promote stopping ventilation when oxygen is sufficient as a general operating rule.

## Furnace master control and heater auxiliary control measure different locations

The parameter table describes one heating zone with furnace master control and heater auxiliary control and lists two temperature controllers. The control drawing should show each sensor's position, controlled object and limits. Two instruments do not automatically mean two independent process zones or two independent overtemperature-protection systems.

Changes in circulation affect heat transfer between the external heater and furnace. Check fan-running feedback and heating permission together. The source describes door interlocks and overtemperature heating cut-off, but not a complete circulation-loss detection and interlock table. The electrical drawings need to clarify these functions; equipment names do not establish implementation.

## Base loading and acceptance on actual airflow and process

The original proposal lists a maximum operating temperature of 550°C and maximum load of 1 t, with an internal trolley and external receiving trolley. It does not establish whether the 1 t includes every loading fixture. The current requirement should provide the complete loaded envelope, total weight and arrangement on the trolley, including blockage of supply/return flow, rather than merely whether the door closes.

Begin with a system diagram marking recirculation, fresh air, exhaust and measuring points. Map actual workpiece materials, temperature curves, required oxygen conditions and released-substance information onto it. The draft proposes empty-furnace commissioning followed by loaded heating. Temperature, ventilation and abnormal states should be checked under agreed loading and control conditions. Existing fans and an oxygen probe alone do not establish acceptable process quality.
