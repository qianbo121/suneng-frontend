Rapid furnace discharge covers only part of hot-workpiece transfer; downstream cooling still needs a defined interface. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s gas-fired roller-hearth quenching-furnace documents dated 13 March 2024 propose rapid discharge after heating, while assigning the quench press, cooling system and settling/deslagging to the buyer. Spray-quenching method is marked “to be determined”. This is an interface reference, not a confirmed or delivered complete press-quenching line.

## Separate furnace, rapid-discharge rollers and quench press

The source identifies plate-shaped workpieces, manually carried or lifted onto a loading frame and fed by rollers for heating/holding. Infrared detection triggers rapid discharge towards the proposed quench equipment. The equipment list separately includes loading/unloading frames, furnace, rapid-discharge frame and inclined frame; the inclined slide frame is unpowered. Receiving and external press actions are not thereby one completed control programme.

## Receiving-equipment readiness belongs in action permissions

The source describes infrared detection at discharge position instructing rear-door opening and rapid transfer, followed by door closure and return to low speed. This describes intended rapid-discharge behaviour, not the interface agreement with a buyer's press.

Using actual plates and receiving elevation, check opening, rapid rollers, inclined frame and press entrance for clearance and continuous support. Define feedback for receiver readiness, workpiece position, jams and failed receipt. If downstream equipment is unready, agree permissible waiting and fault responses rather than merely increasing upstream speed.

Furnace and discharge-speed statements differ between sections. This article therefore calculates no definite transfer time or line cycle. The relevant process interval runs from leaving the effective heating zone to entering the agreed cooling condition. Test that full interval using agreed events; maximum speed of one roller section cannot replace it.

## Buyer-side presses, pumps and cooling must still join the design review

The source explicitly assigns water-supply/drainage pumps, reservoirs and auxiliaries to the buyer, along with press, cooling and settling/deslagging. Describing press components in the body does not change their supply responsibility. With the spray method undecided, no fixed pressure, flow or cooling rate can be added.

Provide plate material, thickness, dimensions, permissible deformation and target microstructure/properties. Confirm furnace heating and post-discharge cooling separately. Maximum temperature 1000°C and cross-sectional furnace uniformity ±10°C are proposed furnace criteria, not resulting plate hardness, metallography or flatness.

## Confirm combustion and movement interlocks with the final configuration

The heat source is direct-fired natural gas. Proposed functions include flame detection, automatic ignition, gas-main emergency isolation and pressure monitoring, with gas shut-off/alarm on low pressure or fan failure. Doors have travel signals, and mechanical actions use programmed and manual modes. Match these to final burners, doors and discharge conditions; an equipment name or protection list cannot replace complete interlock verification.

Combustion-zone count, burner count and some control descriptions vary in the source. This article selects no final burner configuration or claims verified low emissions or better uniformity. Before connecting an existing press, approve mechanical/control interfaces, then arrange cold integrated checks and representative-workpiece trials.

## What does Suneng propose to supply?

Suneng proposes its furnace, associated rollers and corresponding installation/commissioning. The buyer provides lifting/handling, press/cooling equipment and utilities to specified points; remaining connection cables, piping and installation follow the relevant clauses. Final acceptance proposes 3–5 trial furnace batches and product inspection. These are planned acceptance activities, not completed trial results.
