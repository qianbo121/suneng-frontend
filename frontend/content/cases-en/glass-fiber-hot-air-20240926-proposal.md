When comparing recirculating-air bogie-hearth furnaces, assess how fully loaded racks obstruct openings and ventilation space, rather than counting fans alone. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s RT4-320-5 proposal dated 26 September 2024 includes three circulation fans, a loading rack, perforated plates, adjustable lower air plates and three-zone control. Map actual airflow obstruction to temperature-measurement positions before comparing circulation and controls.

## Check perforations and passages with the rack fully loaded

The proposal places perforated plates on the bogie loading rack and provides fibre insulation in the walls, roof, door and bogie. The relevant question here is whether the ventilation space required by the design remains around the rack and plates after loading.

A loaded rack is more representative than an empty one. Workpieces, trays or liners closing existing holes change the available airflow paths. Compare passages and supports on the loading drawing before changing perforations or air guides; more airflow cannot simply replace this layout check.

## What do the air guides and adjusting plates do?

The source separately describes perforated plates on the rack, shell-mounted air guides controlling furnace-air direction, and adjusting plates at the guides' lower outlets regulating local flow. It states that segmented recessed ribs in the guides “increase structural strength and accommodate thermal expansion”. That is the original design explanation, not an effect inferred here.

These descriptions identify component positions and functions but do not form a complete airflow drawing ready to copy. For new loading, check openings, obstructions and actual flow directions against the layout. Do not invent a confirmed sequence linking perforated plates, guides and lower outlets.

Denser loading or blocked outlets calls for checks of loading gaps, perforated passages and adjustment space. After changes, use agreed sensor positions to assess temperature distribution. Without loaded temperature records, the presence of adjusting plates alone establishes no uniformity result.

## Agree the uniformity requirement before assessing results

The main table specifies furnace uniformity ≤±10°C, while the control section specifies ≤±5°C. The latter is stricter and the requirements are not necessarily mutually incompatible, but the source does not establish the final common acceptance criterion. Test temperature, loading, reference basis and sensor layout are also incomplete. Agree these before assessing whether circulation and measurement cover the requirement.

The proposal also specifies control accuracy ≤±1°C, three type K thermocouples, three programme controllers and one recorder, identified in the quotation as three-channel. Control points need not coincide with critical workpiece locations. Define representative positions for verifying obstructed parts of the load; do not confuse control accuracy, furnace distribution and workpiece temperature.

## Fan cooling, door/bogie movement and site supply

Fan cooling is described as self-circulating heat dissipation using a water tank, separate from furnace hot-air circulation. Confirm tank replenishment, heat rejection and maintenance, plus heating responses to cooling or fan faults. “Water-cooled” does not establish a fixed external flow requirement, and listed protection components do not establish verification of all fault responses.

Normal-position door/bogie interlocks are proposed. Once the actual rack is determined, include loaded travel and maintenance conditions in the interface schedule.

The RT4-320-5 quotation of 26 September 2024 includes tax and proposes design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning. Secondary wiring between furnace and cabinet, foundations and construction are excluded. Although fans and air guides are listed equipment, interfaces and commissioning conditions must be reassessed for changed loading.

## What should you send first?

Provide a loaded-rack photograph or drawing, material and principal cross-sections, separate workpiece and fixture weights, the process curve and temperature-verification requirements. Existing perforated plates, air guides and fan-cooling conditions are also useful. Resolve blocked passages first; add verification methods where temperature requirements extend beyond existing representative sensors. Use these differences to decide whether circulation and control need changes.

### Do three zones and three fans mean three independent constant-temperature furnaces?

No. The source gives zoning and equipment counts, without establishing full thermal separation. Actual distribution still depends on loading, air guidance and measurement conditions; numerical correspondence is not a thermal-performance result.
