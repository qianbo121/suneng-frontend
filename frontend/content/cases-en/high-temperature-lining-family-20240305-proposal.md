Two Suneng 2024 high-temperature bogie-hearth furnace proposals for steel components specify an additional layer of type 1400 zirconia-containing fibre blanket at the junction between heating strips and zirconia-alumina fibre modules. The clause identifies a local material location, not a 1400°C whole-furnace operating temperature. Review the modules, local blanket layer and heating-strip mounting together.

## Read materials by their location

| Item | 700 kW proposal | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complete furnace | Maximum working temperature 1200°C | Equipment working temperature cannot be increased from a local material designation |
| Furnace lining modules | Folded zirconia-alumina refractory fibre modules rated in the source for above 1260°C | Check this module requirement against actual material documents |
| Heating-strip/module junction | Additional layer of type 1400 zirconia-containing fibre blanket | “Type” is retained; not a promise of whole-furnace operation at 1400°C |

The 700 kW proposal has a 5500×2000×1200 mm chamber; the 480 kW proposal has a 4000×2200×900 mm chamber. Both specify a maximum working temperature of 1200°C and this blanket at the same junction. This page compares only that structural requirement. It does not establish interchangeable equipment for one project. Confirm power, dimensions and loading separately for the actual project.

## Locate the stated function within the structure

The heating elements are 0Cr27Al7Mo2 alloy flat strips formed into a serpentine arrangement and suspended using high-temperature corundum ceramic screws. The source then specifies a zirconia-containing blanket anchored and bonded at the strip/module junction to increase lining refractoriness. Check its location, coverage and fixing against the heating strips and anchors. Listing “type 1400 material included” in an overall bill of materials does not establish that the clause is fulfilled.

Any replacement of strips, modules or the blanket should be checked in the same local detail drawing, preserving the specified relationships. The source does not give blanket thickness, complete coverage drawings or anchoring details. No values are added here, and the layer is not described as a validated short-circuit barrier, reaction-isolation layer or fixed-life guarantee.

## Local material specifications are not whole-furnace performance

Both proposals also use zirconia-alumina fibre modules in the door, but separately list dense high-alumina load-bearing bricks, insulating bricks and heat-resistant steel hearth plates for the bogie. The general description “all-fibre” therefore does not replace a component-by-component construction schedule. Match furnace body, door, bogie and heating-strip junction separately, without extending a local reinforcement layer or module grade to the entire load-bearing structure or operating temperature.

The tables give furnace-temperature uniformity of ±10°C. Later clauses call for empty-furnace uniformity testing before loaded heat-up commissioning. These are proposed criteria and inspections. A local blanket cannot establish achieved uniformity, 30% energy savings or longer life. Claims of actual results require records for the corresponding configuration, loading and tests.

## Information to request

Provide process temperatures and loading conditions for the intended steel components. Request materials listed by lining location, a section through the heating-strip/module junction and material certificates. These establish whether the supply list covers the local structure in the proposal. Resolve outstanding thickness, coverage and fixing details in the final drawings.
