Suspending a long shaft through the central lid opening does not mean its weight should rest on the lid. Two Suneng gas-fired pit furnace documents place the external supports of the lifting fixture on brackets at either side of the furnace platform. The lid comprises two semicircular halves that move apart. Show workpiece suspension, external supports and lid movement separately, then confirm who designs and fabricates the fixtures.

## Establish where the workpiece weight is supported

Both documents concern large hydraulic-cylinder barrels, hydraulic support columns and long shaft components. Suspension brackets stand on either side of the platform. The fixture rests externally on these brackets and suspends the workpieces through the lid's central opening. This defines proposed support positions; the opening does not mean the lid carries the full load. Verify fixture feet, suspension connections and side brackets in a coordinated general arrangement and load-path drawing.

## Check clearances for both lid halves

The lid description specifies powered lifting followed by horizontal movement. The semicircular halves move apart, leaving a central lifting opening. For loading and unloading, check lifting gear and fixture projections against the lid's vertical and lateral travel envelopes, including platform brackets and site crane space. The proposed motion does not prove that an actual long shaft can pass in every lifting orientation.

The same section describes a sand-seal trough at the furnace mouth and lid/control-system interlocks. Sealing, lid position and fixture support are separate interfaces. “Lid closed” does not verify correct seating of the fixture. Consult the final action description for the relationship between opening/closing permissions, combustion state and lifting operations. No unspecified interlock sequence is added here.

## The source assigns fixture design and fabrication separately

Both documents state that the supplier designs the fixtures and provides drawings, while the buyer fabricates them. This refers to the lifting fixtures in that section. It does not assign manufacture of all platform brackets or the entire equipment to the buyer, nor does it mean the buyer designs fixtures independently. Include actual workpiece geometry, suspension points and weights in the design information, then confirm fabrication and incoming inspection against the drawings.

The buyer's information list also requires workshop plans and sections, crane rail and lower-chord elevations, hook position, height, lifting capacity and duty classification. These directly affect the loading path for vertically suspended workpieces. Effective heating-zone diameter and depth alone cannot establish that site loading and unloading are feasible.

## Carry the interface into supply and commissioning

Suneng was to design, manufacture, install and commission the furnace and provide foundation information, a commissioning programme and relevant drawings. No-load and loaded trials were also proposed. Assign fixture delivery, bracket installation, lid motion and actual-load validation to the appropriate stages. “Turnkey” does not remove the buyer's obligation to prepare fixtures and site information.

Some generic sections retain terms such as “bogie”. The annealing proposal's lid section gives 950°C while its specification table gives 650°C, and the documents also contain differing recorder-channel descriptions. This article uses only the suspension and split-lid descriptions supported by direct structural details. Reconcile the model, temperature, actions and temperature-measurement list in one drawing revision and technical agreement before use in a new project.
