One car moves each batch from a Suneng gas-fired furnace to a free cooling box. The line has two furnaces and two enclosed boxes. The quoted supply includes five sets of heat-resistant trays with two pieces per set.

## Project features

**Heating and cooling occupy separate stations** — The car moves a batch from the furnace to a free cooling box. While it cools, the furnace can receive another batch.

**One car serves the line** — The two furnaces and two boxes share one car. Schedule a free box and time for the transfer before discharging the next batch.

## Equipment arrangement and process sequence

**Heat the batch** — Two gas-fired furnaces provide batch heating.

**Assign a cooling box** — One car transfers loads between the furnaces and the two cooling boxes.

**Cool in the enclosed box** — Each batch occupies its assigned box for the duration of cooling.

**Unload after cooling** — Once the batch leaves, the box can receive the next charge.

## Scope of supply

Suneng supplies two gas-fired furnaces, two enclosed cooling boxes and one charging car. The quotation includes five sets of heat-resistant steel trays with two pieces in each set.

## Acceptance procedure

**Agree the trial charge first** — Both parties specify the trial charge. The buyer supplies the agreed material.

**Two trial batches** — The agreement requires two trial batches and compliance with the performance criteria. The number refers to batches tested.

## Common questions

**How many fork cars and trays serve the two furnaces?** One car and five sets of heat-resistant steel trays. Each set comprises two pieces. The trays are included in Suneng’s quoted supply.

**Can the hourly intake be calculated from the number of boxes?** Two boxes provide two cooling positions. Intake intervals also depend on cooling occupancy and loading and unloading time. See the related scheduling article for an example.

**Who provides the billets and how many batches are tested?** The buyer provides billets, utilities and consumables. Both parties agree the charge, run two trial batches and check the specified performance.
