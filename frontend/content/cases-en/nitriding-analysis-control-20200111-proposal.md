Temperature records describe the heating conditions; atmosphere measurements support adjustment of ammonia dissociation inside the furnace. Neither replaces the other. A pit nitriding furnace proposal from Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd., signed 11 January 2020, proposed programmed control of temperature and ammonia dissociation using thermocouple and hydrogen-analysis signals respectively. Buyers should specify the measured variables, control actuators and final workpiece inspections separately.

## Follow the chain from measurement to control action

In the source, thermocouples provide temperature signals and a hydrogen analyser provides a signal related to ammonia dissociation. The temperature and nitriding controllers regulate heating and furnace gas according to the specified temperature, ammonia dissociation and time. Atmosphere control varies the on/off duty ratio of the ammonia solenoid valve, while temperature control adjusts the heating output. Match the probes, sampling arrangement, instruments and actuating valves; the label “automatic nitriding control” does not establish the complete configuration.

The source describes one specification as “nitriding potential control accuracy ≤±3% ammonia dissociation”. As written, its evaluation variable is ammonia dissociation. It must not be restated as the same error applying to every measure of nitriding potential, or as a test result for case depth, hardness or microstructure. For a current purchase, ask the supplier to define the instrument's measured variable, the expression of error and the calibration method, then list the required workpiece inspection criteria separately.

## Check recording capability and included recording hardware separately

The proposal describes an industrial computer that records temperature curves and enables retrieval, backup and printing of each batch's data. However, the equipment list identifies that computer as optional. Confirm whether it is selected in the quotation and supply scope, what records data if it is omitted, and whether atmosphere data is also saved. Temperature-curve recording must not be expanded into a claim that all atmosphere records and a long-term traceability system are included.

The alarm list covers excessive furnace temperature, sensor faults, abnormal furnace-gas nitriding potential and completion of the scheduled unloading time. An alarm indication does not establish the final logic for shutting off gas, holding conditions or exiting the process in each abnormal situation. Those actions require handover in the wiring and control-programme documentation; they cannot be supplied by inference in this article.

## Atmosphere measurement also depends on loading and site pipework

The heat-resistant steel loading basket in the source has a perforated grate at its base that also guides circulation. The user supplies the workpiece lifting fixture, supported by bases inside and outside the retort. Although the loading table states 28 pieces per batch, it does not give their specific dimensions, so that quantity cannot be applied to arbitrary parts. Confirm the actual fixtures and workpiece arrangement together with the effective working zone and gas-flow passages, then inspect a representative load.

Site interfaces include the user's gas supply, reduced in pressure and dried before entering the furnace-side valve rack, and the lid's exhaust, sampling, pressure indication and water-cooled seal. The historical document describes different exhaust branches but does not provide a complete set of site exhaust collection, treatment and abnormal-condition interlock documents ready for reuse. Establish the exhaust destination and treatment responsibilities before confirming the atmosphere system. A description of venting in the source is not an approved discharge method for the current project.

## Connect Suneng's proposed supply to site inspection

Suneng was to undertake equipment interconnection, wiring and installation. The buyer was to connect water, electricity and gas and complete part of the electrical-cabinet installation. The source also calls for no-load operation, trial production and furnace-temperature uniformity checks. Review these alongside the selected recording options, measurement calibration and workpiece acceptance criteria to create traceable handover records. The historical proposal itself does not prove that these tests were completed.
