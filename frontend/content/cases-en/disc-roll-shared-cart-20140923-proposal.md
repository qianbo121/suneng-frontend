Suneng's 2014 technical agreement for a 1250 mm continuous annealing and pickling line uses disc-roll assemblies with small water-cooled rolls, serviced by a shared roll-changing trolley, a movable track frame and the workshop crane. Beyond trolley availability, check access to every working position, alignment of movable and fixed tracks, and the connections between power, cooling and seal states.

## Shared tooling requires access to every position

| Configuration | Original agreement | Position-specific check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared roll-changing trolley | 1 listed in this configuration; both ends lift together | Access, end supports and lifting interfaces |
| Movable track frame | Lifted to the working position and joined to fixed tracks | Gauge, alignment, support and crane space |
| Movable power connection | Supply between adjacent furnace shells, connected through a movable socket | Power and cable movement at every maintenance position |
| Disc-roll assembly | Each disc carries 2 small water-cooled metal rolls | Locations of the roll being changed, the supporting roll and water circuits |

The source first moves the track frame into position, then places the trolley on it for the change. “Only one required” describes sharing within this proposal. Check access, platforms, rail joints and lifting at every service position. Trolley quantity does not eliminate the track frame or site handling preparations. Confirm whether two lines share the same tools and how simultaneous maintenance is handled in the final list.

## Disc rotation and resealing are different states

A geared motor drives each disc through a chain and sprocket gearbox; each disc carries two water-cooled rolls within a fixed frame. Seals at both ends open before rotation and close afterward. Drawings and control checks must match these states. A rotating disc does not prove reliable resealing, and this mechanism is not interchangeable with another project's roof-lifted rolls.

The agreement also describes main-roll repositioning and slow support-roll rotation during a hot shutdown. That is a different condition from removal and replacement, and is not converted here into a site procedure. Final operating and maintenance documents must define any brief stoppage, slow running or other permission required. Another support-roll section in the source itself retains “brief shutdown or slow running” as a condition.

## Check water and power with maintenance states

Roll cooling includes outlet-temperature indication/alarm, inlet pressure gauges and emergency-water connections. Review how the roll being changed and water-cooled parts remaining in operation maintain the necessary cooling. Mention of emergency water does not establish a complete changeover plan for every power loss, water loss or removal condition. Define states and conditions in cooling schematics and commissioning records.

Suneng's agreement lists disc-roll changing equipment and associated controls, installation and commissioning. The buyer provides agreed foundations and utility connections. Confirm the equipment layout, movable tracks, trolley power and cooling interfaces together, then validate tool usability under actual maintenance conditions. This page retains proposed arrangements without claiming proven uninterrupted changes or extended service life.
