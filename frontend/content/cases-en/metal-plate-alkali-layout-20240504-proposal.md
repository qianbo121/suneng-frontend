Basket transfer between alkali pots, residue removal inside a pot and emergency liquid transfer after a leak are three different tasks. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s alkaline-cleaning section proposal dated 4 May 2024 separately provides inter-pot drip trays, a liftable false bottom and a mobile emergency-transfer device. For a similar project, place basket movement, lid opening, cleaning and transfer equipment on the same layout and allocate their space and supporting services individually.

## Distinguish the task of each device

| Device | Explicit proposed purpose | Layout interfaces to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inter-pot drip tray | Collect alkali dripping from baskets moving between vessels | Basket route and collection coverage. |
| Internal false bottom | Lift out residue | False bottom, lifting chains and cleaning space. |
| Emergency-transfer device | Transfer alkali urgently if a pot leaks | Mobile pump, lifting points, hoses and connectors. |

These address normal-transfer drips, internal residue and abnormal transfer respectively. A drip tray is not a site-wide leak-containment system, and a false bottom is not a second sealed pot bottom. The cited clauses list a pump and connections but do not specify a receiving vessel, transfer destination, capacity or complete response procedure. Equipment and site providers must establish these in the layout and service schedule.

## Compare basket and lid movement with heater positions

The proposed internal electric radiant tubes sit on both sides of the pot, with guards against scraping by plates during cleaning. A motor/gearbox drives lids sliding open to both sides, with manual and automatic opening functions. Both the interior and opening therefore contain movement spaces that must remain clear.

The 4900 × 1500 × 2200 mm internal crucible working dimensions are not a direct maximum basket envelope. Show plate projections, basket, lifting gear, side heaters and guards on the loading drawing, checking entry, removal and inter-pot transfer. Confirm the open-lid footprint against lifting routes. The source lacks a final workpiece/lifting-gear interface drawing; this article supplies no invented clearances.

## The false bottom and pot supports serve different purposes

The false bottom is specified as 10 mm carbon-steel plate with 4 lifting chains. Unused chains hang on lugs along the vessel's long sides; residue is removed by lifting the false bottom. A separate welded steel-plate mounting base closely supports the pot-bottom plate, with space reserved for manual removal of residual alkali. These are different positions and functions, not one load-bearing structure.

Confirm how the false bottom and chains clear normal loading, whether overhead lifting space exists, and where cleaning access remains around the base. Lifting loads, connection checks and execution conditions require the corresponding design and site documents. This article records intended functions, without turning “lift out” into an operating procedure.

## Which responsibilities need to be fixed in supply documents?

Suneng proposes installation, commissioning and training. The buyer prepares facilities from foundation information and provides commissioning energy and an overhead crane. Also allocate inter-pot collection, positioning of emergency-transfer equipment and receiving facilities, lifting conditions and subsequent cleaning. A mobile pump does not imply inclusion of receiving infrastructure or every emergency-response condition.

Controls include audible/visual overtemperature and broken-thermocouple alarms and automatic heater-power disconnection. These proposed protections do not establish coverage of all leaks, transfers or lifting faults. Confirm required detection, action boundaries and site cooperation in controls and acceptance documents. Empty and loaded commissioning and final acceptance are planned steps.

## What should an enquiry include when baskets already exist?

Provide plate/basket dimensions and loading drawings, the inter-pot transfer method, lifting gear and site plan, followed by the specified medium, process requirements and equipment-service boundaries. Suneng can assess basket, heater-guard, lid and drip-tray relationships and identify missing design information for false-bottom cleaning and transfer equipment.
