## Load conditions for the five trials

**Weigh the furnace load and the transport load separately** — Allow 1 tonne of fittings per furnace batch. For the car, weigh the fittings and accompanying fixtures together.

**Prepare each batch** — Check each batch’s fitting sizes, piece count, tray tare weight and arrival date.

**Keep one record per trial batch** — Record the temperature curve, gas consumption and soaking temperatures against each batch number.

## Installation, commissioning and five trial batches

**Prepare the fixtures** — The buyer prepares trays, lifting gear, support blocks and trial pieces.

**Empty commissioning** — After the factory inspection, install the equipment and run it empty at the buyer’s site.

**Loaded commissioning** — Check heat-up under load using the agreed workpieces and fixtures.

**Run five trial batches** — After empty and loaded commissioning, run five batches of the buyer’s fittings on site.

## Division of supply and load conditions

The furnace rating refers to the fittings. The car rating refers to fittings and fixtures together.

| Agreement item | Application to trial production |
| --- | --- |
| Furnace charge | 1 tonne per batch excluding support blocks. Prepare each batch of fittings to the agreed loading pattern. |
| Carrying capacity of the fork car | With a uniformly distributed load including fixtures, the maximum capacity per lift must be at least 2 tonnes. Weigh the fittings and fixtures together for transfer. |
| Trial materials | The buyer supplies the workpieces, raw materials and consumables. Confirm the size, count, tray tare and delivery time for each batch. |

**Supplier’s scope** — Furnace, fork car, furnace-side natural-gas piping and valves, combustion-air supply, exhaust ducting including an 18 m stack, combustion system, electrical controls and equipment documents.

## What does loaded acceptance involve?

Start with factory inspection, then site installation, no-load commissioning and loaded heat-up. Fittings heat on a tray or in a basket and travel to the water with the fixtures. Acceptance requires five trial batches and compliance with the performance criteria: process curve, gas use, furnace uniformity during soaking and workpiece temperature variation.

## Common questions

**Whose fittings are used after commissioning?** Use the buyer’s stainless fittings. The buyer also supplies trays, lifting gear, blocks, raw materials and consumables for the trial.

**Which furnace-side systems are included?** Along with the furnace and car, the supplier provides gas piping and valves, combustion air, exhaust ducting with an 18 m stack, combustion equipment, controls and equipment documents.

**How many trials are required and what is checked?** Five trial batches of the buyer’s fittings. Check the process temperature curve, natural-gas consumption, furnace uniformity during soaking and temperature differences within the workpiece load.
