Suneng's 2019 pit furnace proposal for machine-tool bed castings uses a fixed furnace body and movable lid, with a travelling gantry offered as an option. The structural description assigns the gantry to lifting and moving the lid. Its stated load figures do not establish workpiece-handling capacity. Review lid movement, workpiece lifting and pit-opening clearances separately when selecting site lifting equipment.

| Travelling gantry | Optional, for lifting and moving the lid | Not automatically a workpiece-handling crane |

The lid rises synchronously on mechanical screw lifts; the gantry then travels under its own drive. The overview's gantry capacity of ≥10 t and the mechanism section's 10 t screw lift relate to the lid system. The source supplies no workpiece-lifting method, lifting attachments or rated workpiece duty, so these figures cannot become an allowable workpiece weight.

## Leave clear space for workpiece handling

The source permits lateral gantry travel only after the lid reaches its raised position. Show the parked open lid, gantry tracks and workpiece route together, confirming exposed furnace-opening area and crane coverage. Knowing the chamber is 12 m long and 6 m wide does not establish that a particular casting can be lowered along the intended route.

Two circulation fans are mounted on the lid. Check moving accessories and their connections as well as the bare steel structure when allowing movement space. The source does not detail all moving connections; this page specifies no cable take-up, fan-connection or lifting-clearance solution. Equipment layout drawings must settle these.

## Define optional equipment and site support in the list

Both overview and structure sections identify the gantry as optional. It is not automatically included in the base equipment. If omitted, agree an alternative lid-opening method and responsibilities. Specify routine workpiece handling independently; an installation-support clause requiring an overhead crane does not establish the long-term loading method after commissioning.

The buyer constructs the furnace foundation from drawings provided by the seller. For secondary wiring, cables and protective conduits, the seller prepares the list and the buyer purchases them. Pit foundations, lid travel tracks and power connections must match final equipment drawings. Historical effective working dimensions are not civil pit dimensions.

## Verify lid movement and loaded heat treatment separately

The proposal defines heating permissions after lid closure and movement permissions after lid raising, followed by empty-furnace and loaded heat-up commissioning after installation. Check mechanism travel, position feedback and handling of actual workpieces separately from loaded heat-treatment criteria. A lid moving clear validates only its motion duty; it does not establish casting annealing results.
