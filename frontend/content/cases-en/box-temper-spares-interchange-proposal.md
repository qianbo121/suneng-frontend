A Suneng chamber tempering-furnace agreement text requires new equipment to be broadly the same as existing workshop furnaces in overall form, configuration and operating procedure, with interchangeable parts. This requirement goes beyond chamber dimensions: tray transport, installation interfaces and controls need to be matched item by item.

## Define similarity and interchangeability separately

The source opens by requiring the new RJX-65-3 chamber tempering furnace to be broadly the same as the workshop's 3 existing units in overall dimensions, component configuration and operating procedures, with interchangeable parts between them. This is a proposed compatibility requirement, not a conclusion from completed replacement trials.

Procurement attachments should distinguish permitted differences under broadly the same from the components covered by interchangeable. Priority items can include frequently replaced trays, baskets, support rollers and controls. Compare mounting positions, connections and operating conditions as well as names and dimensions. If old drawings, actual modification records and physical spare parts disagree, first establish which reference will govern.

## Mechanical fit must be accompanied by compatible movements

The structural section permits parts to enter or leave only after the door reaches its raised position. Tray entry and exit have photoelectric limits interlocked with the door. Circulation fans and heating also require interlocking. Reusing old trays or replacing controls therefore requires checking that these conditions can still be detected correctly, rather than merely confirming that the tray fits inside.

The text lists effective internal dimensions of 1600 × 1200 × 750 mm and a rated temperature of 300°C for low-temperature tempering of automotive parts after quenching. As the tray dimensions approach the available space, new or changed load carriers also require checks of complete workpiece placement, ventilation and passage through the door. These dimensions do not promise treatment of every part in any arrangement.

## Compatibility and treatment results need different acceptance checks

Beyond spare parts and movement interfaces, the source specifies a workpiece-hardness spread of ≤2 HRC, statistically assessed at 9 points on the same layer under full loading. It also requires pre-tempering hardness spread to be ≤2 HRC. This explicit incoming condition must not become a promise to reduce every incoming hardness variation to within 2 HRC. The figure also does not establish that all heat-treatment results from the new and old furnaces already match.

A similar additional purchase should agree two sets of acceptance checks: interchange of selected parts and load carriers, mechanism movements and interfaces; and treatment results for actual workpieces, loading and process requirements. Furnace-temperature uniformity and recording-channel descriptions differ within the source and must be reconciled in the final attachments. This article does not choose one value and turn it into a new-project guarantee.

## Suneng's proposed responsibilities

The historical quotation includes equipment design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning, and external power cabling/conduits between furnace and control cabinet. The buyer supplies the corresponding site conditions. The interchangeability list, old-equipment interface reference and treatment of differences should be agreed with the drawings and used to define the new project's scope and acceptance basis.
