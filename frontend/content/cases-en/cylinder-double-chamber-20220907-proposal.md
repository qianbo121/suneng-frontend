Rack oscillation within a chamber and transfer from front chamber to rear chamber are different movements. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s twin-chamber aluminium cylinder ageing-furnace proposal dated 7 September 2022 gives each chamber an independent drive and infrared limits, linked by an intermediate door. The same project's solution and ageing furnaces propose shared racks, but rack interfaces and permitted cylinder lengths still need separate checks.

## Distinguish oscillation from inter-chamber transfer

Each chamber has one variable-frequency gearmotor, capable of synchronised or separate operation. During loading, the rack stops at an infrared limit at one end, the door closes and heating starts. After a set interval, it reverses to the other limit, stops and restarts the movement timer. This describes oscillation timing, not automatic restarting of process holding time on every reversal.

For transfer, workpieces pass through the intermediate door into the preheated rear chamber. After ageing, the rear door rises and is safety-pin locked; rollers switch on a discharge command to a speed coordinated with the external roller frame. Check chamber stroke, intermediate-door passage and outside receiving position separately, rather than merely confirming that rollers turn.

## Shared racks do not establish identical product limits

The source explicitly shares racks with the solution furnace, using 304 stainless-steel square tube: 50 × 50 mm inner frame and 80 × 80 mm outer frame. However, the solution proposal allows maximum cylinder length 2500 mm and the ageing proposal 2300 mm. Check actual cylinders, restraint and rack envelope over the whole route. “Shared” does not establish that both cover 2500 mm cylinders.

Both loading tables specify 9 cylinders per rack for diameters greater than 406 mm and less than 600 mm, and 25 per rack for 348–406 mm inclusive. These are separate size-dependent loads, not 34 on one rack. Although maximum size is listed as φ600 mm, the nine-cylinder condition is strictly below φ600 mm. Exactly 600 mm needs further confirmation.

## Verify oscillation using representative racks

The stated purposes are improved heating uniformity and avoiding bending from rollers remaining stationary for long periods. These are design objectives. Include rack/roller contacts, travel limits, dwell and representative loads in trials, then assess temperature results. Uniformity is written ±3–5°C; final permissible deviation and conditions remain to be agreed.

Although the gas system uses the term “direct-flame burner”, the structure describes combustion inside W-shaped radiant tubes, exchanging heat with circulating furnace air. Burner naming does not establish direct flame contact with cylinders. Verify loading and circulation relevant to oscillation under the actual configuration.

## List site interfaces separately for both furnaces

The ageing proposal places utility-pipe handover 1.5 m outside the equipment; the solution proposal uses 3 m. Both assign equipment piping and connection at handover to Suneng. Do not apply one distance to both units or expand piping distances into all electrical/civil supply boundaries. Suneng also proposes foundation/utility-interface information and site installation/commissioning.
