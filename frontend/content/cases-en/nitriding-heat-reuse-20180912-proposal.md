Retort cooling and drying after washing can share a hot-air connection. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s nitriding package proposal for riveted/welded special electrical-switch parts, dated 12 September 2018, directs hot air from cooling vessels through ducting to a drying vessel for washed parts. Suitability depends on when heat is available, drying loads and a clear boundary from the retort's internal process atmosphere.

## Where does the hot air come from, and which stage uses it?

The cooling vessel has a forced-air fan below and a heat-recovery duct above; the drying vessel has a lower hot-air inlet. As the retort is force-cooled, heated air travels to the drying vessel, whose rack holds washed parts. This proposes linking heat released by cooling to heat needed for drying.

Put retort-cooling times and washed-part drying needs on one schedule. Where they overlap, assess available air temperature, flow and duration against the drying batch. Otherwise, consider waiting, temporary storage or supplementary drying conditions. The source provides no measured values here, so no energy-saving quantity or claim of never needing another heat source can be derived.

## Do not combine cooling air with ammonia-containing process exhaust

The same proposal separately includes a waste-gas combustion/ammonia-decomposition heating furnace for ammonia-containing nitriding exhaust, followed by combustion of hydrogen-containing gas after decomposition. This is a separately named treatment unit. Confirm drying-air origin from cooling-vessel and duct drawings. Do not connect retort process exhaust to workpiece drying or describe recovered hot air as verified compliant emissions.

Atmosphere changes have separate proposed requirements: nitrogen backfill after pre-evacuation, methanol dosing and ammonia during heating, then nitrogen displacement of the mixed gas during cooling/exhaust. The source also lists lid pressure gauges, safety valves, water-cooled seals and furnace temperature/pressure over-limit alarms with heating shutdown. Before implementation, obtain final gas and fault-response documents defining purge completion, exhaust destination and coordination during gas/power loss or treatment faults. This article describes interfaces, not ammonia or methanol operating recipes.

## Where do drying parts come from, and what does the buyer supply?

The package summary marks washing, rinsing and soap-washing tanks as buyer-supplied. Corresponding nitrogen/ammonia cylinders and pressure-reducing valves are excluded from the gas-supply item. Agree washing results, carried liquid and drying-rack configuration, then tank, dryer and duct positions. Structural descriptions of tanks elsewhere do not override buyer supply in the summary.

Heating-furnace working dimensions are φ2000 × 2400 mm, rated power 300 kW and rated temperature 650°C. These are not drying-vessel dimensions, heat input or temperature. Suneng proposes equipment integration, wiring and installation; the buyer connects water/electricity/gas and supplies furnace-to-cabinet power cables. List drying conditions, hot-air paths and energy scope separately, then assess performance after package trials.
