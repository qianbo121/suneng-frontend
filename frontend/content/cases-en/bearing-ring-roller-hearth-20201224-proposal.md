Matching a roller-hearth furnace to bearing rings requires checking individual weight, actual support positions and the full reciprocating travel envelope together. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s protective-atmosphere roller-hearth heating furnace proposal dated 24 December 2020 specifies three 800 kg rings, totalling 2400 kg. Its parameter table gives 500 kg per furnace roller at 300 mm spacing, with both continuous and oscillating operating modes. A similar total batch weight is a starting point; suitability of the roller arrangement also depends on support and space during loading, travel and reversal.

## Break the total load down by workpiece and roller

The RGB4-500-9 proposal is for heating bearing rings. Compare its dimensions, weights and roller conditions on a single loading drawing.

The source states that the roller bodies contact the rings directly and carry their weight. Thus, 2400 kg describes the three-piece loading combination, whereas 500 kg per roller describes an individual roller criterion. Neither substitutes for the other.

For initial assessment, overlay the workpiece contact regions on the 300 mm roller spacing and check support throughout entry, furnace travel and discharge. Dividing a workpiece's weight by the number of contacting rollers does not establish the actual force on each. If load concentrates at a particular position, revise the support arrangement or reassess roller-system capacity before adopting the layout. A total below the proposed batch weight alone does not establish suitability.

The two source documents provide no ring internal diameters, cross-sections or position-by-position load-distribution drawings, and do not clarify whether additional fixtures are included in the loading figure. Any new tray or support must be included in the weight, support and space assessment.

## Allow space for reciprocating movement, not just a static fit

The proposal specifies roller speeds of 0.5–2 m/min, with one motor per roller, grouped speed control and encoder feedback. The structural section groups every 5 rollers. These describe transport and oscillation control; speed alone does not define the necessary residence time.

The proposal also requires a forward/reverse oscillation distance greater than one roller circumference and cumulative oscillation drift of ≤50 mm over the entire oscillating period. Neither drift direction nor measurement reference is defined, so 50 mm is not a permissible clearance to doors or walls. A further roller-drive synchronisation criterion is ≤1%, without its calculation basis. Agree the definition and verification method before comparing synchronisation figures.

Show the workpiece envelope at both travel extremes, not only its stationary position. If it interferes with adjacent pieces, doors or walls, reassess spacing, stroke and required space before detailed design. Uniform-zone length is not automatically the available oscillation stroke.

For position control, the source proposes encoder signals and laser light-curtain detection for reversing, plus heat-resistant steel collision stops. These indicate intended position control and collision protection. Actual detection points, reversal positions and actions after stopping still need to be defined in the new controls specification; listed components do not establish completed site verification.

## After loading checks, compare heating and supply conditions

Similar geometry and load capacity do not establish an identical heating process. The 2020 proposal uses electric radiant tubes above and below, with 3 temperature-control zones, a proposed heating power of 500 kW subject to final design, and a maximum operating temperature of 950°C. These equipment conditions cannot specify the process temperature or holding time for another bearing-ring material.

The parameter table states “chamber temperature uniformity ≤±10°C”, without a test temperature, sensor layout, loading state or deviation reference. It is a proposed criterion for discussion, not a substitute for fully loaded workpiece temperatures or core-temperature records. Once loading is agreed, align the process curve and workpiece measurement requirements with equipment verification. Where core temperature matters, corresponding workpiece measurement evidence is needed.

The associated historical quotation dated 6 January 2021 proposes Suneng design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning, including secondary cables and trays between furnace and control cabinet. It excludes equipment foundations and cooling-water treatment; the buyer provides site water, electricity, gas and safe lifting. The technical proposal locates utility handover points 1.5 m outside the equipment, with the seller responsible for equipment-side piping and connection. Apply that division to the actual building; “installation included” does not mean all site work is included.

The proposal's equipment list includes a methanol cracking unit, but the associated quotation marks it optional. Confirm the protective-atmosphere source and gas-supply scope separately rather than assuming supply with the main furnace.

Inlet and outlet nitrogen-purge chambers are also proposed: close the outer door, purge with nitrogen, then open the inner door after purging is complete. Hoods above the doors connect to ducting for central treatment or discharge. Whether the gas source is optional or buyer-supplied, allocate and verify its main-furnace connection, purge-completion criteria, the final destination of collected emissions, and gas/heating responses to loss of gas or electricity among equipment, gas and site providers. The listed equipment is supported by the source, but operating conditions and responsibilities depend on the chosen gas supply. “Optional” does not remove these interfaces from the assessment.

## Common procurement questions

### Can 2400 kg and roller speed be converted directly into hourly throughput?

No. The proposal includes continuous and oscillating operation, so workpieces do not move continuously towards the outlet during every stage. Define heating and holding requirements, stage durations and handover arrangements before assessing throughput. The two available documents provide no complete workpiece process curve or operating timetable for this calculation.

### Does “non-oxidising heating” establish a verified quality result?

The proposal lists “non-oxidising heating of bearing rings” as its intended use and includes nitrogen-purge chambers. These are a design objective and configuration description. The available evidence is a technical proposal and historical quotation, without corresponding workpiece inspection, delivery or acceptance records. The intended use is therefore not presented as a verified result.

### What information is most useful for a similar requirement?

Provide workpiece drawings, individual weight, proposed quantity, supports and process curve. Suneng can initially check support positions against roller spacing, reciprocating space and heating conditions. Address any layout or loading mismatch before refining configuration and quotation.
