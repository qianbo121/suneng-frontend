Programme temperature control and loading method need separate confirmation. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s box-furnace proposal dated 21 November 2024 uses instruments for heating, holding and cooling, alongside an operator-controlled electric fork trolley for raising, lowering and moving the frame. Electric mechanisms and wireless door operation do not establish fully automatic handling. Define the relationship between operators, frame, forks and door first.

## What does each control function actually operate?

| Proposed configuration | Explicit action or function | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Programme temperature controller | Follows programmed heating, holding and cooling | Automatic loading, discharge or immersion of the complete system. |
| Door mechanism | Wireless remote operation plus cabinet buttons | Automatic frame-position recognition or autonomous transfer. |
| Operator-controlled electric fork trolley | Operator raises/lowers and advances/retracts the frame for loading/unloading | Unattended handling or a guaranteed discharge-to-water time. |

Wireless operation is proposed because an operator at the installed cabinet may have blind spots. It allows a different viewing position; it does not prove all blind spots are eliminated. Plan the operating area around door, trolley and pedestrian routes, confirming emergency stops, action permissions and visibility.

## Separate usable loading dimensions from frame dimensions

The proposal lists an effective chamber zone of 1000 × 1000 × 600 mm and usable frame loading dimensions of 700 × 700 × 300 mm, both length × width × height. The second describes usable loading space, not the frame's outside dimensions. Their difference is not an agreed one-sided safety clearance.

The proposed high-temperature frame uses 310S steel plate with fork-entry passages underneath and is intended to carry workpieces during loading, discharge and water immersion. Provide the external frame envelope, bottom passages, fork dimensions and actual loading drawing to check trolley compatibility. Establish workpiece weight, centre of gravity and supports; usable dimensions do not imply carrying capacity.

## What is missing if rapid immersion is required?

A frame designed to enter water with workpieces is an intended use, not a complete quench system. The cited documents provide neither transfer timing boundaries nor coordinated control details for the trolley. Add tank position, transfer route, loading, operator actions and timing start/end events, then allocate design and verification between equipment and site providers.

## Verify temperatures and mechanical actions separately

The source proposes working-zone uniformity verification at 800°C and 1050°C with a ±8°C requirement. Its guaranteed-values table defines control accuracy ±1°C as steady-state indication minus setpoint; a qualified third party is to measure uniformity. These are proposed requirements, not completed calibration or certification, and furnace criteria are not each workpiece's temperature spread.

Mechanical checks should use the actual load to assess trolley, frame and door. Door-raising heater isolation and lifting-overtravel protection require verification against actual control documents. Some structural text states 2 front/rear zones, whereas parameter and guaranteed-values tables state 1 zone. This article selects no final zoning; reconcile electrical drawings, measurement and acceptance definitions at procurement approval.

## Proposed Suneng work and site support

Suneng proposes transport, installation and commissioning, with foundation, layout and electrical-interface information. The buyer supplies power to the main incoming cabinet. State automation requirements before those documents are agreed: which actions remain manual, whether transfer equipment is added and how loading/movement will be verified.

Start with workpiece dimensions, weights and loading, proposed frame/trolley and furnace-front site information. Add cooling and transfer requirements for quenching. Suneng can distinguish programmed temperature control, operator actions and mechanical scope to form a reviewable configuration.
