Review waste-heat recovery together with heat-exchanger protection. Two Suneng gas-fired bogie-hearth furnace documents use flue gas to preheat combustion air, with temperature measurement at the exchanger's flue-gas outlet and a cold-air pipe at its inlet. Distinguish preheating performance, protective actions and furnace-pressure regulation. Combustion-air temperature is not a measured energy-saving result.

## Combustion air and dilution air follow different paths

Both sources describe flue gas passing through an air preheater and exchanging heat with combustion air, which then supplies the burners. A separate cold-air branch enters the exchanger's flue-gas inlet. If outlet flue-gas temperature is too high, the proposed valve opens to mix cold air into the flue gas and reduce its temperature. This protects the exchanger; it is not an air-cooling process for workpieces.

## Identify the actual protection measurement point

The sources call this function automatic hot-air temperature control, but describe the thermocouple at the flue-gas outlet and the cold-air pipe at the inlet. Mark flue-gas direction, combustion-air direction, measurement location and valve duty separately. The thermocouple is not a workpiece-temperature point, and the section heading does not establish that it directly measures combustion-air temperature.

Both documents consider excessive exchanger temperature caused by reduced combustion-air flow. This explains the protective purpose, but does not provide settings, reset conditions or a complete fault-response sequence ready for a new project. Final design documents must define these and link them to test records. This article is not a site commissioning procedure.

## Match furnace-pressure control to its own measurement point

Both exhaust-system descriptions include furnace-pressure regulation with pressure tapping and a damper. Since both pressure regulation and cold-air dilution affect the flue-gas passage, drawings should identify their separate measured variables, actuators and interaction. The presence of valves in both does not make them the same control loop.

Higher combustion-air preheat temperature cannot simply be converted into a fixed gas-saving percentage. Although the sources mention energy-saving percentages, they provide no accompanying measured consumption under identical workpieces, loading and process conditions for this comparison. This page retains only configuration and temperature information, without subtracting figures from two independent proposals to claim savings.

## Documents needed to clarify the interface

Obtain the final exchanger and piping layout, temperature- and pressure-point schedule, valve-action description and corresponding test records. Check site utilities and equipment supply against each proposal's own list. The documents have not been confirmed as one project or alternative bids; only related clauses are compared, without combining quantities or prices. The 4 m document has a 2023 preparation date and a 2026 final-page date. The 12 m document has a 2017 signature and a 2026 cover. Confirm the applicable revisions before use.
