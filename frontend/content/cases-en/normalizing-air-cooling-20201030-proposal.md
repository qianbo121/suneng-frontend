The cooling section between normalising and tempering must both remove heat and transfer parts. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s roller-supported mesh-belt line proposal dated 30 October 2020 places a forced-air cooler with elevator between the furnaces. Parts enter from the normalising drop opening and are lifted to the tempering inlet after cooling. Check stable transfer, process cooling and belt coordination together.

This page focuses on process-air cooling, equipment-cooling water and responsibilities at the two furnace interfaces. For loading on the incline and receiving at the discharge outlet, see [lift cooling and mesh-belt transfers](/en/case/normalizing-lift-cooling-transfer-to-tempering-proposal). The pages use a 2020 proposal and a 2026 sample respectively; the later date alone does not establish a superseding revision.

## Identify cooling from the structural description

The intermediate unit comprises a steel frame, mesh-belt elevator, forced-cooling fan, temperature measurement and controls. The fan sits above the elevator to cool parts. It differs from oil tanks in other proposals. Reuse of the word “tank” in a measurement clause does not justify adding oil, water or another medium.

Parts drop into the unit, rise on the elevator and drop onto the tempering loading belt. Assess actual shape, individual weight, stacking and contact to determine guidance, protection against jams/concentrated piles, and spreading on the elevator. A transport concept does not establish direct passage for every part.

## A 20-degree slope and 120 mm guards do not guarantee against sliding

The elevator has 120 mm-high side guards and a stated longitudinal slope of 20 degrees, described as preventing parts from sliding down during lifting. These are proposed geometry and purpose, not verified results. Actual sliding, overturning and overlapping require checks of contact shape and representative loads.

One parameter gives elevator speed 30–300 mm/min, while structural text states 50–300 mm/min. Both furnace belts list 50–300 mm/min. Reconcile minimum speed in final drive documents. Matching maximum speeds do not automatically match residence times, layer depths or discharge timing.

## Establish what the intermediate thermocouple measures

One thermocouple monitors the working region. This does not establish direct measurement of every part or its core. Define how exit workpiece temperature is verified for entry into tempering, and the region sensor's control role. This article adds no undocumented temperature-triggered release logic.

The fan description supplies no complete airflow figure, workpiece cooling curve or exit temperature. Request those with representative stacking and part conditions. A fan supports a proposed forced-air method, not an achieved microstructure, hardness or output.

## Separate workpiece air cooling from bearing water cooling

Water-cooled roller bearing housings are provided at the two furnaces; the buyer supplies a cooling pond or tower for equipment water. This serves components, separately from intermediate forced-air workpiece cooling. List bearing circuits and cooling-fan conditions individually, rather than call both a quench-cooling system.

The furnaces have rated temperatures of 950°C and 650°C respectively. Proposed output is approximately 1000 kg/h, dependent on actual parts, stacking and process. Agree both heating processes and intermediate cooling before no-load, loaded and hot-action checks. Suneng proposes positioning, installation and secondary wiring; the buyer provides energy, primary wiring and acceptance workpieces. These are proposed responsibilities, not operating or acceptance records.
