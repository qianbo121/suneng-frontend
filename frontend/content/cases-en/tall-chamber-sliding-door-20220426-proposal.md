A 7.5 m-high chamber does not necessarily accept a workpiece of the same height. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s bogie-hearth heat-treatment proposal dated 26 April 2022 specifies a 4500 × 4000 × 7500 mm chamber, an electric sliding door and 12-zone control. Check loading height against the opening and supports, then map heating zones and sensors to the actual load.

## Check the opening and supports before using chamber height for selection

| Parameter | Source value | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chamber length × width × height | 4500 × 4000 × 7500 mm | Chamber dimensions, not a full-load envelope. |
| Rated power | 2000 kW | Heating configuration, not actual consumption. |
| Rated load | 25 t | Load composition and force distribution require current confirmation. |

The bogie has refractory-brick lining and 30 mm heat-resistant steel hearth plates with reinforcing ribs and hooked joints. Define actual supports, spacers and centre of gravity, then compare the full envelope with opening and chamber. A 25 t total does not replace local-load checks or establish net capacity for every arrangement.

Chamber height is also not building clear height or lifting clearance. Upright parts or tall racks need a complete loading drawing and lifting route to assess placement and furnace access. Internal dimensions alone cannot answer those external-space questions.

## A sliding door needs lateral space and layout-specific actions

The door moves sideways using a motor/gearbox and travelling frame. Heating follows the closed-position signal, is disconnected during sliding, and bogie travel is permitted once the door reaches its opening position. This remains a sliding-door design, not a lifting door; raised-limit heights from other proposals do not apply.

Show lateral door travel and bogie paths on the general arrangement, checking nearby equipment, pedestrian routes and maintenance. Closed position and an open position clearing the bogie are different states. The final action schedule must assign each signal/interlock rather than use one generic “door in position”.

## Twelve zones do not prove vertical uniformity in a 7.5 m chamber

Elements are around the chamber and beneath the hearth. The text describes 12 zones from front to rear, 12 side thermocouple sockets, 12 thermocouples and 12-zone recording. It gives counts and some configuration, but no three-dimensional zone boundaries. Do not reinterpret these as vertical layers or assume independent load sensors at every level.

Rated temperature 950°C and uniformity ≤±10°C are proposed parameters. Assess high and low loads by mapping heating zones, furnace sensors and representative loading, then testing at agreed temperatures, effective regions and conditions. A furnace record represents its sensor location, not equal core temperatures throughout the load.

## What should an enquiry prioritise?

Provide the overall workpiece/support envelope, load and centre of gravity, building plan/clear height and lifting conditions, followed by process curves and temperature-inspection requirements. These establish whether loading fits, the door clears the path and heating/measurement cover the load.

Suneng proposes site installation and commissioning; the buyer prepares conditions from foundation information and provides energy and an overhead crane. The source distinguishes empty commissioning from loaded heat-up commissioning. Correct empty movement cannot replace loaded travel, heating and temperature verification.
