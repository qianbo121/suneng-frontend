A July 2026 group of Suneng documents proposes a bottom-opening solution-treatment furnace and a top-loading ageing furnace, both fitted to sites after old-equipment removal. Their main checks differ: the solution furnace needs a continuous downward path through the opening, basket and water tank; the ageing furnace needs confirmation of chamber shape, crane-entry space and pit supports.

## Effective chamber, clear opening and basket envelope are three dimensions

The solution-furnace draft lists effective chamber dimensions of 2300 × 2300 × 1700 mm, a bottom clear discharge opening of approximately 2200 × 2200 mm and a basket no larger than 2100 × 2100 × 1500 mm. These describe heating space, descent passage and the proposed basket respectively. Checking basket fit inside the chamber alone is insufficient.

Centring a 2100 mm basket in a 2200 mm opening leaves a nominal 50 mm per side. This is only subtraction of proposed dimensions, not verified operating clearance. Lifting lugs, projecting parts, guides, hot deformation and installation tolerances require complete loaded and movement drawings. The draft also leaves the direction of 1700 mm for confirmation; it cannot be treated as a frozen water depth or descent distance.

## Separate pre-positioning from bottom-door transfer timing

The draft first positions and locks the pit-mounted water-tank trolley, then opens the bottom door and lowers the basket. Parameter and acceptance tables define ≤15 s from bottom door fully open to workpieces fully immersed, excluding trolley pre-positioning. Waiting before door movement, opening time and descent into water are distinct stages. This figure is not the complete quench delay from departure from the holding condition.

The process text also uses the general phrase entire transfer ≤15 s. The current project should map every movement's start and end, agree which stages the process limit covers and confirm supply responsibility for lifting, rapid lowering and tank trolley. These mechanisms remain marked for confirmation in the supply table. This article retains timing boundaries without claiming achieved 15-second immersion.

## Establish whether the ageing pit serves a square or round furnace

The ageing-furnace parameter table gives 2500 × 2500 × 2000 mm, while the introduction describes a circular structure. Suneng's draft provisionally uses a square arrangement and requests existing pit clear dimensions, depth, elevations and capacity for clarification. Until then, three dimensions do not establish fit of the shell, insulation, ducts and basket.

The basket is lowered through the top and supported by hearth beams and load-spreading plates; the solution basket instead descends through the bottom into water. Identical 1500 kg workpiece ratings do not establish a shared basket. Check lifting points, support contacts, loaded centre of gravity and each mechanism's total load including basket, then confirm crane headroom.

## Define the site conditions after old equipment is removed

Both drafts provisionally allocate old-equipment removal and pit cleaning to the buyer. The ageing draft separately assigns pit waterproofing and ventilation to buyer civil works. The solution furnace proposes water cooling and steam collection, which must match plant circulating water, discharge interfaces and pit layout. General turnkey wording must agree with these items; it does not establish inclusion of old foundations, power and every external pipe.

Tank volume, furnace power and assumed basket weights differ within the source. This page selects no set as final. Provide site sections, full loading drawings, actual material grades and process curves so that opening, pit, tank and lift can be checked on the same drawings before thermal and supply versions are fixed. Aluminium/magnesium in equipment names and rated temperatures do not replace grade-specific treatment requirements.
