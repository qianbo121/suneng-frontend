A group of Suneng cylinder-equipment discussion drafts leaves basket compatibility for 3500 mm aluminium liners unresolved. The curing section separately distinguishes cylinder-body length from total length including a centre rod. Long-cylinder equipment must be checked against the actual workpiece state at each process stage; a liner, its basket and a wound cylinder on a mandrel are not the same envelope.

## A 3500 mm product entry does not establish old-basket compatibility

The solution-treatment and ageing draft lists aluminium liners up to Ø570 × 3500 mm and expressly says the earlier 3400 mm basket data cannot be assumed suitable. Adapting basket No. 1 to the longer liner is a revision objective, while dimensions, lifting points, weight and full-route compatibility still need confirmation. This is outstanding design assessment, not verified compatibility.

More than internal furnace height matters. The basket has upper lifting-hook points, a lower positive pull-down connection, must pass the opening and must be fully immersed. Draw the liner, frame, supports and connections together to check effective zone, lifting travel and actual liquid level. Subtracting a 3500 mm liner from 3750 mm effective-zone height does not yield freely usable clearance.

These are records for different objects at different stages. They do not establish that a 3500 mm liner becomes a wound body complying with a 3400 mm limit. The drafts lack a one-to-one drawing of maximum sizes from liner to wound product. Establish the actual product family before combining table maxima into one product.

## The basket turns 90 degrees between vertical lifting and roller transport

After solution treatment, a lifting platform hands the basket to a travelling transfer carriage. Its upper level telescopes to receive the load, its middle mechanism turns through 90°, and its lower level travels to the ageing furnace. A longer basket requires checks of swept space, centre of gravity and receiving-roller elevation as well as straight passages. Fit on a stationary carriage is insufficient.

The draft rates transfer platforms and carriage for 3000 kg including basket and parts, whereas furnace rated loading is ≤1500 kg excluding the basket. Add actual basket and fixture weights when comparing these different objects. The 3000 kg rating does not raise furnace workpiece capacity to 3 tonnes.

## Wound cylinders use mandrel interfaces and supports

The curing draft loads cylinders with centre rods. One support is fixed and the other screw-adjusted; the mandrel must match rotation sprockets and inflation connections. The object is now a wound cylinder on a mandrel, rather than a basket-loaded liner. Earlier basket dimensions and transfer methods cannot simply be reused.

Curing furnace No. 1 lists a chamber of 3300 × 4500 × 3250 mm, but the revision explicitly says this is not the effective heating zone. The loading drawing must show the direction of the 4030 mm assembly, the body's effective heating area and connector space. Since 4030 is less than 4500, compatibility of the complete cylinder and interfaces still does not follow.

## Maximum diameter, quantity and rated weight cannot all be combined

The solution section lists 16 liners per basket for the assessment size Ø470 × 1850 mm and 4 for the maximum Ø570 × 3500 mm. The curing section lists 9 cylinders at Ø650 mm and 5 at Ø1050 mm. These are different loading combinations; arbitrary large products cannot all be assigned 16 or 9 positions. Both revisions require separate interpretation of maximum size, quantity and load.

The curing equipment proposes rotating supports and pressure-holding mechanisms; the loading manipulator is buyer-supplied. Both parties must confirm door-position and loading-permission signals. The liner-section transfer carriage does not automatically include the curing loader, and 9 cylinder positions do not establish 9 independent pressure-control circuits. Interfaces and control grouping require agreed drawings.

For similar projects, prepare three corresponding drawings: basket-loaded liners, basket lifting/turning routes and wound cylinders with mandrels. Mark weight, quantity and target furnace for each size. Resolve geometry and interfaces before checking temperature, transfer and pressure-holding performance under confirmed loading. Discussion-draft targets and pre-signature questions do not replace actual trials.
