Suneng's 2020 proposal for a 13-metre steel-pipe bogie-hearth furnace specifies high-temperature finned heat-exchange tubes in the roof to cool workpieces while the chamber remains sealed under a protective atmosphere. It lists a cooling rate of 50–100°C/h. Compare cooling rate and atmosphere conditions as one requirement; the document does not establish a fully defined cooling system.

## Define the cooling duty being compared

The source places workpiece cooling within a “sealed protective-atmosphere furnace chamber”, rather than specifying only a temperature drop per hour. For a new project, identify the workpieces, process stage and atmosphere to be maintained before assessing the cooling rate. Equal rate figures under different atmospheres do not establish equivalent supply capability.

## A finned-tube name is not a complete system description

The source identifies the tubes' location and purpose but does not develop the media inside and outside them, protective-gas supply/exhaust or cooling-control connections in the later structural sections. Define these interfaces in drawings and allocate pipes, valves, measurements and auxiliary equipment. “Heat exchange” does not justify assuming water cooling, air cooling or a particular protective gas. General shell-sealing descriptions are not atmosphere-performance acceptance results.

The control section provides viewing, storage and output of furnace heating, soaking and cooling graphs. It proposes furnace-temperature recording, but does not prove that every location or the core of a 13-metre pipe cools at 50–100°C/h. Specify the applicable temperature interval, loading state and measured object. Where the process concerns workpiece temperature, agree its measurement method separately.

## Accept against the same workpieces and atmosphere

The proposal schedules loaded heat-up commissioning after empty-furnace commissioning and acceptance against the contract and technical agreement. For this cooling function, the final revision must explain how actual loading, atmosphere conditions and cooling records correspond. An empty-furnace curve or the seven heating-side control zones cannot replace inspection of workpiece cooling under sealed protective-atmosphere conditions.

For discussions, provide pipe geometry and a loading drawing, the required cooling interval and atmosphere conditions, then request finned-tube and atmosphere-system interface drawings. The available documents support these checks but do not establish achieved cooling rate, protective effect or energy savings.
