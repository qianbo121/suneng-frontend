This 420 kW electric fork-handling furnace solution-treats stainless fittings and flanges. After soaking, the car transfers a 7-tonne loaded basket to the tank. The specified time from first outward movement to complete immersion is at most 50 seconds.

## Project features

**The basket carries the entire batch** — Load the fittings or flanges into a heat-resistant steel basket and heat them together. All protruding elbows and stacked flanges must remain within the working envelope.

**The car transfers the loaded basket** — The forks support the basket underneath. After soaking, withdraw it, align it with the tank and lower it. Time from the first outward movement until the last part is fully immersed.

## Equipment arrangement and process sequence

**Load the basket** — The basket and its contents weigh 7 tonnes in total.

**Charge the basket** — The basket enters the working zone, which has two heating zones.

**Withdraw the basket** — Support the basket underneath. Start timing when the fittings begin to move out.

**Immerse the batch** — Stop the clock when the last workpiece is fully under water.

**Suneng** — Supplies the furnace, fork car, drives and controls and carries out installation and commissioning.

## Scope of supply

Suneng supplies the resistance-heated solution furnace, door lift, fork charging car and controls. The buyer’s basket carries the same load across the hearth supports, forks and water tank.

## Acceptance procedure

**Inspection at the manufacturer’s works** — After manufacture, both parties check fabrication, assembly, safety and associated equipment under the agreement.

**Heating under load on site** — Once the empty-furnace test is satisfactory, proceed to heat under load. Acceptance requires meeting the main contract specifications.

## Common questions

**Can it hold 7 net tonnes of pipe fittings?** The 7 tonnes includes the basket. Deduct its empty weight to obtain the net weight of fittings.

**How high can the basket be loaded?** The working height is 1.2 m, with a length and width of 1.8 m each. Include stacked flanges and protruding elbows when measuring the loaded basket.

**Does the same party supply the furnace, basket and tank?** Suneng supplies the solution furnace and fork charging car. The buyer supplies the heat-resistant basket and water tank.
