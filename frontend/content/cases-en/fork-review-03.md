One car loads and unloads Suneng’s four independently controlled annealing chambers. There is one loading and one unloading station. The maximum charge per chamber is 5.5 tonnes including coils and rack.

## Project features

**Four independently controlled chambers** — The rack enters the designated chamber. Each chamber has its own heating zone. While one is being loaded or unloaded, the others can continue heating or soaking.

**One car, separate stations** — Incoming coils wait at the loading station. Annealed coils go to the unloading station. The contract lists one car for the four chambers and both stations.

## Equipment arrangement and process sequence

**Prepare coils at the loading station** — The buyer provides the rack. The loaded rack waits at the loading station.

**Transfer the rack** — The shared fork car takes the rack to the designated chamber.

**Anneal in separate chambers** — Each of the four chambers has one zone for heating and soaking.

**Receive the rack at the unloading station** — The fork car retrieves the rack and moves it to the separate unloading station.

## Supply responsibilities

**Suneng** — Designs, manufactures, installs and commissions the system. The contract includes a nine-point temperature instrument for checking differences within the working zone.

**Buyer** — Supplies the racks that enter with the coils and loads them under the supplier’s guidance during commissioning.

## Scope of supply

Each chamber has its own hot-air circulation system and temperature control. One car travels between the loading station, chambers and unloading station.

## Acceptance procedure

**Empty and loaded commissioning** — The contract separates the empty functional check from the loaded test with workpieces and racks.

**Trial production period** — The contract specifies 30 consecutive days of normal operation. Both parties then conduct acceptance under its technical and acceptance terms.

## Common questions

**Does 200 kW per chamber mean 200 kW for the whole system?** 200 kW is the heating power of one chamber. There are four chambers, each with one zone.

**Does the 5.5-tonne batch include the rack?** Yes. Deduct the actual rack weight from the combined coil-and-rack weight to obtain the net copper charge.

**Is there a car for each chamber?** One car serves all four. It takes a rack from the loading station to the designated chamber and moves the annealed batch to the unloading station.
