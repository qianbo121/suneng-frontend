Zoned gas heating for heavy steel structures must be coordinated with loading, support passages and workpiece-temperature sensors. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s proposal dated 12 March 2025 specifies sixteen burner sets, sixteen control zones and sixteen furnace-temperature monitoring points, plus sixteen movable workpiece-temperature points. Furnace sensors observe heating regions; load sensors represent selected workpiece locations. They require separate layouts, and the furnace reaching setpoint does not establish that the workpiece interior has reached temperature.

## Show loading regions and flame passages together

The proposal allows bogie loading regions to be selected for workpiece size. Precast castable blocks support the load; dense refractory material forms the flame-passage areas. For similar structures, show centre of gravity, support-block contacts and flame passages on the loading drawing. Check alignment with the load-bearing structure and obstruction of major heat-transfer paths.

This identifies zones and sensors needing attention, but does not establish that half a bogie load consumes half the gas. The source contains neither gas-consumption comparisons for such loading changes nor evidence of complete separation between furnace regions.

## Why measure load temperature after the furnace reaches setpoint?

The sixteen fixed furnace thermocouples, controllers and burners form proposed regional control loops. This describes control function, not proven uniformity or savings. The sixteen additional movable thermocouples can be secured to workpieces for display, recording and alarms. Furnace curves represent chamber regions; load curves represent actual contact locations. They are not interchangeable.

The same clause says load temperature “can provide interlocking control with the corresponding temperature control”, but does not define interlock conditions, its role in regulation or the start of holding time. This article retains the proposed function without adding automatic-control or holding-start logic.

For structures with varying thickness or shielding, choose representative locations using material, main cross-sections, supports and heating requirements. Surface-attached sensors may not directly represent internal temperature. Where the process requires internal-temperature verification, agree a suitable measurement or verification method before deciding the holding criterion. Neither external dimensions nor furnace setpoint can calculate core holding time.

The proposal states “measurement at the end of holding, temperature difference between points in the effective zone ≤±10°C”, and control-point accuracy of ±1°C. The former is limited to the effective zone at the end of holding; the latter concerns control points. Reference bases, loaded sensor layouts and test temperatures are incomplete. Do not extend the criterion to every workpiece throughout the cycle or convert the notation into another temperature-spread definition.

## Combustion and movement interfaces for zoned heating

Proposed protections include pre-ignition purging, flame-ionisation detection, gas shut-off and alarm after flame loss. The gas main lists filtration, pressure regulation, pressure switches and a fast shut-off valve, with gas isolation on low gas pressure or fan failure. Flue gas exits through a rear flue, heat exchanger and chimney, with exhaust regulation and furnace-pressure control. Agree gas, combustion-air and exhaust conditions together with control logic.

For a new project, use a system drawing with explicit handover points to allocate gas supply, pressure reduction, combustion air, gas venting and flue discharge. Do not combine pressure values from different source locations into a universal start/stop threshold. Purge permission, fault isolation, alarms and recovery require approved control documents and verification records. This article does not provide an ignition operating sequence for site use.

Raised-door safety pins and normal door/bogie/heating interlocks are proposed. For heavy loads, also check position retention on loss of electricity or air, maintenance isolation and recovery against supports and movement mechanisms. Different loading statements in the source cannot yet support a single public load-capacity figure. Align actual loads with the final mechanical design, fixtures and hot-travel conditions first.

## Who designs and supplies buyer-supplied blocks and fixtures?

Item (5) under buyer supply states: “support blocks and fixtures (buyer provides product geometry; seller is responsible for design)”. The design object is the blocks and fixtures in that item. The buyer supplies geometry, the seller designs those items, and their physical supply remains in buyer scope. Furnace design and manufacture are separately assigned in seller-supply clauses; the parenthesis must not be expanded to mean the whole installation.

For a new project, use workpiece geometry and support positions as design inputs, and allocate drawings, manufacture and acceptance separately. The original clause distinguishes physical supply from design responsibility; these should not be collapsed into a general buyer obligation.

Suneng proposes design, manufacture, installation, cold/hot commissioning, inspection and testing of the furnace, bogie, combustion and electrical equipment, plus foundation drawings and rail-installation technical requirements. The buyer provides foundations, site gas and incoming power, among other conditions. Providing rail parameters, installing/adjusting rails and physically supplying rails are separate responsibilities. The historical quotation's rail summary note and itemised listing are unresolved, so this page does not give a complete project total.

## Planning temperature verification after comparison

The proposal schedules furnace-uniformity and energy-consumption tests and trial-production batches after cold and hot commissioning. The supplier provides fixtures and instruments for uniformity testing. These are proposed tasks, not completed results. Agree loading, test temperature, representative furnace and load points, judgement methods and record formats before comparing performance under matching conditions.

### Can an outline drawing determine zoning and holding time?

It starts space and support checks, but material, main cross-sections, welded construction, batch arrangement, fixture weight, process curve and temperature-verification requirements are also needed. If only some measurement locations are unresolved, first allocate the known furnace and load sensor roles. Conclusions about internal temperature must wait for the corresponding verification method.
