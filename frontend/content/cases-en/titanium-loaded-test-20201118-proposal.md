A 2020 Suneng roller-hearth furnace technical draft specifies furnace-temperature uniformity alongside a loaded test using seven thick titanium-alloy plates and sixteen test thermocouples, with data collected four hours after loading. Assess the measurement objects and limits separately. A material-temperature criterion at one cross-section is not whole-furnace uniformity.

## Three temperature indicators concern different measurements

The draft states control accuracy of ±1°C, furnace-temperature uniformity of ±10°C and, in the loaded-test section, permissible temperature deviation at each cross-section of ≤30 degrees. These concern control points, furnace temperature distribution and specific loaded locations. Comparing magnitudes alone is insufficient, and none replaces the others.

The source writes ≤30 degrees, not ±30°C, and does not define whether deviation is the maximum-to-minimum spread or difference from a reference. This page retains that basis pending an agreed calculation method. It does not recast the value as a verified workpiece-core difference or whole-furnace temperature spread.

## Retain material, quantity and collection time together

| Condition | Draft record | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loaded material | TC4 or TA15, 7 plates | Confirm actual grade and common condition of test material |
| Test thermocouples | 16 | Trial measuring-point count, not routine control-sensor count |
| Data collection | 4 hours after loading | Retain the loading start event; do not change it to 4 hours holding after temperature is reached |

Test-plate dimensions are 180–220 × 1000–1300 × 1200–1500 mm, expressly thickness × width × length. The actual chosen dimensions, placement, supports and gaps for seven plates determine the conditions represented by the test and should accompany its records. Minimum thickness cannot be used for weight calculations while maximum thickness is used to claim applicability.

Acceptance clauses require local temperature measurement after adding material during the first uniformity test. This gives explicit loading conditions but does not establish universal conclusions for all steel grades, plate thicknesses or stacking patterns. Define representative material first, then decide whether other sizes require further verification.

## Sixteen thermocouples do not establish their locations

The source divides the length into four equal portions and refers to a cross-sectional layout sketch. The text alone does not identify every thermocouple number and contact location. Formal tests need the corresponding placement drawing, identifying sensor, plate, cross-section and fastening method individually. Surface attachment, placement in a hole and core measurement have different meanings. Sixteen sensors do not imply sixteen core measurements.

Permanent controls separately use duplex thermocouples in each zone, with one circuit for temperature control and the other for monitoring and independent alarms, plus calibration openings near control thermocouples. Register this configuration separately from loaded-test sensors. The source does not give those sixteen test sensors automatic production-control or holding-time-start functions.

## Record roller operating state during measurement

The draft provides roller inching, rapid travel and forward/reverse oscillation, with light curtains near inlet and outlet to detect load position. To compare loaded-test results, identify roller state before and during collection and whether specimens move or are repositioned. Otherwise data from different conditions cannot reliably represent the same measuring locations.

These controls and detectors are proposed arrangements, not completed interlock tests. Controller-series descriptions also differ and require reconciliation for the final equipment. This article uses the explicit test conditions without redefining an entire control system.

## Information to prepare

For similar discussions with Suneng, provide actual grades and thickness ranges, a furnace loading drawing, target processes and intended inspection locations. Separately list furnace-uniformity testing, loaded-temperature measurement and instrument calibration, each with points, timing and assessment method. The draft requires both parties to accept against the technical agreement and confirm records. This page cannot replace the final test report or technical sign-off.
