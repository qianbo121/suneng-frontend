Buyer-supplied baskets and water tanks affect whether rapid transfer is feasible. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s March 2024 high-temperature box-furnace proposal uses internal blocks to support workpieces and a rail-mounted fork-handling machine for loading and extraction. Baskets and the tank are buyer-supplied, while furnace loading and handling-machine capacity have different definitions. Check all interfaces on one loading/transfer drawing.

## List furnace load and moving load separately

The table gives 8–10 t/batch excluding fixtures and support blocks. The handling-machine section separately gives a maximum single load of 8–10 t, uniformly distributed. The former cannot replace the latter: baskets and supports travelling with workpieces must enter the moving-load calculation, including distribution and centre of gravity. The source provides no complete breakdown of net and auxiliary loads, so no spare capacity can be invented.

## What space do baskets and furnace blocks occupy?

Working dimensions are given as length × width × height 1.2 × 6 × 0.6 m, and chamber dimensions as 1.7 × 7.1 × 1.35 m, with an exclusion for fixture support blocks. This article preserves that directional order. Workpieces rest on internal blocks; the fork machine loads, extracts and lowers them into water. Show blocks, basket envelope and fork-entry/withdrawal spaces together. A workpiece smaller than the chamber does not establish successful extraction or door closure.

The buyer-supplied basket section specifies heat-resistant steel and service conditions, but does not establish compatibility for any basket of that material. Provide basket weight, projecting lugs/reinforcements, loaded contact positions and hot dimensions. These affect moving load, opening clearance and effective fork contact. Buyer supply does not remove joint approval of the interface drawing.

## Approximately 45 s cannot be compared without matching timing events

The proposal says “workpiece discharge-to-water time approximately 45 s” and includes machine travel and hydraulic lifting. It does not specify door opening as the start or complete immersion as the finish. Do not rewrite it as “door opening to full immersion ≤45 s” or directly compare with differently timed equipment. Define timing along actual exit, travel and lowering actions.

The buyer-supplied tank is required to have automatic circulation and replenishment. Also align tank position and water level with the lowering path, confirming internal space, medium conditions and circulation/cooling. Automatic replenishment alone establishes neither temperature control, venting nor process cooling capacity. The listed maximum operating temperature of 1200°C describes the furnace, not actual workpiece immersion temperature or a material process.

## Verify interlocks and acceptance with buyer-supplied equipment included

The proposed door has raised-position pneumatic safety pins. Heating is inhibited if the door is open, not fully lowered or not locked, or if forks are not fully withdrawn. A furnace-front station has an emergency stop. These are proposed measures. Use the final action schedule to relate doors, forks, hydraulics and tank position; interlock names alone do not establish verified hot transfer.

Suneng proposes site installation/commissioning; the buyer prepares foundations, energy and lifting support. Provide a representative loading drawing, basket/block list, existing tank drawing and desired timing events, then verify empty and loaded conditions separately. This identifies whether an issue belongs to equipment, buyer-supplied parts or their interface.
