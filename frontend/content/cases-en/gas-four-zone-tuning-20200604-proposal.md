Suneng's 2020 gas-fired bogie-hearth furnace proposal permits individual burner adjustment, mainly for temperature-uniformity commissioning. Control points are on both sidewalls opposite the burners. Match control points, commissioning measurements and actual loading. Independent adjustment does not establish that billet temperature differences during soaking already meet the requirement.

## Three arrangements answer different questions

| Proposed arrangement | Purpose | Relationship to establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individually adjustable burners | Mainly furnace-uniformity commissioning | Which burner affects which test positions, and the supporting adjustment records |
| 2 control thermocouples on each sidewall | Opposite the burners, forming 4 zones | Positions of control points and heating zones; not automatically workpiece points |
| 2 trial-production batches after performance requirements are met | Check process curves, utility consumption and billet temperature differences during soaking | Actual charge size, quantity, measurement points and acceptance basis |

## Mark wall-control points separately from test points

The temperature section forms loops from wall thermocouples, intelligent controllers and automatic burners, with paperless recording of chamber temperature. A wall curve records that control location. Four matching curves do not prove equal billet temperatures throughout the furnace. Show the four control locations, uniformity-survey points and billet-temperature points separately in the loaded arrangement, then check whether placement and shielding affect representativeness.

Individual burner adjustment is a proposed commissioning capability, not a valve-adjustment instruction provided by this article. The text gives no final adjustment amount, sequence or measured loaded improvement, and does not say billet measurements participate in automatic control. Control documents and commissioning records must establish those details; they cannot be inferred from four-zone control.

## Define loaded acceptance with the actual charge

The source lists process-curve execution, industrial-utility consumption and soaking-period billet temperature difference as acceptance subjects. Both parties are to agree the trial plan and the quantity, quality and specifications of the buyer-supplied charge. The requirement should identify the actual charge, measurement positions, soaking-period criteria and approved standard, rather than merely “uniformity passed”.

The proposal also schedules no-load trials, loaded trials and uniformity testing. Once performance requirements are met, two trial-production batches and a signed memorandum follow. Suneng was to supply furnace, combustion and electrical controls; the buyer provides fixtures and trial conditions. These arrangements support acceptance preparation, but the current proposal includes no memorandum of completed trials.
