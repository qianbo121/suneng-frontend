Suneng's 2012 relocation proposal for a rapid stainless-steel pipe-fitting solution-treatment furnace renews the lining and natural-gas heating system while reusing the rapid loading/unloading fork truck, with new controls and limits. Review the existing forks and trays together with renewed hearth supports. Keeping the truck does not establish unchanged furnace-entry and water-immersion routes after relocation.

Workpieces sit on a movable tray, transferred together into and out of the original furnace and down into water. New hearth piers must support baskets/trays while leaving passages for forks and flames/hot gases. Lining renewal therefore involves more than insulation. Check support positions, tray underside and fork geometry, then relate them to tracks, tank and new floor elevations. This is a review sequence derived from the configuration, not completed site measurement.

## Reconcile “working dimensions retained” with the new table

The cover gives 2500×1500×1200 mm, and the lining section says original working dimensions are retained. Yet the post-retrofit table gives 2300×1500×1000 mm. The source does not explain whether these concern different locations or alternatives. Do not claim unchanged clear space or assess tray reuse against the larger figures. Final sections must reconcile door clear opening, pier-top level, fork passages and usable space.

The post-retrofit table gives 1 t/batch excluding fixture support blocks. The reused truck carries workpieces and tray together, but this document provides no final truck-capacity or stroke-validation record for direct adoption. Check workpiece, tray and block weights and support positions against existing mechanism data. One tonne is not an all-in load guarantee for the complete moving system.

## Define fuel conversion and air-path reuse

The background includes unstable original liquefied-gas supply. The proposal adopts piped natural gas at the new site, replacing fuel pipes, valves and chimney ducts while modifying/reusing some combustion-air ducts and the preheater. This is neither a fuel-name change alone nor all-new air/exhaust equipment. Confirm reusable items from their condition and new operating requirements, then allocate renewal and supplementary work in a responsibility list.

Three burners and three control zones are proposed, with thermocouples on the opposite sidewall corresponding to burners and protection for gas-pressure and fan faults. Added remote control and limits change control/operation interfaces; they do not prove improved truck motion or temperature distribution. Final controls must define and test permissions, stopping and positioning between new and existing mechanisms.

## Confirm reuse conditions before testing retrofit objectives

Suneng's scope covers local body modifications and lining/combustion-control renewal. The buyer handles workshop foundations, tracks and utility construction. Acceptance is planned for process curves, utility consumption and soaking-period billet temperature differences, with post-trial sign-off. This is a reference for retrofit scope and review, not evidence that relocation occurred. Historical energy-saving percentages are not actual savings here.

For an enquiry, prepare existing furnace/truck drawings, tray and loading drawings, new-site track/tank locations and gas conditions. Resolve whether the new lining and existing mechanism fit together first, then confirm combustion, controls and final trials. That provides a stronger basis than selecting a new-furnace quotation from the old equipment name alone.
