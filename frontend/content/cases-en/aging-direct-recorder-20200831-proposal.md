Two instruments may both display temperature while obtaining their signals differently. Suneng's 2020 technical agreement text for a Type III aluminium-alloy gas-cylinder ageing furnace requires thermocouples to connect directly to the paperless recorder, without feedback transmission through the temperature controller. Check the signal source before reviewing channels, measurement points and acceptance conditions.

## Identify the signal behind each recorded curve

The equipment is intended for ageing Type III aluminium-alloy gas cylinders, with a usual temperature of 160–180°C and a maximum working temperature of 300°C. The source explicitly requires direct thermocouple-to-recorder transmission, without an intervening temperature-controller feedback signal, to avoid deviations in the feedback value. This specifies a signal path; recorder brand or curve-display capability is not a substitute.

| Check | Source requirement | Supporting document or condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recording signal | Thermocouple directly connected to paperless recorder, without controller feedback transmission | Actual wiring from measurement point to recorder input |
| Recording channels | 4-channel paperless recorder | Measurement name, wiring and use assigned to every channel |
| Uniformity test | 300°C, 9 points, ≤±3°C; no temperature overshoot during measurement and no controller bias setting | Review these test conditions alongside calibration and operating records |

Direct connection still requires verifiable wiring and channel assignments. The designer should identify each input's thermocouple, wiring and compensation arrangement, and relationship to the control circuit. Do not assume that wires from one thermocouple should simply be connected in parallel to two instruments. The source provides no final wiring drawing here, and this page does not invent one.

## Three control zones and four recording channels do not establish a three-plus-one arrangement

The specifications list three control zones and a four-channel recorder. The control description calls for real-time curves from furnace control-temperature measurement points. This warrants checking the channel arrangement, but does not establish that the extra channel is allocated to load temperature, an overtemperature alarm or standby use. Before acceptance, obtain a channel schedule naming each measurement point, signal source and purpose. Hardware capacity is not an installed measurement function.

The source also proposes automatic curve saving and backup export. Exported files still need to match the batch, channel names and wiring. Exporting a curve does not prove that it records a specific workpiece's temperature, and furnace-temperature records are not inspection results for cylinder material properties.

## Wiring and nine-point uniformity are separate checks

The agreement specifies nine-point uniformity of ≤±3°C at 300°C, without temperature overshoot during measurement or controller bias settings. Final acceptance separately requires furnace uniformity and instrument and thermocouple accuracy to pass verification by the buyer's metrology department under relevant standards. Wiring checks, instrument/thermocouple verification and uniformity testing each need their own records; none replaces the others.

The usual 160–180°C operating range and the nine-point test at 300°C refer to different conditions. Both are retained in their original scope. If the current product needs validation at its actual ageing temperature and a specific load, agree that separately. The 300°C proposal criterion is not an accepted result for every working temperature and load.

## Complete the handover package

The text assigns installation, commissioning, trial operation and acceptance assistance to Suneng, together with electrical drawings, operating instructions and relevant test documents. The buyer provides workshop information, civil foundation design and construction, primary incoming power cabling and other site conditions. For the recording interface, prioritise the final wiring drawing, channel schedule, verification documents and corresponding test records before confirming programmes and product requirements.

The opening also mentions “two carbon-fibre fully wrapped cylinder curing furnaces”, which needs reconciliation with the later ageing-furnace application and quantity of one set. This page cites only the proposed requirements in the ageing-furnace section; it does not combine that opening into a claim for two contracted machines.
