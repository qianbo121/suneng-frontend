A Suneng mesh-belt quenching-and-tempering technical agreement for a Vietnamese customer separately defines Chinese-port delivery, buyer site provisions and final acceptance. After port arrival, unloading, placement, utility connections and site commissioning still remain. Procurement scheduling must connect these activities with document handover.

## Define the proposed treatment stages

The draft concerns protective-atmosphere quenching and tempering of small and medium metal parts, with feeding, quench heating, oil quenching, oil removal, washing and tempering units and proposed capacity of approximately 300 kg/h. This describes proposal scale. Actual geometry, loading and process time still require matching acceptance conditions; total installed power cannot establish guaranteed output.

The process overview mentions a cooling tank after tempering, whereas the detailed cooling section lists two 2.2 kW fans using the tempering-furnace belt and drive. Reconcile cooling method on the equipment list and layout before the customer prepares liquid-tank foundations and pipes for equipment actually designed with air cooling.

## Delivery, installation readiness and acceptance are three milestones

| Milestone | Original agreement | Handover work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese domestic delivery | Shanghai Port | Domestic port transport does not establish placement in the customer's factory |
| Factory pre-acceptance | Seller's factory; clause excludes testing | Define checks before shipment and tests deferred to site |
| Site final acceptance | Buyer's site; 24 h continuous fault-free operation | Establish representative parts, operating conditions, tests and records |

The draft specifies delivery to the Chinese port within 100 calendar days after contract effectiveness, subject to extension conditions. It does not define 100 days as the time to production in an overseas factory. Schedule transport, arrival, site preparation, commissioning and testing separately, with their dependencies linked.

## Allocate power connection and unloading to achieve commissioning readiness

The buyer handles unloading, mobile cranes and internal transport. It also provides primary wiring from the transformer low-voltage cabinet to the control cabinet, atmosphere-medium main valves, external exhaust, drainage and water supply. The seller provides secondary cabling, sends personnel to guide installation and commissions the equipment. Check connection locations, arrival sequence and personnel availability accordingly, rather than planning from turnkey wording alone.

Packaging must suit long-distance export transport to Vietnam and mark centre of gravity and lifting points. Freight terms expressly cover domestic road transport to Shanghai Port. Sea freight and overseas onward-transport costs and organisation require the complete commercial documents. Wording on packaging/transport damage responsibility up to site is not converted here into inclusion of all international freight.

## Match languages, drawings and equipment identification before site acceptance

The documentation list requires two sets of Chinese/English parallel texts and one electronic electrical-description copy, with drawing identifiers matching equipment wiring labels. It also lists inverter and instrument parameters, control-system points, wear-part drawings and lubrication locations. Overseas receiving staff need documents corresponding to actual equipment and the current configuration for installation checks and fault location, rather than a generic manual alone.

The 24-hour continuous-run requirement does not automatically establish every part's properties or uniformity throughout every temperature range. For example, tempering is listed at 200–650°C, while uniformity compliance is limited to 400–650°C. Acceptance should define actual operating temperatures, representative loading and measurement methods, and separately assess operation, temperature and product results.

For similar projects, assemble equipment units, the port-to-site handover plan, utilities and intended parts/processes. Suneng's configuration, documentation and commissioning tasks can then be checked item by item before final pricing and acceptance arrangements.
