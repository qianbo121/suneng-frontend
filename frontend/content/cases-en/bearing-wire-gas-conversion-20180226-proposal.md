Suneng's 2018 retrofit recommendation for a bearing-steel wire spheroidising annealing furnace proposes replacing coal-derived cooled fuel gas with natural gas and modifying combustion-ratio control. Two oxygen analysers are optional, each shared by two temperature-control zones. Two separate audible/visual carbon-monoxide alarms are buyer-supplied for key locations around the valve station and furnace area. Their duties and supply boundaries must be checked separately.

## Separate instrument duties and purchasing responsibilities

| Item | Proposal list | Interface requiring confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oxygen analysis | 2 sets; one shared by two temperature-control zones; optional | Sampling locations, scanning sequence, display/recording and any connection to regulation |
| Audible/visual carbon-monoxide alarms | 2 sets, buyer-supplied; at key valve-station and furnace-area locations | Site locations, power, alarm integration and responsibilities |
| Existing temperature zones | Retain 8 burners and 4 control zones, with their existing zoning | Burner replacement or reuse awaits final revision; check quantities and positions separately |

Oxygen analysis is associated with control zones, whereas carbon-monoxide alarms are explicitly located around the valve station and furnace area. They are not one common in-furnace atmosphere-measurement system. Audible/visual alarms do not inspect workpiece decarburisation. Allocate instruments, sampling or mounting accessories, signal integration and site commissioning individually, retaining both “optional” and “buyer-supplied”.

Although one oxygen analyser serves two zones, the list gives no sampling points or switching arrangement. This page retains the shared arrangement without claiming continuous simultaneous measurement of both zones. Analyser quantity does not establish uniform oxygen concentration throughout the chamber.

## Ratio control does not establish oxygen-feedback control

The proposal changes pulse control to proportional control, regulating the fuel side according to combustion-air pressure through an air/gas ratio valve. The burner clause limits approximately equal air and gas pressures to the optimum operating range. This describes the proposed ratio regulation, not equal pressures under every condition. A pressure ratio is not a measured flow ratio.

The temperature-control loop comprises thermocouples, temperature controllers and combustion controllers. Optional oxygen analysers appear separately in the work list. These statements do not prove that oxygen signals participate in automatic regulation. If required, obtain a control description and signal relationship rather than changing “analyser provided” into “automatic oxygen-based ratio control implemented”.

## Define replacement and reuse during fuel conversion

All fuel-gas pipework was to be replaced because the source identifies residual coal tar in the old fuel-gas pipes. Suitable parts of the combustion-air system were to remain, but the furnace-front main and branches were to be replaced. The high-pressure centrifugal blower and heat exchanger are explicitly reused, as are exhaust pipework and induced-draught fan. Separate these items: blower reuse does not mean all air ducts remain, and replacing gas pipes does not mean rebuilding the entire furnace.

The work list includes connecting-cable installation, but a parenthetical note assigns removal, cable purchase and wiring to the buyer. Reconcile this boundary in the final responsibility schedule. It is not recast here as all wiring by Suneng or all installation by the buyer. Optional instruments, buyer-supplied alarms and existing-system signal connections should be checked within one wiring and commissioning scope.

## Results this proposal cannot establish

The original aims to improve air/fuel matching and reduce workpiece oxidation and decarburisation, using proposed performance-assurance wording. Here these remain the intended problems to solve. Actual atmosphere, oxidation/decarburisation and temperature uniformity require operating and workpiece-inspection records. Fuel conversion and added instruments are not reported as achieved compliance, reduced oxidation loss or energy savings.

Design inputs also conflict: the gas-main description uses total burner power of 1840 kW, while a later heat-supply calculation gives eight burners at 250 kW each, totalling 2000 kW. The stated 202 Nm³/h is therefore not presented as a confirmed maximum gas requirement for a new project. Reconcile burner power, calorific value and supply conditions before selecting equipment.
