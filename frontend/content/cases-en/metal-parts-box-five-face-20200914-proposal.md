Five-sided heating describes where heating elements are installed; two-zone control describes which elements share temperature regulation. The counts are not interchangeable. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s high-temperature box-furnace proposal dated 14 September 2020 places elements on both side walls, the rear wall, door and hearth, divided into front and rear control zones. For similar metal-part heating, map loading to those zones before checking measurement and verification. Five heating faces do not mean five independent temperature zones.

## Map heating faces and control zones together

The five faces are explicitly the two sides, rear, door and hearth; the two zones run front to rear. Heat from the hearth resistance strips radiates to workpieces through 40 mm-thick dense-corundum hearth plates. Element position, surface coverage and control circuits are distinct configuration levels.

For new loading, mark each workpiece and support, then overlay zone boundaries and element coverage. If front and rear loads differ in thickness, quantity or required curve, assess the need for separate regulation and whether sensors represent the corresponding positions. If one side within a zone needs independent adjustment, check circuit allocation or revise the design. Five faces alone do not provide independent side control.

## Include hearth plates and door heating in the assessment

Dense-corundum plates cover the hearth elements, so supports and placement must correspond to this structure. The source gives neither permissible local loads for arbitrary workpieces nor position-specific support checks. Hearth-plate thickness cannot be used to invent a furnace load rating. For new blocks, trays or broad contact supports, supply contact locations, weight and coverage to the designer, and check effects on bottom heat transfer and loading clearance.

The heated door has a metal cable carrier protecting its power cable. Heating is permitted only after the door is compressed into position; raising it automatically disconnects heater power. Travel switches limit movement, with a dual-switch arrangement against upward overtravel. Check door power, moving cables and position signals together, rather than simply counting lifting mechanisms.

Check the door movement envelope against actual handling tools and operator positions. Agree who verifies which actions during signal faults, interrupted movement and recovery. The documented interlocks are proposed arrangements, not proof of site verification or a basis for inventing automatic fault-response sequences.

## What can two-zone temperature records establish?

A 2-channel paperless recorder records real-time zone temperatures and heating/cooling times; each zone is regulated from its thermocouple signal. Two furnace curves show the zonal process, but do not establish identical temperatures at every workpiece location or core. Changed loading may require workpiece measurements according to product quality requirements.

The source defines “control accuracy ±1°C” relatively specifically as the difference between steady-state process indication and setpoint. This differs from “furnace uniformity ≤±10°C”; it is not the spread throughout the effective heating zone. Uniformity measurement is assigned to a qualified third party, but the cited proposal has no actual report. Agree temperature, sensors and loading before verification.

Empty-furnace heating from 20°C to 1200°C is specified as no more than 3.5 h. This cannot directly schedule a loaded batch's heating, holding and handling. Where production depends on thick-section core temperature, obtain the workpiece heating and measurement requirements before assessing the cycle.

## Compare supply scope together with verification records

Suneng proposes transport, site installation and commissioning, and foundation, layout, utility-interface and electrical documents. The buyer supplies one three-phase, five-wire AC380V feed to the equipment's main incoming cabinet. Establish cabinet position, foundations and equipment/site wiring boundaries before comparing installation scope; “installation included” does not include all building work by implication.

The proposal calls for pre-acceptance before shipment and subsequent delivery and final-acceptance procedures under the parties' technical agreement. These are proposed procedures, not evidence of delivery or acceptance. Align loading, zoning and required records so later tests can assess suitability of the five-face, two-zone design.

## Common procurement questions

### Can two furnaces with two zones but different heating faces be directly equated?

No. Compare element layout, workpieces, supports and sensors. Two zones describe only the number of control groups. Suitability of different heating-face arrangements depends on loading conditions, not zone count alone.

### What should an initial enquiry include?

Send drawings, individual and total batch weights, placement and supports, actual process curve and inspection requirements. Mark where and why separate front/rear adjustment is needed. Include existing handling tools, foundations and cabinet layout. This supports loading/zoning assessment before refining instruments and acceptance scope.
