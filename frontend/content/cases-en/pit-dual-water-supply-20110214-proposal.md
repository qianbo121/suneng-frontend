Seal cooling on the furnace does not mean that external water-supply equipment is included. Suneng's 2011 proposal for a steel-wire pit spheroidising annealing furnace describes dual water supplies for the lid and retort-mouth flanges, but the quotation explicitly assigns the external circulating pump and plastic water tank to the user. Define furnace cooling components, external water sources and backup-supply changeover conditions separately.

## Identify what is being cooled

The water-cooling system serves the lid perimeter flange and vacuum-retort mouth flange to protect their sealing rings. Separately, a lid-mounted fan circulates the furnace atmosphere, and forced air cools the outside of the retort. Show seal water cooling, internal circulation and external air cooling separately in the drawings. Seal cooling is not a workpiece quenching system.

## Clarify how the second supply starts

“A second system can be started” shows that another supply path was considered. It does not establish whether the sources are independent, how they operate normally, or who or what signal initiates changeover. Obtain the water-circuit drawing and action description to confirm changeover conditions and the cooling that must be maintained at the seals. The wording does not prove fully automatic redundant water supply.

Even once the seal circuit is connected, check that the external pump and tank match that drawing. If an existing site system will be used, both parties should confirm supply conditions, connection points and the second supply. The source lacks complete conditions for selecting pump flow, head and tank capacity for a new project; no generic selection figures are added here.

## Read the total-price notes alongside “pump circulation system”

The itemised lid-cooling entry lists “water jacket, pump circulation system, pipes and valves”, while the total-price notes exclude the external water-cooling circulation equipment and assign the pump and plastic tank to the user. Reconcile these statements in the equipment list and boundary drawing, identifying furnace scope and site preparation. The item name does not establish that the entire external circulation unit is included; equally, the final exclusion does not establish that all furnace-mounted water-cooling parts are omitted.

The historical quotation also excludes lifting fixtures, external secondary power cabling and conduits between furnace and control cabinet, and foundation construction. Inclusion of design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning does not bring these explicit exclusions back into scope. Confirm a common scope before pricing a new project. Historical line-item amounts do not establish the price of the current external water system.

## What can this proposal help establish?

It identifies a specific purchasing interface: furnace seal cooling may be designed into the proposal while the buyer remains responsible for external circulation equipment. Confirm the final water circuit, pump and tank list, second-supply conditions and commissioning checks together. The source includes no completed water-supply failure/changeover test, so the proposed arrangement must not be reported as a verified result.
