Suneng's controlled-atmosphere roller-supported mesh-belt furnace proposal combines an oxygen probe and carbon-potential controller with oil-fume handling at the furnace exit. Carbon-potential measurement alone is insufficient for comparison. As workpieces drop from the protective-atmosphere heating zone into oil, the chute's oil-level seal, oil curtain and extraction also affect the proposed atmosphere-control conditions.

## Measurement, regulation and passage sealing have different duties

| Configuration | Proposal | Conditions to match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atmosphere feedstocks | Methanol and propane | Confirm against gas-supply drawings and material/process requirements; no recipe is provided here |
| Measurement and regulation | 1 oxygen probe, 1 carbon-potential controller, valves and flowmeters | Measurement location, controlled variable and verification method |
| Discharge chute | Extends below quench-oil level to form a seal | Actual level and workpiece passage conditions |
| Oil-fume suppression | Oil curtain and extraction, with separate extraction/combustion arrangements | Chute and exhaust-system connections, not only probe presence |

The proposal uses the oxygen probe and carbon controller with atmosphere-supply equipment for regulation. It separately uses an oil curtain and overhead extraction to reduce fumes entering the heating zone and disturbing control. The former measures and regulates; the latter addresses downstream connection conditions. Neither replaces the other. Follow the workpiece route from heating to oil and check connections, seal boundaries and exhaust destinations individually.

## Show the oil seal and level alarm together

The chute extends into the oil to seal the passage, while the tank includes low-level control and an alarm. Level therefore affects both cooling and the chute seal. The source gives no final setpoint, seal depth or complete abnormal-action logic suitable for adoption here. A level alarm does not establish validated protection for every loss-of-level condition. Confirm the final tank/chute drawing, detector location and interlock description together.

A high-purity nitrogen purge connection above the oil is provided with a ball valve. This establishes a reserved connection, not complete purge procedures for start/stop, gas loss or maintenance. Equipment process and control documents must define supply permissions, exhaust and abnormal shutdown. A connection description is not converted into nitrogen-purging instructions.

## Two extraction locations are not one hood

The quenching-furnace section extracts and burns discharge-chute waste gas. The oil-tank section places a fume hood above the workpiece elevator, connectable to workshop extraction. These are different locations. Confirm equipment-side supply, workshop connections and coordinated combustion, ventilation and gas conditions for each. One extraction pump or hood does not establish inclusion of all exhaust treatment and abnormal protection.

## Carbon-potential curves and workpiece inspection need separate evidence

The heating zone is 400 mm effectively wide and 3200 mm long, with approximately 150 kg/h conditional on shape, piece weight, piling and process. Confirm material, surface requirements and loading before reviewing atmosphere control and inspection. The wording “ensure no oxidation or decarburisation” is a configuration objective. A probe or displayed curve does not prove satisfactory results for arbitrary materials.

Hot acceptance separately includes three-point uniformity testing in the same zone at 800°C in the quenching furnace, plus agreed distortion and hardness inspections. Furnace temperature and oxidation/decarburisation concern different objects. If surface quality is a purchasing objective, add representative materials and inspection criteria while keeping downstream sealing/exhaust conditions consistent with the tests.

The sample includes quenching furnace, oil tank, washing, tempering and controls. The buyer provides agreed primary power and site utilities; secondary wiring between machines is seller scope. Supply actual workpieces, atmosphere/surface requirements and oil-tank/workshop-exhaust layouts to assess whether suitable chute and atmosphere-system interfaces are available.
