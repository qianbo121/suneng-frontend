A quench tank may be included while external cooling remains the user's responsibility. Suneng's bogie-hearth furnace proposal lists the tank, agitators and support platform, but separately states “quench-oil tank cooling system: user-supplied”. Separate internal tank equipment from external cooling when defining the complete supply, and reconcile the medium and batch-loading conditions.

## What does “quench tank included” cover?

The principal equipment list includes a quench tank. Its section specifies two submerged agitators, a support platform and an overflow system referring to an outdoor water pool, followed by the user-supplied cooling system. Confirm tank equipment, return connections and external cooling separately. Agitators promote fluid movement, but their quantity or a circulation-pump flow figure does not prove that site cooling equipment is included or adequate.

## Furnace capacity does not settle tank conditions

The furnace has a rated load of 3 tonnes. However, the tank entry headed “maximum quench weight per batch” gives “3T/h (including fixtures)”. Batch weight and an hourly unit are inconsistent. Confirm whether the entry means batch quantity or hourly capacity; do not silently remove “/h” or promise 3 tonnes per hour. The support platform's ≥5-tonne rating is a different quantity and cannot replace either figure.

List workpieces and fixtures together, then specify entry temperature, medium requirements, permissible fluid-temperature change and conditions for the next batch. Equipment and process personnel can then confirm external cooling. This article does not import recovery times from other tank proposals or convert spare structural load capacity into processing capacity.

## Reconcile the medium first

The tank section alternates between “quench-oil tank”, “water capacity”, “water agitation” and return to an outdoor water pool. This does not establish whether the final medium is oil or water. Cooling, agitation and return conditions must follow the confirmed medium and operating duty. The mixed wording is not a specification for equipment universally suitable for both.

The source also states a water-temperature difference requirement after agitation starts. That is distinct from incoming workpiece heat and recovery before the next batch. Reconcile the medium and test conditions before defining measurement points, loading and records. The statement is not presented as an accepted quenching-uniformity result.

## Close the supply interfaces

Obtain the tank equipment list, connection drawing to the outdoor system and cooling-system responsibility schedule. Specify how user-supplied equipment connects and participates in commissioning. The source provides principles and line items, insufficient to allocate the complete supply of the circulation pump, external pool and all connecting pipework without a final list. Suneng's listed installation and commissioning work does not override the explicit user-supplied cooling condition.

The first page gives a preparation date of 15 March 2024, while the final page is signed 12 May 2026. Confirm the applicable revision before a new purchase. This article describes only identifiable proposed structures and interfaces requiring clarification.
