Circulation-fan speed and support-frame cooling serve different objects. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s steel-part bogie-hearth proposal dated 21 March 2024 lists two high-temperature axial fans with water-cooled frames, using variable-speed operation: faster at low temperature and slower at high temperature. Confirm temperature-dependent speed and cooling-water circuits separately. Two fans alone do not establish complete high-temperature operating conditions.

## Variable speed needs defined temperature/speed conditions

The source specifies high speed at low temperature and low speed at high temperature, without dividing temperatures, speeds or switching conditions. This supports a temperature-dependent speed concept, not invented temperature shutdowns or frequency limits. Obtain the operating range compatible with impeller, shaft and motor from the designer, then compare it with the process curve.

## Water-cooled frames and furnace airflow are separate interfaces

Each fan uses a dedicated water-cooled frame with a 1.5 kW motor; impeller and hot shaft section are Cr28Ni48 heat-resistant steel. Water cools equipment components, while the fan circulates furnace gas. This is not workpiece water quenching. Provide site water according to final fan documents, including flow, pressure, quality and equipment response to water loss. No undocumented thresholds or interlock logic are added here.

Impeller/shaft material does not establish that the complete fan assembly can run continuously at the furnace's maximum temperature. Check component limits, frame cooling and variable-speed range together before defining fan use during the actual heating programme.

## Match furnace temperatures to every relevant component

The table lists rated operating temperature 1200°C, while the hearth-plate description permits long-term use at 1100°C. Their compatibility requires design review. Temperature affects more than fans: confirm hearth plates and other components for proposed temperature, duration and load, then form consistent supply and acceptance conditions.

The source also gives both 3 t and 5 t loading statements. Neither is selected as an available capacity here. The differences do not prevent explaining speed-control and cooling roles, but do affect applicability to specific workpieces. Reconcile loading and fixture inclusion before equipment approval.

## How should trials cover these conditions?

The proposal has 2 control zones and corresponding recording, with Suneng site installation/commissioning and empty trials before loaded heat-up. Correlate the actual temperature programme, fan-speed states, frame-cooling states and furnace records. Assess proposed uniformity ±8°C through agreed tests, not variable-speed equipment alone.
