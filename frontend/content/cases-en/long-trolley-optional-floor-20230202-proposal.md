Optional hearth plates must be identified explicitly when comparing loading and supply scope. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s 19 m bogie-hearth furnace proposal dated 2 February 2023 records a 50 t-class loading arrangement, 12 heating zones and 6 roof circulation fans, while marking 30 mm heat-resistant steel hearth plates as a buyer option. Selecting or omitting them changes support, element protection and bottom heat-transfer checks; the difference cannot disappear from the base configuration.

## The option is a physical interface, not just a note

The source first lists dense load-bearing and lightweight insulating bricks within the bogie frame, then heat-resistant steel plates above them. The plates have underside reinforcing grids and hooked sectional joints. Their stated purposes are load support, distribution of bottom heat and reduced scale entry that could short-circuit elements. These are design explanations, not operating results.

If plates are selected, show joints, supports and workpiece/spacer contacts. If omitted, the designer must define alternative support and heater protection. Removing an option from a quotation cannot leave the same contact surface, protection and heat path implicitly guaranteed.

## Separate rounding from auxiliary loads in the 26-piece, 50 t statement

The loading line reads “1.93 tonnes/piece × 26 pieces = 50 tonnes”. The stated factors actually total 50.18 t, a rounding difference. It is not an exact mass equality or an approved capacity margin. Check weighed workpieces, spacers and relevant optional-component loads, defining what 50 t includes and how it may be distributed.

Over the 19 m working length, placement is more informative than total weight alone. Show contact areas, support spans, longitudinal gaps and plate joints, checking local loads against permitted locations. Averaging total load cannot establish compliance at every support.

## Match 12 zones and 6 fans to actual loading

Elements around the chamber and beneath the hearth are divided into 12 front/rear and upper/lower zones, with 6 roof axial circulation fans. Counts are supported by the text, but no complete zone/fan-coverage drawing is given. Do not assign two zones per fan or claim verified temperatures across all 26 pieces.

Maximum temperature is 950°C and proposed uniformity ±10°C. Changes to optional plates, spacers or loading require representative-load checks of bottom heat transfer and airflow obstruction under agreed conditions. Fan count alone cannot preserve a uniformity conclusion. High-temperature fan operation is a proposed requirement; actual applicability, cooling and maintenance must match final component documents.

## Make the option explicit in procurement and acceptance

State whether plates are selected, their coverage, support connection and supplier, accompanied by the loading drawing. Check door, bogie and heating actions for the final load. The source proposes heating after door compression, heater isolation on opening, bogie travel after the door reaches position, and bogie limits/stops. These do not replace loaded movement verification.

Suneng proposes site installation/commissioning; the buyer prepares facilities and supplies energy/lifting support. The source distinguishes empty from loaded heat-up commissioning. Include the selected plates and representative supports in testing so acceptance loading matches subsequent production.
