Workpieces, lifting fixtures and insulating covers in a furnace group must be assessed along the actual transfer route, not against one shared load rating. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s historical proposals from December 2023 to April 2024 separately list protective-atmosphere pit, nitrate-salt and tempering furnaces, air-cooling and washing equipment, and water-cooling/handling auxiliaries. The salt-furnace proposal also sends an insulating cover through heating and transfer with the load. First identify which components enter each station, then compare space, thermal load and interfaces.

## The transfer cover creates component handovers, not just a lifting stroke

The salt proposal heats a heat-resistant steel insulating cover together with the workpiece in the pre-evacuated furnace. Above the nitrate crucible, upper stops support the cover while the workpiece enters the salt on its lifting fixture. Check immersion space for workpiece/fixture, the retained cover position, stop contact and later removal space separately. Hook reach alone is insufficient.

The proposed transfer surface-temperature drop is ≤30°C. This is not a measured batch or core-temperature result. Confirm initial condition, surface sensors, timing interval, cover/workpiece assembly and actual route. A lifting speed cannot directly establish compliance with the temperature-drop criterion.

## Different stations have different load definitions

| Key information | Historical record | Source conditions |

| Nitrate-salt batch | ≤300 kg | Workpieces + rack; salt-temperature rise ≤15°C within 5 min after quenching. |
| Tempering rated load | 1000 kg | Workpieces + rack. |
| Manipulator rated lifting load | 3 t | Handling-equipment parameter, not hot-assembly verification. |

The salt criterion combines load definition and bath-temperature rise; 1000 kg belongs to another furnace. A full tempering batch cannot automatically enter the salt furnace together, nor is the entire group uniformly rated at 300 kg. Check mass, heat and occupied space station by station for the actual route and batch split.

The manipulator reuses workshop rails, rail accessories and conductor bars, with hooks and telescopic arms entering hot areas. A 3 t rating alone does not verify reused rails, hot fixtures, cover connections and the complete assembly. Handling specialists must confirm hot-load forces, dwell, clearances, brakes and limits. Stainless-steel selection at one location is not verified high-temperature lifting capacity.

## Separate component cooling water from workpiece cooling media

The February 2024 water-cooling proposal serves pre-evacuated-furnace seals, fans, ignition/dosing/sample tubes and vacuum-line heat exchangers, plus tempering fans and salt-furnace agitators. Its 12 m³/h and 37–32°C condition concerns those components, not quench-tank flow or whole-group workpiece cooling capacity.

Piping scope includes mains and branches to the furnace front. The pre-evacuated-furnace proposal additionally includes water temperature/pressure monitoring, low-pressure/overtemperature alarms and emergency-water connection. Align circuits, interfaces and fault responses across both documents. One total flow figure does not establish complete cooling provision.

## Review hot transfer and media conditions together

The pre-evacuated furnace distinguishes nitrogen protection from a carbon-potential atmosphere and describes oxygen, furnace-pressure, water and fault monitoring. The salt furnace separately includes agitation, external air cooling and temperature interlocks. These are distinct systems. Atmosphere equipment does not establish satisfactory surfaces; agitation/cooling does not permit arbitrary thermal loads.

Provide media conditions, substances carried by parts/fixtures, hot-transfer constraints and downstream liquid/salt collection destinations, then allocate faults and site responsibilities. Reserved process functions require design and verification before use; they are not default enabled features. This page explains handovers rather than turning the equipment list into charging, medium-adjustment or emergency-bypass instructions.

For review, provide workpiece drawings/process route, stage loading schedules, fixture/cover drawings, lifting clearance, reused-rail information and water, electricity, gas and discharge interfaces. Assess the actual assembly entering each station rather than comparing furnace diameter or tonnage alone.
