In Suneng's 2018 mesh-belt atmosphere heat-treatment line proposal, the upper row of support rolls carries the forward belt run and the lower row carries its return. They are not two workpiece-loading levels. For preliminary saw-blade assessment, trace the actual loading surface and discharge position first, then compare representative sizes, loading pattern and heating time.

## Count the rolls and identify what each row carries

| Position | Original proposal | Loading interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upper chamber rolls | 32, carrying the forward belt run | Check load support and heating along the forward route |
| Lower chamber rolls | 16, carrying the return run | Not an independent second loading level |
| Entry chamber | 7 forward-run and 5 return-run rolls, plus reversing rolls | Connect separately to the internal forward and return paths |
| Representative capacity | φ350×1.5: 209 pieces/h, noted as 5000 pieces/24 h | Source's representative saw-blade basis, not a guarantee for other sizes |

Large-diameter reversing rolls are provided at belt turns, and the entry chamber separately supports forward and return runs. The two levels describe one belt circuit, not two independent conveyor lines. Mark the actual load-bearing surface, blade orientation and usable width in the loading drawing. Two roll rows must not multiply hourly output.

## Keep representative blades and daily counts on the same basis

The main table gives maximum production of 5000–6000 pieces/day, explicitly over 24 hours. A later section gives 209 pieces/h for φ350×1.5 blades, noted as 5000 pieces/24 hours. The latter provides a clearer representative workpiece, but does not guarantee the table's upper output for all sizes. A 24-hour capacity is not one shift's output. Confirm actual loading and heating with representative parts for the new project.

The effective heating zone is 9300×1000×100 mm, with adjustable heating time of 5–40 minutes. These are equipment dimensions and an adjustment range, not a suitable heating time for every saw blade. Match spacing, occupied width and approved process duration before comparing continuous capacity. Adjustment range does not prove the workpiece meets its process requirement.

## Follow discharge through the oil tank and downstream receiving

The furnace chute extends into quenching liquid to form a seal, with an oil curtain and extraction near the liquid surface. After the oil-tank elevator, a rapid roller conveyor sends blades toward a press. Check chute, tank transport, lifting and downstream receiving together. The description does not establish that the press itself is included in Suneng's package, and “sent to a press after oil quenching” must not become “press-quenched directly from the furnace”.

The source gives elevator speed as 20–60 m/min, while heating uses a 5–40-minute duration range. These belong to different mechanisms and are not combined into a confirmed total cycle. In particular, verify elevator units and actual speed against adopted drawings and the drive list before calculating receiving capacity.

## Confirm atmosphere and quality against the actual process

The document title says “carburising atmosphere”, but the supply section specifically describes nitrogen or nitrogen plus dissociated ammonia gas, with a low-temperature protective solenoid shutoff. This page therefore calls it an atmosphere heat-treatment proposal and does not establish carburising from the title. Match actual material, atmosphere purpose, supply composition and start/stop protection in final process/control documents. A reserved nitrogen purge connection is not a complete purging procedure.

Uniformity of ≤±2.5°C across the soaking-zone section is a proposal criterion, not measured temperatures at every blade location. Provide representative blade drawings, material, loading and quality requirements first. Check this carrying route and downstream receiving, then agree temperature-survey scope and product inspection.
