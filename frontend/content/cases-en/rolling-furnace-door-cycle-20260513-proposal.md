A Suneng chamber-furnace discussion draft for a hot-rolling installation proposes door lifting/lowering within 3 seconds, with door heating, final-position clamping and front/rear temperature-control zones. Procurement should assess door movement, actual material pickup and delivery to the rolling mill separately. One mechanism's speed is not the complete production cycle.

## Define the movement covered by 3 seconds

The lifting section pairs a 1200 mm stroke with a time of no more than 3 seconds. Pre-acceptance separately lists rapid lifting/lowering, interlock and sealing tests. These are proposed mechanism and inspection requirements. They do not include gripping the workpiece, withdrawing it through the opening, delivering it to the mill and establishing permission for the next pickup within those 3 seconds. This page does not present that figure as a measured line cycle.

## The moving door also heats and seals

The proposal installs heating elements on the door's inner face with flexible electrical connections. It calls for the heating circuit to be disconnected when the door is not in position and for pneumatic clamping after the door is fully lowered. Integration must define confirmation of handling-tool withdrawal, door closure, completed clamping and permission to restore heating together. Recording lift-motor running time alone does not cover these states.

The flexible connections move with the door. Proposed service life, temperature resistance and temperature-recovery effects need specific component selections, installation locations and verification evidence. Furnace working temperature cannot be used as the permissible ambient temperature of the entire moving cable assembly.

## Assess the removal path with parts, fixtures and tools together

The source proposes an integral raised edge along both sides and the rear of the baseplate, leaving the door side open for loading and unloading. This structure can be used to check billet placement, tool approach direction and turning space after withdrawal. A billet fitting inside does not establish that the tool can remove it in the intended orientation. Edge height, actual gripping position and door opening should be confirmed on one handling drawing.

The application is heating and holding steel parts at 1050°C before hot rolling, with a separately listed maximum working temperature of 1100°C. Five-sided heating describes elements on both sides, the rear, door and bottom; front/rear zoning describes control division. Neither establishes that workpiece temperature recovers after every opening or determines the rolling-entry temperature of all steels. Actual pickup frequency, quantity per pickup and workpiece requirements remain process decisions for the project.

## Separate mechanism checks from on-site line verification

The draft proposes door and temperature-field checks before shipment, followed on site by simulated-workpiece verification and integrated testing with the rolling mill. Procurement can require records of door movement, actual pickup, delivery to rolling and conditions for the next operation, with workpieces and loading defined. Only these linked conditions establish whether the rapid door suits the required repeated unloading. The source contains no record that this integrated verification has been completed.
