A 2022 Suneng heating proposal for small iron-core workpieces uses pneumatic clamps to hold the bottom pallet during heating. For discharge, the clamps release and a double-speed accumulating chain carries the pallet out. Assess pallet positioning, residence and release conditions before comparing conveyor speed.

## The clamps hold the pallet; conveyor speed is not heating time

The double-speed chain drive section specifies a pallet beneath the parts and pneumatic clamps on both sides of the chain. During heating, the clamps grip the pallet and stop workpiece movement. At discharge, they release and the chain moves the pallet. This describes a heating dwell followed by release for transfer.

An enquiry therefore needs the pallet underside, clamping-contact locations and workpiece arrangement together. Part dimensions alone do not allow the supplier to confirm reliable pallet positioning or smooth removal after release. The movement description should also define what ends the heating dwell and how clamped and released positions are confirmed. This proposal lacks a complete sensor list and fault-handling programme; it cannot be used to construct site operating instructions.

## One control zone and three fans serve different functions

The parameter table lists 1 temperature-control zone, while the circulation system has 3 fans and baffles forming internal passages. Three circulation fans do not mean three independent control zones. With the pallet and parts in the working space, assess blockage of circulation passages and how representative the temperature measurements are. Fan count alone does not establish full-load temperature uniformity.

The door section specifies double-leaf doors, manual linkage locking and heating-power disconnection on opening. Door closed, pallet in the required position and clamps engaged should each be checked in the movement relationships. The source does not describe how these signals combine into automatic release conditions. Door-open power disconnection must not be expanded into a claim of complete interlocking for all mechanisms.

## Return scope discussions to pallet interfaces and the adopted configuration

The structural scope lists the furnace body, inner casing, heating elements, doors, trolley, circulation fans and baffles, double-speed chain conveyor and temperature-control system. Proposed documentation includes a general assembly drawing, electrical schematic, layout/foundation drawing and operating and maintenance instructions. These provide a basis for requesting pallet interfaces and clamp positions on drawings, while separately confirming pallet supply responsibility, quantity and replacement arrangements. Mentioning a pallet in the structural text does not establish that every pallet and accessory has been quoted.

Model designation, heating power, control descriptions and itemised prices require reconciliation. This article does not combine them into a definite configuration or price. Similar projects can start with an overall pallet-and-workpiece drawing, permissible dwell time and upstream/downstream transfer arrangements, followed by agreement on the final thermal, mechanical and control attachments.
