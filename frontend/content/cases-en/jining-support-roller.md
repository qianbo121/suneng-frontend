Suneng's May 2026 track-roller heat-treatment line proposal for Jining Wuchuang Machinery Co., Ltd. specifies two heating furnaces, two automatic quenching machines and one tempering furnace. One route uses a turning mechanism before joining tempering feed. Review discharge and quenching on each route, then receiving after the merge. The opening “preferably 2 minutes” and individual-machine “4 minutes per piece” are not one confirmed cycle.

Two oscillating pushers transfer parts from heating discharge rollers to quenching and then from quenching to tempering feed. A chute after the other quenching machine includes a turning table. The tempering inlet has a lifting device and feed pusher. Whole-line review must therefore examine simultaneous arrivals, inlet receiving and upstream response when receiving is unavailable, not only one furnace's discharge interval. The source describes action sequences and position detection; abnormal-condition interlocks require validation against final equipment documents.

## Reconcile “2 minutes”, “4 minutes” and “30 pieces”

The opening targets “preferably within 2 minutes”, while heating-furnace parameters give 4 min/piece adjustable over 3–8 min/piece. Parallel routes may involve different single-route and merged counts, but the source does not validate that relationship as a capacity calculation. Two four-minute routes are not automatically a promise of one finished-line piece every two minutes. Define counting position, pieces per feed, staggering and waiting conditions, then test with operating records for the relevant parts.

Basic parameters list maximum design quenching capacity as 500 kg/h and 30 pieces/h. Earlier public wording called this “approximately 30 pieces/h by proposal conversion”; the 30 is actually a source-listed quantity, not a conversion of 500 kg/h for arbitrary workpieces. Tempering is listed as 500 kg (30 pieces) ×2/h, while detailed tempering parameters give 2×450 kg/h. These are proposal figures, not accepted output. Reconcile the two tempering values rather than selecting the larger for a whole-line guarantee. kg/h and pieces/h are not interchangeable across different track-roller weights. The source already makes throughput dependent on dimensions and piece weight, with adjustable cycle; confirm overall capability against representative weight, both feed routes and tempering capacity.

The discharge roller-table section also gives approximately 7 seconds for transfer. This concerns only that transfer section, not positioning, rotary spray quenching, post-quench pushing, turning and tempering feed together. A new project can time these actions separately, using actual workpieces and defined action boundaries.

## Preserve quenching-quality conditions when pursuing cycle time

The proposal covers PC200–PC400 track rollers, with rotary positioning, a vertically moving water-cooling ring and multiple locating/clamping devices. The spray ring has six controlled sections; each pipe has flow/pressure indication and on/off control, with spray duration and flow set by the process. These configurations address multiple sizes but do not establish identical hardening results for all of them. Merged throughput must respect each representative workpiece's quenching and tempering requirements.

The source also includes quenching-fluid circulation, filtration, cooling and temperature detection, with a hooded quenching ring to limit splashing. Check stable fluid supply, positioning, spray state and transfers together. Six spray sections are not six furnace heating zones, and furnace-temperature records alone do not prove each roller's required hardness.

## Information needed before quotation

Suneng's proposal covers heating, quenching, tempering, spray cooling and electrical controls, making it a reference for reviewing two-route integration. Prepare drawings, piece weights and process requirements for each roller size. Confirm single-route and whole-line target counts, tempering loading, transfer layout and quality criteria. Historical quantities and structures are discussion starting points; final supply, site utilities and acceptance require the complete technical agreement for the current project.
