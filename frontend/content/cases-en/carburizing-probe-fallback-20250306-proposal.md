A Suneng carburising test-furnace proposal lists both oxygen-probe feedback control and manual settings following a fault. Keeping the equipment running does not mean carbon potential is still controlled under the same measurement conditions. Comparisons of trial data should retain the control mode, measurement status and workpiece inspection results.

## The two control modes use different information

In the proposed automatic mode, an oxygen probe provides the sensor feedback and a solenoid valve regulates enriching gas. If the probe develops certain faults, a manually entered numerical setting can control the drip rate. The first mode adjusts through measured feedback; the second operates from a manual setting. Continued operation must not be described as proof that carbon-potential accuracy and workpiece quality are unaffected after a fault.

For test work, records should retain the changeover time, control mode, probe fault and recovery information, linked to the corresponding workpieces or specimens. Whether a trial continues and which data remain comparable must follow the agreed process and abnormal-condition documents. This page identifies review items; it does not give manual operating instructions for faults involving a combustible atmosphere.

The equipment structure lists rated heating power of 35 kW. The shell section lists one heating-control zone and two thermocouple sockets, respectively for control and monitoring/alarm duties. Furnace-temperature and carbon-potential measurements serve different purposes. A retained temperature curve cannot make up for lost atmosphere feedback.

## Verify carbon-potential control and case results separately

The proposal states furnace-atmosphere carbon-potential control accuracy of ≤±0.05%C and effective case-depth control accuracy of ≤±0.1 mm. The former concerns furnace-atmosphere control; the latter concerns treated parts. One cannot directly establish the other or remove the need to consider material, initial condition, actual loading and inspection position. The required trial results should be separately identified when confirming the process.

Furnace-temperature uniformity is specified for empty-furnace testing, and the document also lists oxygen-probe reference-air, cleaning and maintenance systems. Procurement should confirm calibration and maintenance interfaces, status records and actual trial inspection methods. An empty-furnace temperature check does not establish that fully loaded parts meet case-depth or microstructure requirements.

## Agree trial records alongside supply attachments

The proposed package includes a temperature-control cabinet, carbon-control system and oxygen-probe maintenance provisions. The configuration description excludes external power wiring between furnace and cabinet and foundation construction, while including design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning. Agree which data are stored, how manual mode is flagged and which curves are handed over together with the hardware list.

This article retains the boundaries of trial control and recording. Furnace-lid drive, fan and temperature-sensor quantities differ between source sections and require a final reconciled configuration table. These unresolved items are neither presented as final equipment specifications nor treated as evidence of completed trials.
