Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s pit resistance furnace proposal dated 19 May 2026 first evacuates the muffle retort, then backfills it with nitrogen. Gas admission and discharge maintain the retort's working pressure during heating and cooling. Check the evacuation stage, nitrogen-protection stage and pressure control during temperature changes separately.

## Identify the capability from the sequence in the source

The proposal describes rapid pre-evacuation of the muffle retort followed by nitrogen backfilling for protection. During operation, automatic gas admission and discharge respond to changes in furnace temperature to maintain working pressure. If your requirement is to sustain a specified vacuum throughout heating, the word “vacuum” in the equipment name is insufficient to establish suitability. Ask the designer whether additional configuration and validation are required.

## Ultimate vacuum and evacuation time are separate acceptance questions

The source's vacuum-system section separately lists an ultimate vacuum of 1×10⁻¹ Pa and a pre-evacuation time of ≤30 min. It does not specify the loading, initial pressure or measurement location under which the two are verified together. It must not be rewritten as “any full load reaches 0.1 Pa within 30 minutes”. Before drafting an acceptance requirement, establish whether the figures apply to the pump set or the complete retort system, whether the test is unloaded, and what constitutes completion of pre-evacuation.

Working pressure after nitrogen backfilling belongs to a different operating stage. The proposal describes a pressure gauge, safety valve, gas admission and discharge, and over-limit alarms, but the cited clauses do not provide the complete pressure settings or transition logic. Confirm the conditions for ending evacuation, permitting backfill, maintaining heating and adding gas during cooling separately. Ultimate vacuum does not substitute for these operating settings.

## Sealing, cooling and loading form part of the same atmosphere conditions

The upper retort uses a rubber sealing ring and a water-cooled structure; the lid includes water-cooled vacuum pipework. A flow-guide cylinder and load-bearing base are fitted inside, while a top-mounted forced-circulation fan organises gas flow within the retort. Supply the cooling-water interfaces, seal checks and loading drawing together, rather than confirming only the vacuum-pump model. The base provides its intended support and the guide cylinder provides the proposed circulation path; workpieces and fixtures must not occupy those spaces without verification.

The required alarms and actions on opening the lid, loss of power, or loss of cooling or gas supply must follow the supporting gas-circuit and control documents. The source specifies cutting heating when the lid opens and stopping heating if furnace temperature or pressure exceeds limits. These proposed measures are retained here, without adding unprovided procedures for loss of water or gas.

## Verify the low-oxidation objective using actual workpieces

The intended application is low-oxidation heat treatment, with nitrogen protection described as preventing oxidation and decarburisation. Suitability for a specific part requires confirmation of the process and inspection method against its material, surface requirements and representative loading. “Nitrogen provided” cannot be turned into “no oxidation for every material”. Suneng was to undertake equipment interconnection, wiring and installation; the buyer was to connect utilities and complete part of the electrical-cabinet installation. Trial production and checks including sealing are scheduled in the source. These remain proposed responsibilities and acceptance arrangements.
