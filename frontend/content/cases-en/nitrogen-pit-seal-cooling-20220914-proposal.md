Cooling a seal and circulating hot gas around workpieces are different duties. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s nitrogen-protected pit tempering furnace proposal dated 14 September 2022 provides water cooling at the retort flange seal and water-cooled circulation-fan bearings. Confirm sealing, fan cooling and furnace atmosphere separately. Cooling water is not a workpiece-cooling system, and a nitrogen inlet does not establish verified protection.

## Identify the components protected by water cooling

| Position or system | Proposed configuration | Relevant assessment object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retort/lid flange | Water cooling at the silicone-rubber seal | Seal operating temperature and gas-tight interface. |
| Lid circulation fan | Water-cooled drive bearings | Fan supports, not water cooling of workpieces. |
| Internal hot-gas circulation | Central lid fan with guide cylinder | Gas circulation within the retort along the guides. |

The source separates the seal from the hot zone: the seal has its own temperature limit, so water cooling is proposed to keep that region cooler. Furnace operating temperatures cannot be applied to seals, bearings or water-system components. Confirm temperatures, cooling supply/return and monitoring for each.

Identify which circuits cool seals, fans and other parts, then check supply capacity, connections and faults. The cited clauses provide no cooling flow, water-loss detection or complete interlock sequence. “Water cooling provided” does not establish that these conditions are resolved, and no operating response to water loss should be invented.

## Verify nitrogen supply separately from protective results

The gas section lists a solenoid valve, flowmeter and pressure gauge. A sealed retort/lid connection, guide cylinder and forced-circulation fan provide proposed equipment conditions for nitrogen operation. Actual protection also depends on workpiece, loading, gas quality, supply/exhaust and purging. Nitrogen piping alone does not establish oxidation-free surfaces.

Where surface condition is specified, include gas quality, inlet/exhaust connections, atmosphere detection and assessment methods in design and acceptance documents. Confirm fault responses and site responsibilities too. This article describes historical interfaces without providing purge counts, flow settings or manual bypass instructions.

## Allow space for buyer-supplied lifting fixtures and air guides

Effective working dimensions are Φ1000 × 1200 mm, with a retort, guide cylinder and heat-resistant steel bases inside/outside the retort. Workpiece lifting fixtures are buyer-supplied. The combined assembly must satisfy entry, support and circulation requirements. Effective dimensions are not a lifting-fixture envelope, and workpiece diameter alone is insufficient.

| Proposal information | Source record | Applicable definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Effective working dimensions | Φ1000 × 1200 mm | Assess workpieces, lifting fixtures and guides together. |
| Heating power | 85 kW | Configuration, not a direct heating-cycle prediction. |
| Operating temperature | 100–750°C | Material process separately determined; not a rating for every component. |

Uniformity ±8°C is explicitly limited to thermal steady state at 180–750°C, while the operating range begins at 100°C. Do not extend it to the whole range or redefine it as workpiece temperature spread. Lower-temperature or different-load requirements need their own tests and acceptance criteria.

## What information is most useful initially?

Provide workpiece and lifting-fixture dimensions, loading, specified tempering/annealing requirements and surface targets, plus site nitrogen and cooling-water conditions. Suneng can check retort, seals, guides and gas interfaces against equipment/site responsibilities. Raise cooling and atmosphere requirements during configuration agreement; “connect water and gas” after installation cannot replace a complete handover.
