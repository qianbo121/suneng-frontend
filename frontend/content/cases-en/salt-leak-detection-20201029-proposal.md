Suneng's 2020 salt-bath heating proposal for steel parts provides a leak-prevention sealing groove at the furnace top plate and salt-leak detection electrodes in a collection trough at the furnace bottom. Confirm sealing, collection and detection locations separately. A salt-leak alarm does not mean the crucible cannot leak or that all subsequent response actions are defined.

## Match top-plate sealing to bottom collection

| Item | Proposal | Review scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top plate | Leak-prevention sealing groove | Seal location and crucible/plate connection |
| Furnace bottom | Leakage collection trough with salt-detection electrodes and alarm | Whether leakage reaches the detectors, collection coverage and alarm testing |
| Crucible | φ500×700 mm, welded heat-resistant steel | Dimensional definition and workpiece/salt space require a section drawing |
| Lid | Manual lever-operated lift | Lid mechanism, not workpiece lifting equipment |

The source does not provide a complete leakage-path drawing connecting the top-plate groove and bottom trough. Review a section identifying crucible, lining, plate and collection area, then establish the electrodes' actual coverage. An alarm should demonstrate detection and indication under agreed test conditions. Electrode presence does not prove timely detection in every leakage direction.

## Material and welding specifications do not replace leak verification

The crucible is specified as tightly welded from 16 mm Cr25Ni20 heat-resistant steel plate. This describes proposed material and construction, without completed weld inspection, service-life or leak-test records. Confirm crucible and sealing requirements against actual salt medium, temperature and loading, then agree manufacturing and alarm-function inspections. General wording such as “no deformation, oxidation or cracking” is not adopted as an unconditional guarantee.

The application and parameters place steel-part salt-bath heating below 950°C, while the crucible section also says “usual temperature ≥950°C”. Clarify whether these concern equipment use or material description. The latter does not increase the process limit here. Reconcile actual working temperature and salt conditions in the adopted documents.

## Define alarm response in control documents

The proposal lists programmed temperature control, fault alarms and recording, but the five reviewed pages do not provide complete actions following an electrode trip. Purchasing documents should identify signal destinations, display/alarm testing and which document defines equipment actions and subsequent handling. This page invents no shutdown, salt-draining or personnel procedure.

Installation and acceptance include pre-acceptance safety checks, empty-furnace commissioning and loaded heat-up. Suneng supplies general arrangement, electrical, layout and foundation drawings; the buyer prepares site conditions and an installation crane. Include collection/detection locations, alarm functions and actual workpiece handling in acceptance preparation. Site installation and signed reports must be obtained as evidence; planned steps are not reported as executed.
