A Suneng technical agreement for an 1850 mm coil annealing line separately provides air cooling, air/water mist, water spraying and drying, with strip-temperature measurement at the heating, air-cooling and drying exits. Selection should assess how these measurements relate to airflow, water flow and main-line operation. Total cooling length alone does not establish exit temperature.

## Identify the strip material before defining the cooling endpoint

The product schedule includes titanium, nickel, zirconium and their alloys, with different width and thickness ranges by material. The main line sends product type, dimensions, code and running speed to the furnace-area controls. Different strips on the same equipment therefore require their own heating-exit conditions, permissible cooling process and downstream receiving conditions. One curve cannot be applied to all products.

The parameter table proposes strip temperature ≤45°C at the cooling exit as an assessment criterion. Confirm it with representative material, dimensions, speed, coolant and measurement location. This page has no corresponding loaded-test records and does not present ≤45°C as achieved for every size.

## Three measuring positions support different adjustments

| Location or structure | Proposed arrangement | Relationship to establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heating-furnace exit | 1 infrared strip pyrometer | Strip temperature corrects furnace-temperature control setpoints |
| Air-cooling exit | 1 infrared strip pyrometer | Exit temperature adjusts air-cooling fan frequency |
| Drying exit | 1 infrared strip pyrometer | Display, monitoring, alarms and coordination with water cooling and main line |

These locations follow strip travel and have different duties. The air-cooling section also measures low differential pressure to regulate its exhaust fan; this is section pressure, not strip temperature. Link strip temperature, pressure and exhaust state to their respective actions. Connecting all signals to controls is not an explanation of their relationships.

Pyrometer ranges vary by location, with 0–500°C at the drying exit. Before acceptance use, confirm surface condition, measuring position and calibration method. A reading at one surface location should not automatically become a conclusion for the full strip width or through-thickness temperature.

## Coordinate air, mist and spray cooling with the line

The proposal separates air cooling, air-plus-mist and water-spray sections. Mist headers are staggered above and below the strip, with separately adjustable water pressure. The spray section has a main regulating valve, flowmeter and pressure measurement. Confirm which sections operate and permissible adjustments for each material, thickness and speed. Adding nozzle counts does not establish cooling capability.

On overtemperature at the drying exit, the draft proposes increasing water flow in the water-cooling section and sending an alarm from furnace controls to the main line, which decides whether to initiate furnace shutdown. This defines a system handover, not a complete stop programme. Responses to high strip temperature, inadequate water and drive faults require a unified integration description and test records.

## Separate furnace-component cooling from direct strip cooling

A dedicated closed-loop furnace-roller cooling system supplies roller journals, door frames, radiation pyrometers and similar components. Mist and sprays contacting the strip are another water system. Cooling-section wastewater enters a collection pit, after which treatment and recirculation are outside the seller's scope. Assess equipment cooling, strip cooling and wastewater destinations separately rather than giving only one total water quantity.

Supply scope also needs reconciliation: the main text primarily assigns cables, cable trays and process platforms to the buyer, while the responsibility table assigns some furnace-area cables, trays and operating platforms to the seller. This page selects neither version. Start with a located interface drawing, then confirm design, supply, installation and commissioning item by item.

## Prepare one operating-conditions schedule

List material grades, strip width/thickness, line speed, temperature targets by section, water/air conditions and allowable downstream states together, then match sensors and actuators. The draft requires loaded trials and final assessment with materials, utilities and equipment states agreed by both parties. Only after those conditions are confirmed does the exit-temperature criterion have a verifiable application range.
