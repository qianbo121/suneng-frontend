Buyer-supplied support blocks need to be specified together with the steel plates, bogie supports and burners on both sides, before loading arrangements and temperature verification are agreed. A thick-steel-plate gas preheating furnace proposal from Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd., dated 19 December 2023, specified 30 t per batch excluding fixtures and support blocks, with the blocks supplied by the buyer. Suneng proposed supplying the furnace, bogie, combustion and control systems, and carrying out installation and commissioning. This supply split does not remove the need to coordinate block contact positions, loading height and hot-gas passages with the furnace design.

## Separate plate weight, fixture weight and actual support conditions

The parameter table explicitly states “30 t/batch, excluding fixtures and support blocks”; the structural section separately requires a maximum single-load bogie capacity of at least 30 T. The first defines the loading basis and the second is a proposed structural requirement. Together, they do not establish a total load limit for an arbitrary fixture weight.

List the steel-plate weight and the weight of all fixtures separately. Show contact positions, support spans and load distribution on the bogie layout so the equipment supplier can check capacity during heating and bogie travel. If weight is concentrated on unconfirmed support locations, revise the fixture layout or reassess the load-bearing design before agreeing the batch arrangement.

The quotation of the same date lists 14 heat-resistant steel support blocks, marked 1500 × 350 mm, and recommends buyer supply. It does not identify the direction represented by either dimension. This is evidence of the quoted configuration at that time, not a manufacturing drawing: neither a complete block cross-section nor an assembly layout is provided. In particular, dividing 30 tonnes by 14 does not give the permissible force on each block. Plate contact locations and whether the supports engage simultaneously depend on the actual arrangement.

## Show block height and position on the loading drawing

Support blocks take up loading height and set the gap between plate and bogie. Check whether the complete plate-and-block assembly can pass through the opening and fit the working space, then compare it with burner positions and hot-gas passages. Plate length and width alone are insufficient.

| Item to check | 2023 proposal record | Application to a new project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chamber working dimensions | Width 8.3 × length 3.4 × height 1.0 m | Preserve the source's width/length order. These are not maximum plate dimensions; allow for blocks and loading clearances. |
| Furnace opening | Width 8.3 × height 1.0 m | Check the complete plate-and-block envelope and travel direction. |
| Bogie surface | 8.3 × 3.4 m | Check bogie supports and block contact locations; similar surface area alone does not establish load capacity. |

The proposal places high-velocity gas burners on both sides and uses combustion-gas flow for heating, with furnace-pressure regulation, exhaust and heat recovery. Changing block position or height changes the space available for hot gas beneath and around the plates. The supplier therefore needs to assess plate placement, blocks, burner elevations and jet directions together. Clearances and positions must be determined for the new design; the source provides no universal dimensions to copy.

Aluminium silicate fibre modules were proposed for the roof and four sides, with a compression-sealed door and an exhaust-side heat exchanger. These features address insulation, sealing and heat recovery respectively. Once loading is agreed, heating performance must be assessed through the relevant temperature tests; the names of structural features alone do not establish uniform plate heating.

## Applying furnace-temperature criteria to thick-plate preheating

The proposal specifies a rated operating temperature of 500°C and maximum operating temperature of 800°C. These are equipment proposal temperatures. The actual plate temperature and holding time depend on material grade, thickness, preceding and subsequent operations, and process requirements; they cannot be selected simply from the maximum temperature.

Zoned furnace-temperature measurement and control, with paperless recording, were also proposed. Furnace sensors observe the heating process and are not automatically workpiece sensors. The method for measuring plate temperature and correlating it with furnace records must be agreed for the actual plate-and-block layout.

| Source criterion | Original 2023 value | Clarification needed before assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Furnace temperature uniformity | ≤±15°C during holding | Sensor region, deviation reference, loading state and test temperature are not detailed. This is not automatically the plate temperature spread. |
| Furnace temperature stability | ≤±5°C | Sensor location, assessment period and reference are not detailed. Compare separately from uniformity between locations. |
| Empty-furnace heat-up time | ≤2 h to 500°C | Starting temperature and full timing conditions are unspecified. This is not the time for loaded thick plates to reach temperature. |
| Heating rate under load | 150–300°C/h, described as “steplessly adjustable” | It is unclear whether furnace or workpiece temperatures are assessed; loading and calculation period are also unspecified. This is not a guaranteed plate-core heating rate. |

The source calls for loaded heat-up commissioning after successful empty-furnace commissioning. Where blocks are buyer-supplied, loaded verification should use the plate-and-block combination agreed by both parties. Record furnace temperatures and the workpiece temperatures relevant to the process separately, defining sensor positions, the criterion for reaching temperature and the start of holding time. If the workpiece has not met the process requirement, check loading, sensors and the heating process before revising the proposal. Existing furnace-temperature values cannot replace that assessment.

## Confirm gas connections and hot loading and unloading conditions

This natural-gas-fired proposal includes automatic purging, flame detection and flame-failure shut-off, with gas isolation and an alarm upon low gas pressure or fan failure. The electrical section also specifies fail-closed combustion valves on power loss. The source lists a furnace-side gas supply pressure of 8–10 kPa, the buyer's responsibility. These are historical design descriptions. For a new project, agree the gas handover point, purge medium and coverage, completion criteria, and fault reset and restart requirements against the final combustion configuration. Allocate design, supply and verification responsibilities between the equipment and site contractors.

Compressed air operates control valves and compression seals. Furnace pressure is controlled through pressure sensing, regulation and exhaust equipment. Gas, air, door sealing and exhaust are interconnected heating services; buyer procurement of an individual valve does not separate it from equipment controls or commissioning.

The bogie also interfaces with lifting hot plates after discharge. The proposal prohibits bogie movement during heating, permits travel only after the door reaches its specified raised position, and includes door safety pins for maintenance. A new project must separately define position retention on loss of power or air, permitted hot-travel and lifting conditions, personnel exclusion, maintenance and recovery. Existing interlock and safety-pin descriptions do not replace design and verification of these states. Conversely, an omission from the record does not prove that protection is absent.

## Put buyer-supplied parts, equipment and site work in one scope schedule

The historical quotation dated 19 December 2023 includes tax and proposes design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning, plus secondary cables and cable trays between furnace and control cabinet. It excludes the equipment foundation, and its summary lists support blocks as buyer-supplied. This records the scope of that configuration, not current commercial terms.

| Work | Allocation in the source | Handover to settle for a new project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixture support blocks | Buyer supply in both proposal and quotation | Who produces fixture drawings, checks compatibility with bogie and burners, and approves the loaded-test combination? |
| Furnace, bogie, combustion and electrical controls | Seller supply | Agree the layout, interfaces and coordinated actions in each state with the buyer-supplied parts; align drawings and supply schedules. |
| Foundations and embedded parts | Buyer scope includes foundation construction; the proposal assigns equipment-foundation design to the seller | Distinguish equipment foundation information from site civil design, construction, embedment and checking. |
| Site connections and lifting | Buyer provides site water, electricity, gas and safe lifting, among other support | Allocate inlet valves, external pipes and cables, lifting attachments and site assistance. “Installation included” does not mean all site services are included. |
