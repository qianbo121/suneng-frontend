Bogie travel and door lifting do not necessarily use the same power connection. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s bogie-hearth proposal dated 8 April 2022 separately describes a metal cable carrier for door heaters, moving/fixed contacts for bogie heaters and an automatic cable reel for the bogie drive. Distinguish their equipment and movement paths during installation and action checks rather than calling all three a “bogie cable”.

## Separate connections by the equipment they supply

| Supplied equipment | Source arrangement | Site check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Door heaters | Power cable protected by a metal carrier | Cable route and connections during lifting. |
| Bogie heaters | Rear electrode box with moving and fixed contacts | Engagement at bogie position and heating conditions. |
| Bogie drive | Cabinet-fed cable automatically paid out/retracted by a reel | Cable handling and route during travel. |

The reel pays out as the bogie exits and retracts as it enters, to prevent tangling or damage by other objects. It serves the drive, not the heater contacts. The door carrier follows separate vertical movement. Match supply items individually; a reel does not establish inclusion of every moving heater circuit.

## Match position and heater-isolation conditions to actual wiring

The source permits heating after the door-compressed signal, disconnects heater power when the door rises and permits bogie travel after it is fully raised. Electrical and mechanical designers must map controlled circuits, contact engagement positions and permissible cable routes across the full stroke. No live contact connection/disconnection or bypass operation is implied.

Bogie travel ends have limit switches and mechanical stops, and the drive uses a brake motor. Check travel, lifting and cable handling against approved drawings, then heater isolation and limits. Successful empty-bogie exit does not establish correct handover of door-heater wiring, bogie-heater contacts and drive cables.

## Preserve working and chamber dimension definitions

Working dimensions are 2100 × 1100 × 1000 mm and chamber dimensions 2300 × 1250 × 1050 mm, both length × width × height. Bogie capacity is ≥3 t, heating 180 kW in 2 zones, and maximum working temperature 950°C.

Check workpieces, supports and clearances to door heaters on the loading drawing. The empty chamber is not a permissible workpiece envelope.

Uniformity is stated as ±5°C in features and ≤±8°C in the table. Insulation is listed as both ≥350 mm and ≥280+20 mm. Reconcile procurement criteria before acceptance. This article selects no more attractive value or assumes equal uniformity for all loads from two-zone control.

## Allocate installation materials through drawings and schedules

The buyer constructs foundations; the seller supplies foundation drawings and installs/commissions equipment. The buyer also prepares secondary wires/cables and protective conduits in advance. Check the three moving-power assemblies and site installation materials individually. Their electrical nature does not assign carriers, reels, contacts and site cables to one party automatically.

The proposal requires successful empty commissioning before loaded heat-up commissioning, then acceptance/report signing under the technical agreement. Provide loading, door/bogie strokes, installation positions and cable routes to confirm connections before defining trials. Structural descriptions do not replace delivery or operating records.
