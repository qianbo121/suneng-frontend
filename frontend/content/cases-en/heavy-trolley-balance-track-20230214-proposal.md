Heavy-bogie loading checks extend from workpiece supports through wheels, rails and foundations. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s gas-furnace proposal dated 14 February 2023 is for post-weld stress relief of large weldments, with a 100 t load definition, wheel-pair equalising frames and QU80 rails. Equalisation is intended to accommodate uneven loading and fabrication/installation tolerances; it does not replace checks of actual supports, wheel loads and rail installation.

## Interpret equalising wheel assemblies by their stated purpose

The long, heavy bogie uses wheel-pair equalising beams to compensate for fabrication/installation errors and distribute wheel loads. There are 6 wheel sets in 3 rows, including 4 driven sets. Longitudinal/transverse frame beams and wheels support the load together. Six wheel sets are not six drive motors, and equalising frames do not allow unrestricted eccentric loading.

Show workpiece/support contacts, centre of gravity and rail locations together, then have the designer assess wheel loads and foundation reactions. The basic table states 350 kN wheel load without fully defining the wheel, wheel set or operating condition concerned. Multiplying this figure by wheel count cannot establish permissible total loading.

## A 1.3 design factor is not permission to load 130 t

The source uses 1.3 in two places: large precast castable blocks approximately 600 mm wide beneath support blocks are designed for 1.3 times rated load, and traction capacity is also designed at 1.3 times. They concern different objects. Do not multiply them together or raise the 100 t load rating to 130 t.

The bogie uses different refractory structures beneath support blocks and in burner flame passages. Changed block positions require joint checks of local support and flame paths, so contacts are not moved onto a region designed for another purpose merely because the envelope fits. List workpiece weight, block weight and local contacts separately.

## Rail installation is not entirely buyer civil work

Seller scope includes rail type, specification and structural installation requirements, and rail installation adjustment before equipment installation in coordination with civil works. The buyer provides foundations, embedded parts and secondary grouting. Define construction, measurement and handover order; buyer foundation work does not automatically remove rail adjustment from seller scope.

Blocks and fixtures are buyer-supplied, with the seller designing them from buyer-provided product geometry. That design duty applies to those items, not all building or utility construction. Keep fixture designs aligned with wheel, rail and foundation drawings.

## Retain separate loaded-travel and heat-treatment records

The traction description gives 4 gearboxes, while the energy table states “4.0 × 6 units”. Travel speed is stated as both 5–8 and 5–7 m/min. Reconcile drives and speed before testing; the ability to move a bogie does not make configurations identical. Entry/exit limits, side seals and door interlocks must match confirmed actions.

Maximum operating temperature is 950°C and maximum design temperature 1050°C. Uniformity concerns effective-zone points at the end of holding, with temperature difference ≤±10°C. Design temperature is not the post-weld process temperature, and successful loaded travel is not temperature acceptance. Cold/hot commissioning, uniformity/efficiency tests and 1–2 trial batches are proposed acceptance activities, not completed results.
