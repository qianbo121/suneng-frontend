A Suneng discussion draft for a high-temperature laboratory chamber furnace proposes a maximum working temperature of 1400°C and a continuous working temperature of 1300°C, but lists thermocouples with a range of 0–1300°C. These figures do not automatically establish a verified high-temperature capability. Procurement should confirm that the complete measurement configuration matches the intended operating range, then assess the loading platform and actual specimens.

## Distinguish maximum, continuous and actual test temperatures

Maximum working temperature is the draft's proposed equipment upper limit; continuous working temperature is a separate operating condition. Pre-acceptance provisions specify heating from ambient to 1300°C and a 9-point temperature-uniformity test at 1300°C. This identifies the proposed test temperature but does not replace verification at 1400°C. A maximum rating also does not establish continuous full-load use at that temperature.

| Check | Discussion-draft record | Condition to distinguish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum and continuous temperature | 1400°C / 1300°C | Confirm each basis separately |
| Temperature sensor specification | Type S, 0–1300°C | Check actual model, protective tube and operating range |
| Proposed uniformity test | 1300°C, 9 points | Does not establish verification at a higher temperature |
| Heating-zone dimensions | 250 × 300 × 250 mm | Assess specimens, fixtures and measuring points together |

## Type S alone is not a complete sensor specification

The issue is the actual range of the thermocouple assembly and protective structure listed in this draft, not a claim that every Type S thermocouple is limited to 1300°C. Match the specific model, supply description, installation conditions, instrument input and calibration range. If the equipment must operate at a higher temperature, confirmed components and test documents must support that requirement. Changing an instrument's display limit is not a substitute.

## The pull-out platform's load must be tied to hot conditions

The proposal also includes a manually withdrawable platform with a target load capacity of at least 300 kg, constructed from a steel frame, high-alumina load-bearing bricks and a heat-resistant steel baseplate. Slide-rail ratings, total platform capacity and workpiece support at high temperature are different matters. One material's heat resistance or the rails' rated load does not establish full-load movement of the entire platform at maximum temperature.

For actual specimens, define geometry, contact area, support positions and total fixture weight, together with the temperature at loading and unloading. The draft mentions applications involving high-density materials but provides no confirmed document for the actual materials and atmosphere conditions. Rated temperature does not establish suitability for every process involving those materials.

## Acceptance should address the intended conditions of use

Proposed documentation includes test reports, general assembly and electrical drawings, and software. Procurement can require the final measurement configuration, temperature-use conditions, specimen loading and test scope in one agreed technical-document version. This creates a verifiable operating boundary. This page does not treat proposed energy savings, material suitability or maximum-temperature capability as delivered results.
