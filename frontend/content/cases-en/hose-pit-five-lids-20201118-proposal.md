Historical Suneng low-temperature hose-heating furnace documents propose a 12 m working length, five small lids, five control zones and five circulation fans. Equal quantities do not establish five independent chambers, a one-to-one lid/zone relationship, or permission to open one lid while other zones continue heating. Review hose loading length, lid opening, zone positions and heating control after opening in one layout drawing.

The body text describes shell, inner liner and insulation without defining independent compartments or the lid-to-zone layout. Dividing 12 m by five does not establish each usable chamber's clear length. Equal fan count does not prove that each fan serves only one section. Place actual hose lengths, arrangement and accessible space on a loading drawing, then assess sectional handling against the equipment layout.

## Confirm opening method and heating shutdown together

After describing five small lids, the lid section also mentions side-opening and double-opening doors and states that opening a door completely disconnects heating power. Reconcile these opening forms in the final drawing rather than choosing one as confirmed. For local loading/unloading, define which lid opens, which heating circuits are affected and when restart is permitted. The current wording does not support continued heating elsewhere while one lid is open.

## Distinguish circulation and exhaust for the low-temperature process

The application is low-temperature heating or drying of hoses below 200°C, usually at 130–140°C. These are historical proposal conditions. Actual hose material, substances to be removed and process curve need current documentation. An adjustable temperature range is not a universal hose-drying programme.

Five internal fans circulate hot air. Separate roof exhaust ports, an exhaust fan and a manual regulating valve provide an outlet. Internal circulation and exhaust cannot replace each other. Check obstruction of circulation passages by hoses and the exhaust/downstream connection requirements for actual released substances. A roof fan alone does not establish completed exhaust treatment.

## Confirm the layout, then validate an actual load

The documents list general arrangement and foundation layout drawings and operating instructions, with checks against the technical agreement during commissioning and loaded trials. For a new purchase, define hose loading, lid/zone positions, opening actions and circulation/exhaust interfaces before agreeing temperature validation. The historical ±7°C figure is proposed furnace uniformity, not a measured and accepted workpiece temperature difference.
