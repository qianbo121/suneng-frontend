Buyer-made hearth plates are more than steel plates that carry weight. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s metal wire-coil bogie-hearth proposal dated 2 November 2021 assigns heat-resistant steel hearth plates to the buyer. They must support loads, distribute heat into the furnace and reduce scale falling into bottom elements. The main procurement task is to coordinate these plates with Suneng's proposed bogie, heaters and loading arrangement.

## Identify precisely what is buyer-supplied

The source marks the heat-resistant hearth plates, total thickness 30 mm and grade ZG3Cr18Mn12Si2N, as buyer-supplied. They cover dense refractory bricks, with underside reinforcing grids and hooked sectional joints. This does not extend buyer supply to the whole bogie or establish that the historical quotation already deducted plate costs.

## Support, heat transfer and scale shielding must work together

Elements are placed around the chamber and beneath the hearth, with moving/fixed contacts supplying bogie heating. The plates are intended to support loads, emit bottom heat into the chamber and reduce scale-induced element short circuits. Check coil/spacer contacts, plate joints and structural space above the elements. Fitting inside the furnace is not sufficient acceptance.

If material, ribs or joints change, submit revised drawings for assessment of support and bottom heat transfer. The source describes a proposed structure. Reduced scale entry does not mean a sealed or maintenance-free hearth, and ordinary steel plate is not an equivalent replacement by implication.

## Submit coil layout and circulation conditions together

The proposal specifies 220 kW, 2 front/rear zones, one 5.5 kW roof circulation fan with guides, and rated temperature 700°C. Coil geometry, individual weight, orientation and bottom spacers affect loading. Show these together to confirm supports and intended circulation space. Two zones do not establish two independent air circuits, and a fan does not prove equal inner/outer coil temperatures.

The purpose section's temperature wording differs from the 700°C rating. Do not silently correct it and publish a coil-annealing process. Actual operating temperature and curve need separate agreement. Proposed furnace uniformity ≤±10°C is not a coil-hardness or all-workpiece temperature test result.

## Hand over buyer-supplied parts before installation and loaded trials

Suneng proposes site installation/commissioning; the buyer prepares facilities and supplies commissioning energy and an overhead crane. Loaded heat-up commissioning follows successful empty commissioning. Agree final plate drawings, quantity, delivery timing and incoming inspection along that handover, so trial plates match the intended production structure.
