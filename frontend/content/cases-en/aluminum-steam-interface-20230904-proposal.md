With a quench tank directly below a solution furnace, tank-water temperature and steam at the opening need separate checks. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s aluminium T6 proposal dated 4 September 2023 places compressed-air curtain pipes beneath the door, with an opposing suction hood and extraction fan to direct rising steam sideways. The movable tank must also coordinate with basket lifting and transfer to ageing. Reserving one exhaust connection on the general arrangement is insufficient.

## The air curtain and hood are a coordinated proposal

A row of compressed-air curtain pipes beneath the solution-furnace door blows rising water vapour to one side. The opposite hood and fan exhaust it outdoors. The stated purpose is to reduce steam entry and its effects on lining and heaters. This is a design and purpose description, not a measured capture rate or service-life improvement.

Confirm curtain direction, hood position and their relationship during door movement, basket lifting and tank travel. Alignment at one stationary position does not establish alignment in all moving states. The designer must define when blowing/extraction is needed, action coordination and site interfaces. Do not invent flow rates or treat one curtain clause as a completed steam-collection design.

## Tank cooling and opening steam extraction are different duties

The tank includes agitation, measurement, replenishment, overflow and sludge drainage, plus an automatically controlled recirculating water chiller. Its table states adjustable 30–80°C; structural text states 30–100°C, adding water above 75°C and closing the valve below 60°C. Cooling text also contains the incompletely defined phrase “within the ≥30°C range”. Reconcile actual liquid temperature, cooling method and controls before fixing chilled-water and supply/drain interfaces.

Steam equipment directs and extracts vapour at the opening; tank cooling controls liquid conditions. Neither curtain/hood nor chiller proves the other's duty is met in all states. The source only says steam is exhausted outdoors; it does not establish downstream treatment or site environmental acceptance.

## Include the next transfer after the tank moves away

The proposal transfers solution-treated workpieces to the ageing furnace's dedicated electric trolley. After tank movement, another trolley carrying untreated workpieces interfaces with the lifting hook. Include these receiving/waiting positions in the exhaust layout so hoods and ducts do not occupy transfer paths. Although remaining operations are described as automatic, loading/unloading and attachment are explicitly manual. This is not an unattended line.

The process section defines liquid-entry time of less than 10 seconds from opening the furnace lid to complete immersion. The tank table abbreviates this as frame entry into water after door opening. Align measured object and finish event for acceptance; first frame contact is not full immersion of all parts. The interval excludes earlier loading/attachment and later ageing transfer.

## Keep parameters with their own equipment and conditions

Solution rated temperature is 650°C, with uniformity ≤±3°C below 550°C using spatial nine-point measurement in the effective zone. Ageing maximum design temperature is 350°C, with a separate ±5°C effective-zone spatial nine-point requirement. Do not transfer one section's test result or design temperature to the other, or prescribe processes for all aluminium parts from them.

Suneng proposes both furnaces, tank and cooling equipment, transfer systems and controls. Agree layout/interfaces before loaded trials under the agreement and retain commissioning records. The tank-trolley motor is listed as both 2.2 kW and 1.5 kW in the source. This remains unresolved; neither is selected here as final supply.
