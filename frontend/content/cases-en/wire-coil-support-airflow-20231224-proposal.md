Wire-coil stands determine both support and the hot-air passage beneath the load. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s recirculating-air bogie-hearth resistance-furnace proposal dated 24 December 2023 assigns hearth stands to the buyer. They support the workpieces while leaving a bottom circulation passage connected to side air guides. Specify stands together with the bogie surface, loading pattern and guides; do not select a set of blocks solely by total weight after the furnace design is fixed.

## Show support positions and bottom passages together

The proposed stands rest on dense load-bearing bogie bricks. They must withstand loading impact and hot loads while preserving circulation passages linked to the side guides. Buyer supply does not remove their position, contact area or passages from the complete furnace layout.

Show coil dimensions, orientation, contact positions and stand height, then compare with guide inlets/outlets and lower passages. If stands or added spacers block the intended route, revise support or airflow layout and verify temperatures under the corresponding load. Larger fans or higher setpoints alone do not establish a solution.

## Check fans, zones and workpiece temperature separately

Roof fans and heat-resistant steel air guides are proposed, with elements around the chamber and beneath the hearth in 16 zones. Furnace uniformity is ±10°C and control accuracy ±1°C. Define measurement objects, reference bases, temperatures and loading for each; these values do not establish identical temperatures in all 44 coils.

The source lists 16 thermocouples and a 16-channel paperless recorder. Map furnace points to actual loading regions, then select workpiece-verification positions for the coil process and quality requirements. No internal coil-temperature records are supplied. Changes to stand height, arrangement or quantity cannot be assumed thermally equivalent simply because component counts remain unchanged.

## Buyer-supplied and reserved items need defined handovers

The structural list includes nitrogen protective-atmosphere piping marked “reserved pipework”, plus a cooling-fan rapid-cooling exhaust device. These establish proposed interfaces, not a fully supplied and verified atmosphere or rapid-cooling system. If nitrogen protection is required, define source, inlet/exhaust paths, purge criteria and final exhaust location. For rapid cooling, check compatibility with the atmosphere state and jointly allocate changeover, fault response and verification.

Suneng proposes site installation, commissioning and training; the buyer provides energy and safe lifting. Hand over stand requirements when loading and guides are agreed, rather than discovering a dimensional or airflow mismatch at loaded testing. Empty-furnace commissioning, loaded heat-up commissioning and final acceptance against agreed criteria are planned steps, not completed events.

## Can another coil type use the same 44-piece arrangement?

Compare geometry, weight, winding condition and supports first. The same count with different outer diameter, bore, stacking or contact changes space and airflow. Check the new drawing against stands and guides, then verify against current process requirements. Forty-four pieces is only the combination listed in this proposal.

## What information matters most initially?

Send coil dimensions, individual weight, intended count and layout, plus stand drawings, material and expected service conditions. Add process curve, atmosphere requirements, workpiece measurement and inspection requirements. For existing stands, provide actual dimensions and photographs so Suneng can assess compatibility with bogie and guides before refining responsibilities.
