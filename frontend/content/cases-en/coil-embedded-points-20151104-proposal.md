A furnace reaching its set temperature does not establish that the interior of every wire coil has reached the required process temperature. Suneng's 2015 pit furnace proposal for wire-drawing coils specifies seven pieces evenly distributed per batch, two thermocouples measuring furnace temperature and two further sheathed thermocouples embedded in workpieces to measure load temperature. Agree the insertion positions and their relationship to all seven pieces; two readings cannot represent every location in all seven coils without validation.

## The measurement method is specified; representative locations are not

The duty section requires heating under nitrogen protection and holding until workpieces reach the required process temperature, with real-time monitoring by load thermocouples. The control section specifies two furnace-temperature probes and two probes embedded in workpieces, with simultaneous furnace and load temperature recording. This identifies the measurement objects more clearly than “temperature recording provided”, but does not specify which two coils are instrumented, insertion depth or how they represent the remaining coils.

## Show the seven-piece load and measurement points together

Each probe directly records only its own location. Following the specified seven-piece arrangement, mark every coil, lifting fixture, load thermocouple and insertion method on the loading drawing. Process and equipment personnel should then confirm the basis for selection and any additional validation. “Embedded in the workpiece” does not establish that a probe reaches the slowest-heating location of each coil. Nor does it justify adding automatic soak timing based on an average of multiple measurements.

The chamber height is also listed as 1900 mm, matching the stated individual coil height, while the configuration includes heat-resistant steel lifting fixtures, a base plate and a protective cylinder. General arrangement and lifting drawings must reconcile the workpiece envelope, bottom support and fixture allowance before confirming that the proposed orientation fits. Equal dimensions do not prove suitability. Individual weight, monthly output and daily output are blank in the duty section and are not filled in here.

## Distinguish internal circulation from external retort cooling

The proposal separates hot-air circulation from forced-air cooling. A 5.5 kW lid-mounted fan circulates the internal atmosphere during heating; at the specified process temperature, forced air cools the retort's external surface to shorten cooling. External cooling air and internal protective gas follow different paths. Cooling outside the retort does not mean air is blown onto the workpieces. This is a proposed cooling arrangement, with no measured cooling curve for the intended load.

Confirm how loading and temperature records relate: when external cooling begins, which temperature signal governs it, and what condition permits unloading. These must follow the process and final control documents. The duty section includes a temperature requirement before unloading, but it is not a universal cooling schedule for all wire-drawing materials.

## Prioritise the handover documents

The proposed scope includes design, manufacture, transport, installation and commissioning, excluding external secondary power cabling, conduits and trays, and equipment foundation construction. For a new project, first supply the actual seven-piece loading and lifting-fixture drawing, installation and selection details for the two load thermocouples, and the associated process and recording criteria. The source also contains both 320 kW and 380 kW. Reconcile power in the final schedule rather than selecting a value from the model designation.
