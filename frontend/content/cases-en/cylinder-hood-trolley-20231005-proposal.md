Hood lifting and bogie travel need joint space and interlock checks. Jiangsu Suneng Industrial Furnace Co., Ltd.'s two bogie-hearth hood-furnace proposals dated 5 October 2023 concern cylindrical and tubular workpieces. They permit heating after the hood is compressed into position, disconnect heating as it rises, and permit bogie travel only once it is fully raised. These relationships are useful references, but do not establish inclusion of workpiece lifting, immersion or a complete quenching cycle.

## The hood and bogie move; workpiece lifting has a separate interface

The heating hood uses columns, beams, wire-rope pulleys and electric hoists. Workpieces enter on the bogie. Raising the hood clears the bogie path; it does not mean the hood mechanism lifts workpieces. Show hood travel envelope, bogie stroke and loading/unloading lifting routes together, rather than merely checking that the cylinder fits the chamber.

| Proposed state | Associated action | Confirmation needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hood compressed in position | Heating starts after the control signal is received | Relationship between compression position and signal. |
| Hood starts rising | Heater power automatically disconnected | Isolation scope and state feedback. |
| Hood reaches raised position | Bogie travel permitted | Clearance for the actual bogie and workpiece assembly. |

These are explicit proposed interlocks. A complete design still needs lifting/travel limits, fault states and maintenance supports. The three clauses do not establish complete anti-drop, power-loss protection or site lifting responsibilities. Cross-reference action descriptions and electrical/mechanical drawings; “automatic interlock” alone is not a handover definition.

## Keep the two sizes separate; do not scale performance by power

| Size | Chamber diameter × height | Rated power | Rated load |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Larger | Φ3800 × 2150 mm | 600 kW | 10 t |
| Smaller | Φ1700 × 1450 mm | 220 kW | 2 t |

Both specify a maximum operating temperature of 1100°C for the heating stage of quenching and other heat treatments of cylinders/tubes. Dimensions and load ratings support initial selection only. Include supports, lugs, spacers and handling space on the loading drawing. Similar structure does not establish interchangeability or justify calculating heating time from the power ratio.

## Bogie heater power is part of the movement interface

Both proposals locate moving/fixed electrical contacts at the bogie rear to supply its heaters. Sectional heat-resistant steel hearth plates provide support, distribute bottom heat and reduce scale entry into the elements. A bogie-position check must therefore cover contact engagement, de-energised states and maintenance protection as well as workpiece/hood position.

The larger unit has four control zones and the smaller two, with corresponding furnace recording. These equipment configurations do not replace verification of thick cylinder walls. To prove workpiece temperature or transfer time, add workpiece sensor positions, process requirements and downstream transfer details. Hood lifting alone does not establish immersion time.

## What information supports layout agreement?

Provide cylinder/tube dimensions, wall thickness, loading, lifting gear, building clear height, bogie discharge position and downstream station layout. Suneng proposes site installation/commissioning; the buyer prepares facilities and provides air, water, electricity, gas and overhead-crane support. Assess equipment motion and building lifting conditions together to determine how far the historical proposal applies.
