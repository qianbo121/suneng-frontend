A Suneng heating-unit proposal for tube-blank specimens includes sidewall service openings, dedicated supports and controls located in a separate room. For workpieces connected to external equipment, chamber fit is only the first step. The supported centreline, service routes and loading/removal path must also align; fixtures should not be left for adjustment after the furnace body has been completed.

## Support positioning determines alignment with the service opening

The source requires the supported workpiece centreline to coincide with the centreline of a Ø50 sidewall opening. The same wall has an upward service slot with a movable sealing shutter. Fixture height, installed furnace elevation and opening position therefore form one interface. Separate checks that a support exists and a hole has been made are insufficient.

Use a common drawing datum for the support surface, actual workpiece geometry, opening and slot, and external connection points. A workpiece change may alter support diameter or support method without increasing total length, still requiring a centreline recheck. This is a geometric compatibility assessment, not proof that the external test system is ready to operate.

## Whole-furnace loading and individual-support capacity are different

The proposal defines furnace load as the combined weight of parts and fixtures and separately specifies dedicated-support load requirements. These concern different objects. A whole-furnace load rating does not directly guarantee any single support, contact arrangement or externally connected pipe load. The source also says that the support structure is an experience-based estimate and must be customised from final specimen drawings, with fabrication following the buyer's signed drawing approval.

## Remote temperature control does not make every movement remote

The control description places the temperature-control system in a different room from the furnace, with remote cabling at least 15 m long. Hinged-lid operation is located beside the unit so that final position and sealing can be observed. List separately what can be viewed and set remotely, what can be operated locally and how the two positions maintain consistent states. This cannot be summarised as fully remote, unattended operation of every movement.

The proposal requires buyer approval and signature on final structural drawings before production. For comparable purchases, workpiece and support drawings, installed elevation, service interfaces and both operating locations are more useful in defining supply scope than chamber dimensions alone. Power remains an estimate requiring calculation in the original draft; this page does not use it as a final selection or delivery criterion.
