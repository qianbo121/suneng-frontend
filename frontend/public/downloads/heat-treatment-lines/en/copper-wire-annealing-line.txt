Continuous Copper-wire Annealing Line | Inquiry Preparation Checklist

Organize existing information so engineers can assess the process and equipment direction.
Provide known facts; leave uncertain items blank. Entering parameters does not establish compatibility. A tick only means the information is prepared, not that technical requirements are met.

01 MATERIAL AND TARGETS
Identify the wire and the required results.

[ ] 1. Material and incoming condition — information prepared
Copper grade, oxygen condition and incoming cold-work state; identify bare, plated or enamelled wire.
Plated/enamelled wire requires separate assessment; do not assume the bare-copper annealing process applies.

[ ] 2. Diameter and wire count — information prepared
Minimum, maximum and typical diameters with units; single or parallel wire count. List specifications separately and match each to the speed/output in item 4.

[ ] 3. Properties and surface — information prepared
Tensile strength, elongation, resistivity, softening and surface requirements. Provide available target values, units, specifications and editions; describe the application if requirements are not yet established.

02 PRODUCTION AND EQUIPMENT
Describe specifications, production timing and interfaces together.

[ ] 4. Speed, output and production cycle — information prepared
For each specification give diameter, wire count, target steady speed (m/min) and total output (kg/h). List different diameters/speeds separately and identify per-wire versus whole-unit figures.
Describe shifts, normal running, start/stop, specification changes, reel changes and wire-break downtime. Provide either existing speed or output if only one is known.

[ ] 5. Project scope and heating method — information prepared
State whether this is a new complete line, connection to existing drawing/take-up equipment or retrofit of an annealing section. List retained equipment and requested supply scope.
Identify indirect tubular-furnace heating, direct electrical annealing, or 'undecided — engineering assistance requested'.

[ ] 6. Reel handling — information prepared
Reel dimensions, full-reel mass, payoff/haul-off/take-up arrangements and allowable tension. Provide photos, models or layouts of existing equipment.

03 SITE AND VALIDATION
Report existing conditions accurately. Engineers verify required capacity and safety provisions.

[ ] 7. Atmosphere and cooling — information prepared
Surface targets, existing gas source and known supply conditions, cooling method, exit temperature and residual-liquid limits. Leave unknowns blank; you do not need to choose a protective-gas recipe yourself.
Engineering note: oxygen-bearing copper risks damage in high-temperature hydrogen-containing atmospheres. Hydrogen availability alone does not justify its use; assess material and process compatibility.

[ ] 8. Site and interfaces — information prepared
Space, existing power, cooling water, gas and exhaust, reel handling and upstream/downstream interfaces. Include building/equipment layouts if available.
Customers provide existing conditions; engineers confirm required supply capacity, safety measures and interface responsibilities.

[ ] 9. Wire samples and inspection information — information prepared
Representative grades and minimum/maximum/typical diameter samples; existing reports, sampling methods and traceability requirements. Confirm trial and acceptance conditions separately during project discussions.

USE BOUNDARY
This checklist collects preliminary requirements; it is not equipment selection, quotation or acceptance approval. Both parties must confirm heating, process schedule, atmosphere and safety provisions from the material, targets and site conditions.
