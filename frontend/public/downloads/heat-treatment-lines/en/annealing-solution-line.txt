Strip Annealing & Solution-treatment Line | Inquiry Checklist

1. Grade, hot/cold-rolled condition, initial microstructure and surface.
2. Minimum/maximum/typical width and thickness, coil mass, inside/outside diameters, edges and joints.
3. Annealing/solution-treatment microstructure and property targets, product standards, surface and flatness requirements.
4. Existing temperature-time-cooling requirements; unknowns may remain blank.
5. Output for representative sizes, shifts, coil/product-change frequency and existing speed.
6. Cooling, water quality, exit temperature, oxidation or protective-atmosphere requirements.
7. Existing uncoiler, accumulators, pickling/washing, recoiler and communication interfaces.
8. Building layout, lifting/coil handling, utilities and main-contractor responsibilities.
9. Company, contact and phone/WeChat/email.

Agree separately on representative materials, test conditions, inspection methods and criteria for equipment and product acceptance.
